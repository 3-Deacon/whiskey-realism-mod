# Tactical Operations Ledger

Living reference for the tactical operations-ledger command system, command assignments, posture execution, smoke checks and rollback. Build, deploy and smoke status live in [`docs/handoff.md`](handoff.md); orchestrator architecture, the doctrine clock, intel confidence and battle write ownership are in [`docs/tactical-orchestrator.md`](tactical-orchestrator.md).

- **Execution:** the battle core's posture producers (`Tactical/Battle/Producers/`, run by #68 `BattleCoreArbiterPatch`; #61 `BattleCommandPostureExecutorPatch` was deleted in BC5a); #62 `PlayerSubordinateOrderPatch` is the W&L player-subordinate order bridge ([`docs/wl-player-order-doctrine.md`](wl-player-order-doctrine.md)).
- **Config contract:** `Tactical Commander Mode = Active` is the release default; `MonitorOnly` runs the ledger and telemetry without vanilla writes; `Off` is rollback.

The system turns the orchestrator's command tree into a per-side operations ledger. The ledger classifies the battle operation, assigns command-node tasks and doctrine orders, monitors whether commands are validly idle or illegally stuck, and lets the posture producers submit bounded orders the battle-core executor issues when the mode is `Active`.

## System Overview

- `TacticalBattleCoordinator` ticks each active side; with heavy-path throttling on (default) the ledger re-ticks only on a new heavy snapshot, changed command-node intents, a Commander Mode change, or 60 battle seconds. Its clock is the doctrine clock (frozen overnight), so director commit windows span the same battle time at any compression.
- `ArmyOrchestrator` owns command-tree snapshots and the `TacticalOperationsLedgerRuntime` (operation shape, phase, command assignments, last-order/progress timing, posture summaries).
- `TacticalOperationDirector` selects and commits battle operations; `CommandDoctrineAssignment` and `CommandDoctrineOrder` publish per-command task, primary/support/fallback targets and SOP.
- `TacticalSopDoctrine` stamps each order with explicit authority (`Scout`, `Probe`, `Screen`, `Attack`, `Assault`, `Hold`, `Fallback`, `Reserve`, ...) and a reason. Telemetry only: no behaviour reads it. (Its risk budget, reacquire cadence, support-before-charge, fallback-if-pressed and allows-major-attack fields, and the massing cycle that rewrote them, described a gate nothing enforced and were deleted; the lone-main case is `MainFlankGuard`, docs/tactical-orchestrator.md.)
- `TacticalDecisionDoctrine` classifies contact before commitment (`ClassifyObjective`, `ShouldCommitFixAndFlank`, `ShouldBreakOffRecon`). In `Scouting`, `CommandDoctrineAssignment.ResolveTask` gives the recon tasks (`ReconnaissanceTaskFor`: Main effort and the Probe role probe, supporting/fixing/screening forces screen) on reconnaissance contact and, since BC9, with no contact at all, except for the `DefensiveNetwork` / `DelayAndFallback` shapes. The operation director takes the side's `BattleStance` (`TacticalOperationDirectorInput.WithStance`, from `ArmyEvidenceBuilder.ReadBattleStance` via the army's cached force-balance stance): a Defender is never committed to, nor left in, an offensive shape whatever path selected it (every shape but `DefensiveNetwork` / `DelayAndFallback` becomes `DefensiveNetwork`, a `Committed` / `Exploiting` phase becomes `Forming`; reasons `defender-screen` in Scouting, `defender-hold` otherwise). `TacticalDecisionDoctrine.TaskForStance` guards every task assignment (node task planning in `CommandNodeOperationsRuntime`, so every reader of the army's command operations; the doctrine assignment; GT recon with or without a visible enemy; the posture pipeline after all its rewrites and the leaf cascade, through the pure `PostureStanceGuard`): a Defender's Probe becomes Screen and its Attack/Support/Fix tasks HoldObjective. #45 never gives a Defender attack stance: its ledger stance is capped at defend (2), a visible weak point included, and its default sector scorer's `attack-weak-point` / `defensive-counterstroke` become holds at 2. The stance is `ArmyOrchestrator.CurrentBattleStance`, the side's one stance reader, refreshed by `ArmyPostureInputsRuntime.RefreshPickInputs` on every coordinator tick (before any plan pick) independent of `Enable Tactical Reinforcement Opportunity Doctrine`; the division play reads it too. Charges stay vanilla's (only groups vanilla escalated to assault are armed). A Defender's cavalry still scouts (recon producer). `CommandNodeRole.Probe` is assigned only by the BC9 probe rewrite in the posture pipeline (the allocator's `DirectChildRole` has no Probe).
- `TacticalIntelligencePack.cs` holds the battlefront geometry (`TacticalBattlefrontGeometry`), fallback ladder (`TacticalFallbackLadder`) and artillery mission (`TacticalArtilleryMissionPlanner`) gates. (The reserve mission `OperationalReserveDoctrine` / `DoctrineConsumerDecisions.DecideReserve` was deleted in BC5c: #59 decides from the Reserve role alone.)
- `TacticalApproachAvenuePlanner` scores Grand Tactician entrypoints, scheduled arrivals and deployment-group starts against each objective and publishes an enemy-origin to objective axis with confidence.
- `TacticalDefensiveLineAnchorPlanner` turns confident road/crossing approach evidence into an intercept line in front of the map objective (reasons `approach-intercept`, `road`, `crossing`, `objective-lane`).
- `TacticalBattleLinePlanner` produces frontage endpoints, objective lanes, flank anchors, echelon depth, artillery line and main/support/fix/screen/reserve/fallback targets, spreading forming commands across the frontage.
- `TacticalReserveAssemblyPlanner` scores reserve rally candidates: behind the objective, at least 325 units from the threat, at most 520 lateral units from the line, reachable, congestion below 0.80.
- `TacticalNavMeshPlanner` (with `TacticalMovementCostField`) turns ledger targets into covered-lane approach and fallback breakoff points (Scout and Probe tasks no longer go to a recon stand-off at the objective: since BC9 their target is the recon planner's look point, `BattleRecon.LookPoint`, a 200 m bound along the front); the posture producer feeds it runtime `NavMesh.CalculatePath` samples taken with a `NavMeshQueryFilter` (BC10: the live area costs copied from `NavMesh.GetAreaCost`, then vanilla `AddPath`'s four values, `PostureNavAreaCosts`: Roads 1, Bridges 1, FencesWalls 2, Rivers 500000, R5); the global `NavMesh.SetAreaCost` loop is gone. The path scores are read from the terrain grid every 10 m along the path (`PathTerrainScorer`): `roadPreference` = road (7) plus bridge (6) share, `terrainRisk` 0.85 only for an open river or a fence with no ford or bridge carrying it, `bridgeRisk` 0.55 on a ford or bridge (0.70 within 2 m of river or fence), `deadGround` 0.70 from a `Map`-layer raycast between eyes (+4 m) when a threat exists.
- `TacticalNestedDivisionPlayPlanner` expands a single parent command with at least three direct brigade/battery children into flank / main / depth-reserve brigade states, keeping the parent as HQ anchor.
- `CommandPostureExecutor` is the pure decision model for formation, waypoint, reserve, fallback and stuck-order recovery; the posture producer turns its decisions into intents.

Harmony patches do not write ledger state. Ledger state is written during the orchestrator tick; the posture producers read ledger assignments plus the per-tick battle snapshot, then submit nothing or bounded intents.

## Objective Anchors

`TacticalVisionRuntimeAdapter` builds objective records from vanilla state before synthetic evidence: `AIBattle.objectivechain`, then per-group `Regiment.currentsetobjective`, then live scene `Objectives` components. Visible formed enemy-line evidence is appended as a `VisibleEnemyLine` objective even when vanilla anchors exist. Approach avenues come from `BattleUnits.entrypoints` (`entrypointposition` / `trooptargetposition`), `BattleUnits.scheduledarrival` and deployment groups, with crossing evidence from bounded `BattlefieldSetup.SearchTerrainInRangePos` checks. With no map objective and no legal visible line, weighted vanilla `Regiment.lastsetwaypointposition` becomes `movement-anchor-<alliance>`.

The doctrine rule: screen contact is not a target for a formed assault. `TacticalOperationDirector` transitions:

- `contact-elsewhere-hold` -> a Defender whose best-scoring objective has no enemy at it but whose picture holds enemy at another objective forms its `DefensiveNetwork` (`Forming`) on that best-scoring objective, the ledger's own pick of ground (`ObjectiveSelectionScore`), instead of scouting; it does not walk to the objective the enemy was perceived at. A non-Defender not yet on the empty objective works the best-scoring objective with perceived enemy (the `VisibleEnemyLine` objective, fog-honest) through the ordinary gates below (army-children fix, 2026-10-17: both sides stayed `Scouting` through heavy contact).
- `no-contact-scout` -> initial `Scouting` when a newly selected objective shows no enemy at all (BC9; before BC9 own / max(1, 0) odds committed the operation); an operation already active on that objective keeps the rules below
- `defender-screen` / `defender-hold` -> a Defender side's (vanilla `GetBattleType` 2) decision with its offensive shape made `DefensiveNetwork` (Scouting / any other phase; Committed and Exploiting become Forming) (BC9)
- `recon-contact` -> initial `Scouting`
- `fix-and-flank` -> committed attack only after formed-line exposure, odds and reserve gates pass
- `contact-downgraded` -> `Scouting`
- `contact-lost` -> `SoftAbort`
- `odds-collapse` -> `SoftAbort`, only once the committed force (the nodes tasked to attack or support) has stood below the abort line for `OddsCollapseDwellSeconds` (600 doctrine seconds; `TacticalOperationsLedgerRuntime` keeps the time)
- `soft-abort-rest` -> a soft abort holds for `SoftAbortRestSeconds` before the side may commit again
- `commit-window` / `commit-hysteresis` -> a chosen operation (Forming, Committed, parallel, fix-and-flank) holds for `CommitWindowSeconds`, the plan's commitment (3600 doctrine seconds)

### Task churn (2026-10-18)

Smoke 2026-10-18 (Union Attacker, main `a6b49fc`): every Union node changed task together, every 30 to 60 s of real time (FixEnemy, ReserveWait, AttackObjective, FallBackToLine, FormUp), in lockstep with the operation's phase; a brigade carried a path 1.1 km to the vanilla objective with no enemy in sight. A battle is played at 20x to 120x, so every constant in doctrine seconds is a fraction of that in real time: the old 900 s commit window was 8 to 45 s. Proven causes and the fixes (branch `fix/battle-task-churn`):

- **The enemy line counted an enemy once per own unit that saw it** (`TryVisibleEnemyLineFromAggregate` summed each own unit's closest visible enemy), so the more of the army was in sight, the stronger the enemy; the operation's odds fell as it made contact. Each enemy is counted once.
- **Commit and abort were judged on different forces, and an abort lasted one tick.** The operation commits on the whole army (nothing is assigned yet) and is judged on the nodes tasked to attack (T14); the next count had no committed force, weighed the army again and committed again: Committed, SoftAbort on every tick (reproduced in `TaskChurnTests`). Now: an abort needs the collapse to last `OddsCollapseDwellSeconds`, and rests `SoftAbortRestSeconds`.
- **The enemy line is a one-tick sample of what is visible**, so a count without a glimpse of it dropped it from the picture; the operation kept working on `enemy-line-0` while the doctrine planned from the first objective left (an empty map objective): every node went FormUp, Probe, then back. `EnemyLineMemory` keeps the last line, unchanged, for an hour out of sight (`HorizonSeconds`, the plan's commitment).
- **The reconnaissance rule of the posture pipeline read this tick's sight** (FixEnemy, Screen, Probe in seven seconds): a group keeps the contact it had for the same hour (`GroupContactMemory`).
- **A held Main was displaced by its own escalation** (`DirectChildRoleHold`): the allocator gives Fix and the refused flanks only to a child that is not the main effort, so a Main allocated Fix has been replaced by the flicker of the pick, which passed as an escalation, left the army without a Main and let the fresh pick take it the same tick.
- Telemetry: `TacticalOperationChange` (one row per change of phase, shape or primary objective with the director's reason, the army, the assigned part, the enemy at the objective and the collapse time).

- **Vanilla's objective march walked a group Whiskey had told to stay** (the 1.1 km march): vanilla's `FollowObjectives` (decompile 6880-7010, runs while `macroai` is not -1 or 3, and the ledger maps Committed, SoftAbort and a defensive shape to a vanilla macro) re-sends a chain's centre unit to its objective with `SetWaypoint` whenever the group has no path. Whiskey's hold, form-up and screen tasks submit no move and `CommandPostureExecutor` yielded to any active move (`movement-in-progress`, `already-correct`), so the march went on, and the BC14O chain contest answered only a re-issue of a Whiskey move, never a hold or a recon group. Now (behind `Enable Objective Doctrine`, the contest's own flag; the executor's `TacticalWriteGuard` and Commander Mode gates apply as to every intent): `MoveContest.DecideChain` answers a move that is not the executor's own on a stay task (`MoveContest.IsStayTask`: a hold, a form-up, a screen) of any AI group, a due chain's own group or not (the smoke's brigade never was: the 45 `TacticalMainEffort` rows of the Union all read `vanillaChainContest` 0 while it walked 11 minutes) with the contest's Stop where the group stands, or, for a form-up or a screen, a move back to its ledger point, once per `ReissueMinHours` for as long as the group walks it. Left alone: a group in a fight or flanked, in vanilla's assault stance, a recon group, a group its parent's own Whiskey march carries (an ancestor with a path of its own and a move Whiskey issued it, the group with no Whiskey move of its own since: vanilla writes no `lastsetwaypointposition` for a group its parent re-lays, smoke16's 2nd_Brigade read 0,0 with a path all battle, so a group with no waypoint is never shown foreign, and one with a waypoint is carried when it lies within `ParentSlotMeters`, 150 m, of the parent's), a group carrying the allocator's `ReleasedFromReserve` mark (#59's regiment-level commit moves its subordinates and the Stop is group-wide), the player's and the feud instance's (the executor's guard); and the posture pipeline no longer reads a move as "already correct" when it is not the one the executor last issued the group (`MoveContest.IsForeignStayMove`, with the same exclusions), so a form-up or a screen is sent to its point instead of left to vanilla's march.
- **The role hold was released by every plan re-pick and phase change**: `RoleHoldPlanKey` is now the side, the main effort band and a count of plans adopted in a crisis (`ArmyOrchestrator.Replan(..., crisis:)`); a plan re-picked under the hysteresis, or a phase that moved on, releases no role.

### Terrain picture in planning (BC12)

`TerrainPicture` (`Tactical/Battle/`, built once per battle on BC10's grid; flag `Tactical` / `Enable Battle Terrain Picture`) feeds the planners' terrain branches that no producer reached before:

- **Objective classification.** `TacticalVisionRuntimeAdapter.ApplyTerrainPicture` reads every vanilla (`UnknownVanillaObjective`) or scene victory-point objective against the picture within 60 m: Bridge > Ford > Town > RoadJunction > Ridge (a crest cell of at least 3 m) > unchanged, `TypeAnchorVerified = true`, so `TacticalObjectiveSourceModel.Normalize` keeps the type. The observation carries `TerrainStrength` and `ApproachDifficulty` read per objective on the own side of it (the axis runs from the objective toward the side's centroid, so the water and the approach are the ground the own side has to cross), which `BuildBattlefieldPicture` passes on instead of zero. `ChokePoint` keeps its one producer (a corridor anchor's line objective).
- **Terrain strength** = `1 - (1 - c)(1 - h)(1 - w)(1 - b)`: `c` the best cover value within 60 m (wall 0.35, sunken road 0.30, embankment 0.20, fence 0.10, creek 0.08), `h` = `min(1, crest prominence / 15 m) x 0.5`, `w` = 0.4 for a river or creek across the front (60 % of a lateral transect 100-300 m ahead), `b` = 0.7 for a town. The planners' `>= 0.55` anchor test (`TerrainPicture.AnchorStrength`) is met by a wall on a 10 m crest (0.57), a 10 m crest behind a river (0.60) or a town. **Approach difficulty** = the mean `terrainspeccost` of the 300 m ahead, normalised grass 2 to swamp 6.
- **Director sign.** `TacticalOperationDirector.ObjectiveSelectionScore(objective, stance)`: an enemy-held objective subtracts terrain strength and approach difficulty (the shipped sign); an own or neutral one adds terrain strength, a Defender also half the approach difficulty.
- **Terrain lines.** `TacticalTerrainLinePlanner.BestLine` finds the best crest run, wall, sunken road, fence, embankment or river line within 400 m of the objective (own side or at most 100 m in front, within 30 degrees of perpendicular to the threat axis, at least 200 m long; score = strength - distance / 1,000 m). `TacticalDefensiveLineAnchorPlanner` turns a strong terrain objective with no corridor evidence (the old `defensive-line-terrain-anchor-preserved` `None`) into an anchor on that line: a crest at its forward edge (80 m behind it under `ReverseSlopeDefense`), a river line held 150 m behind it, anything else on its centre; frontage = the line length clamped to 350-760 m; reason `terrain-line:<crest|wall|sunken|fence|embankment|river>` plus `:anchored-left` / `:anchored-right`. The line planner then stands the group on the line's own side (`:defensive-side-terrain-line`), and its FormUp line slots stand on the anchor itself (stand-off 0; a corridor intercept still forms up 210 m behind it). Every defensive anchor, a generic approach intercept included, stands its slots on its own side (never in front of the intercept point). The lanes run along the line's own direction (a wall up to 30 degrees off perpendicular keeps every line slot on its cover); past the line's ends they fall back to the perpendicular through the nearest end. An end is anchored by vanilla's rule (`CheckIfFlanksAreAnchored` 6599-6640: river id 4 within 80 m sideways, or a crest within 50 m; a crest line's own ends rest only on a river).
- **Anchored ends keep the line task.** `CommandDoctrineAssignment.ApplyScourgeSlotTask` gives no `GuardFlank` end slot on an anchored side (index 0 is the +L end, index n-1 the -L end).
- **Reverse slope.** The army passes `plan == ReverseSlopeDefense` through `StoreDoctrineForCommandStates(..., reverseSlope)` to the doctrine build.
- Scope `tactical.terrain-line` (1.0 ms) wraps the doctrine build per ledger tick; `[once:tactical-battle-core:terrain-line] [BattleTerrain] first terrain-line anchor: <reason>` marks the first anchor.

Wide command sets (six or more nodes) convert the first and last forming slots into `GuardFlank` and keep near-wing slots as depth (`scourge-slot-flank-guard`, `scourge-slot-depth`, `scourge-slot-line`); an end on a river- or crest-anchored line side keeps the line task (BC12).

### Ground and the defended front (reserve-slot fix, 2026-10-17)

Smoke 2026-10-17 (CSA Defender, `DelayingDefense`, primary `enemy-line-1`): reserve posts, assembly points and flank guards were laid forward of the line and past the enemy centroid ("4th, 7th and others sent troops across the river"). Two causes, both in the planning half (the executor wrote none of those posts in the window; its crossing gate only sees moves it writes):

- **Command nodes were planned from the map origin.** `CommandNodeOperationsRuntime.Build` made every `CommandNodeOperationalState` from the intent alone (X = Z = facing = 0); with an approach avenue the geometry never read the node, so it showed only once the primary became the avenue-less `enemy-line-N` fallback (the evidence's `fallback` targets all lay about 300 m from (0,0)). The ledger now holds an `ICommandGround` (`SetGround`, set by `DriveOperationsLedger` from the side's battle snapshot: `CommandGroundRuntime`, the node id is the group's GameObject id). `Build` places each node it knows (`WithPosition`); a state without a position (`HasPosition` false) is planned nowhere (`battle-line-no-position`, no target) unless an avenue gives the direction, and `TacticalBattlefrontGeometry` measures placed nodes only.
- **A Defender's line was laid relative to the enemy line.** An `EnemyLine` objective has no defended-line anchor, so the slots were `Approach` points 210 m short of the enemy line (and the depth slot `BehindLine`, 325 m past it). A Defender whose objective is the enemy line now stands on its own front (`TacticalDefensiveLineAnchorPlanner.PlanOwnFront`, reason `own-front`, `:defensive-side-own-front`): centre = mean of the placed nodes, axis = enemy line to centre, frontage = the nodes' spread (350 m to 180 m a node); line slots stand on it, the depth slot, reserve post (425 m) and flank guards behind it. The depth slot of an unanchored, avenue-less line lies short of the objective for every stance.
- **Own bank.** A position the doctrine build computes stays on a bank (`OwnBank` over the ground's `ICrossingPicture`, pulled back along the line from an anchor on the bank to the target in eighths, the anchor itself at worst). A Defender's posts (primary, support) keep the side's **home bank**; every other stance's posts keep the bank their node stands on, so an Attacker's or a Meeting side's bridgehead post stays where the plan put it, and a task that goes to the enemy (attack, support, fix, probe, scout, screen) keeps its targets for the executor's crossing gate. A maneuver's target, which the plan chose with its crossing, replaces the primary after the bank rule and is not touched. A Defender node that has already crossed is sent back (`KeepOnBank` pulls from the nearest placed node on the home bank, not from the crossed node).
- **The home bank is fixed for the battle.** `HomeBank.Settle` (via `ICommandGround.HomeBank`): the bank of the side's own deployment zone (`OwnDeploymentZoneRuntime`, the zone's point nearest the placed nodes' centre), else the bank that centre stands on (the placed node nearest it when the centre is on a river), settled at the first ledger tick that can, recorded per alliance and reset per battle (`BattleScope`). It never moves with the force: a head-count of where the force stands flipped once most of it had crossed (verify round 3).
- **Fall backs, for every stance.** A fall-back target (a soft abort, a withdrawal) is the target as it would be without the clamp unless it lies off the home bank, then it is pulled onto the home bank; and a clamp never moves it forward toward the enemy (`OwnBank.KeepFallBack`: if the clamped target is nearer the enemy along the node's line to the objective than the unclamped one, the unclamped one is used). A crossed node's way back is not clamped to the river edge on the enemy's bank, and a node still at home is not sent forward over the river. Whether and by what crossing it gets back is the executor's (a fall back is not crossing-gated today).
- **A node with no position holds.** The order of a node the ledger could not place carries reason `battle-line-no-position` (`CommandDoctrineOrder.HasNoPost`); `CommandPostureExecutor.Decide` answers its movement tasks (form up, advance, scout, probe, attack, support, fix, screen, guard flank) with `NoWrite` `no-position`, so the posture pipeline cannot substitute an objective stand-off from the node's live position. Its holds, a reserve's legal idle and a fall back keep their own handling. Known minor: a pool group with no ledger order at all takes `TryBuildSingle` (no ground, so unplaced) and the `Decide(state, ...)` path, whose assembly and reserve areas are stand-offs from the objective computed from its live position and are not stance-gated; it predates the ground and is left as it was (stopping it would stop every no-order group moving).

Not changed (findings): `TacticalOperationDirector.ObjectiveSelectionScore` adds 0.20 to an `EnemyLine` and 0.35 to an exposed objective, which can make the visible-enemy-line fallback a Defender's primary over its own ground; the own front makes that primary harmless, and the director's pick is left to its owner. The reserve-drift patch (`BattleReserveDoctrinePatch`) is telemetry only: it reports vanilla's `AssignReserves` placing a ledger reserve into a line group; whatever vanilla then sends is vanilla's order (the W&L `ai-chain` move-new rows), not a Whiskey write.

## Posture Producers (formerly #61)

Run in the battle-core arbiter tick (#68, Postfix on vanilla `AIBattle.AdjustGroupFormations` 5870). Battle gate (`PostureExecutorBattleGate.Allows`): no action before battle state 5, with `macroai < 0`, during vanilla army retreat (`macroai 3`), during the end-of-day phase (`eodcycle > 0`), on the W&L feud instance, or on the player-side instance outside `ai_vs_ai`. The producers read the per-tick `BattleSnapshot` (unit state as `PostureSense` proxies, perception, the D5 parent map) and the side's ledger; they write nothing. The order executor checks every target with `TacticalWriteGuard` and issues:

- stance through `TacticalStanceWriter` (task to stance by `TacticalCommandTaskToStanceMapper`; ownership rules in [`docs/tactical-orchestrator.md`](tactical-orchestrator.md));
- movement through `BattleUnits.SetWaypoint`, with vanilla courier order delay except for an in-contact withdrawal; Attack/Support/Fix/AdvanceToAssembly group moves over 150 m through `AIBattle.BlockedMovingOrder` (see below);
- formation through `BattleUnits.SetFormation` (courier), facing/refuse through `BattleUnits.SetGroupFormation(newpath: false, modifylastwaypoint: true, refuseflank: ...)`, and in-contact facing through `Regiment.RotateRegiment` (per regiment, skipping detached or player-owned subtrees, 119865);
- fire control through `TacticalCombatBehaviorWriter` (`BattleUnits.ChangeCombatBehavior`, per regiment).

AI-issued writes keep `showmovementoptions: false`.

Producers: `Posture` (Executor priority: AI-pool groups and the leaf brigades nested under them), `FacingPulse` (Scorer; `Enable Tactical Contact Facing Pulse`), `FireControl` (Executor, CombatBehavior per regiment), `DivisionPlay` (Play).

Rules:

- **Ledger resolution:** exact `CurrentCommandOperations` node by GameObject id, then component id; otherwise `ArmyOrchestrator.ResolveCommandIntentForGroup(...)` plus the current ledger. If both fail, nothing is submitted.
- **Formation writes** (`CommandFormationWritePolicy`, applied by the executor): never while the group or its subordinates move; in combat only rotate settled non-artillery regiments in place, never re-lay; out of contact use the courier `SetFormation`; wheel/refuse only with no perceived enemies in the group's own reach (`BattlePerception.GroupRangeReach`); cooldown 0.05 battle hours, 0.1 under fire. The refused flank (BC11 decision 2) reaches vanilla: out of combat (`combatstatussubordinates <= 0`, vanilla's own re-form gate, `AdjustGroupFormations` 5886) and not moving, `NeedsRefuse` is the wheel (`SetGroupFormation` with `refuseflank`, `SkewedPosition` 78109-78168) whatever the enemies in reach; only a facing-only wheel keeps the enemies-in-range refusal. In combat it is `FormationWriteKind.RefuseInPlace`: the executor rotates in place (`Regiment.RotateRegiment`, no position change, no re-lay) at most four wing regiments per group per tick, the ones whose setback is at least 0.1 of the refuse depth and whose skewed-line rotation vanilla's own per-regiment micro turn (`StopMicroUnitsCloseToEnemyAndRotateTowards` 4539-4613: 40 m, or the fire range once the regiment has received fire; 25 degrees, wider in cover) would not turn back (`FlankFacing.RefuseRotations`). `PostureSense.RefuseFor` needs flank risk and either close engagement or perceived flank pressure on one side (`BattleSnapshot.FlankPressure`); the side is the flank with more pressure, the 45-150 degree geometry test runs on that side's slice-pressure bearing, and a tie falls back to the closest visible enemy. A refuse already ordered on that side (`OrderExecutor.LastIssuedRefuse`) is not re-ordered, and a due refuse overrides a posture verdict of `already-correct` / `already-valid` only. Flank-pressure facing (decision 1, `Enable Flank Response And Envelopment`): a holding brigade or division faces the 3-bin window of its slice pressure with the most Visible plus confidence-weighted Remembered strength, keeping its facing while that window holds at least 0.8 of the best (`FlankFacing.MostPressuredBearing`); the regiment pulse stays on the closest visible enemy, vanilla's per-regiment rule. Telemetry `facingSource` = `pressure|closest|entry|none` on `TacticalCommandPosture` and `TacticalFacingPulse`; `TacticalRefuseInPlace` Write rows (`side`, `rotated`, `skippedByMicroTurn`). Envelopment orders (decision 4): the doctrine order of every node of a maneuvering child carries the `EnvelopmentManeuver` task (FormUp hold, AdvanceToAssembly march on 180 m lanes along the rect's lateral axis, AttackObjective at the enemy line end), a `ManeuverHeading` the posture pipeline turns into a group Facing on arrival (`envelopment-turn`), and a hold target-less (`ManeuverHold`: no nav plan is spent); see [`tactical-orchestrator.md`](tactical-orchestrator.md) § Battle Core.
- **Movement** (executor): an interrupted path or stale movement flags without paths do not count as an active move; a target within 60 m of the current waypoint is a duplicate (`OrderArbiter.DedupeMeters`); recent-order cooldown 180 battle seconds per (channel, source); a write that does not materialize backs off 0.05 battle hours and gets one ±30 degree retry; a queued courier order counts as issued (D6).
- **Outbound orders:** one courier move per group per 0.25 battle hours (`OrderExecutor.CourierIntervalBattleHours`, was `TacticalOutboundCourierCadence`); a same-task re-issue waits `Brigade Task Hold Seconds`.
- **Leaf brigades** nested in an AI group move only for movement tasks, only to their slot in the parent's formation (`LeafSlotTarget`: the parent from the snapshot's D5 map, its `lastsetwaypointposition`/`rotation` and the leaf's layout offset, 92050-92086; the refused slot only for the flank the executor last refused), clamped to vanilla map bounds, and never while the parent has an order in flight. No stance write.
- **Main-arrival hold** (`MainArrivalHoldLatch`): arms once per role/task, releases after supports close, 15 battle-minutes, or an aggressive commander, and stays released until the role or task changes.
- **Opportunity override** (`TacticalOpportunityTaskOverride`): `DefensiveHold` is no opinion; withdrawal, wait-and-consolidate and attack-now adjust tasks.
- **Local flank emergency:** a close-engaged attacking, supporting, fixing, advancing or released-reserve command with flank risk executes as `GuardFlank` (`CommandFormationCorrection.TaskForLocalFlankEmergency`).
- **Fallback targets** (`CommandFallbackTargetResolver`): step away from the objective by the fallback standoff, else away from the closest visible threat, else fail closed.
- **Scourge conversions:** `TacticalDivisionPlayExecutor` (division play anchored on the best engaged subordinate), `TacticalCavalryFollowDoctrine` and `TacticalGrandTacticianReconDoctrine` (cavalry guard/scout/screen/raid; no blind assault without FOW contact), `TacticalFireControlDoctrine` (infantry long/medium/short and cavalry behavior from task, range and readiness).
- **Facing pulse** (`Enable Tactical Contact Facing Pulse`) rotates regiments only and never touches artillery.
- **Close engagement and flank risk** are snapshot proxies: melee in the subtree or a visible enemy within 250 m (`PostureSense.CloseContactMeters`), and #44's perceived flank-risk rule (`BattleWriterPolicy.PerceivedFlankRisk`) instead of vanilla's unfiltered `groupenemiesinrange` / `flanksthreated`.

**Division play vs the executor (plan D8):** the spec ladder is binding, Executor beats Play. #61 used to replace a child's ledger task with the division-play task before deciding (the play overrode the ledger). Now the division play submits its own intents at `Play` priority, and a play order wins a channel only where the posture producer submitted nothing for that group on that channel. **Covered crossings:** an Attack/Support/Fix/AdvanceToAssembly group move farther than 150 m carries `IntentFlags.AvoidCoveredCrossings`, and the executor issues it through vanilla `AIBattle.BlockedMovingOrder` (3054-3150) with the crossings and fire points the side's **perceived** enemy covers (`CoveredCrossings`, BC10: vanilla's two `UpdateBlockedCrossings` rules, 7917-7973, over the snapshot's Visible and recently Remembered observations at their last seen heading, fire range and cover, instead of vanilla's `blockedcrossings`, which reads every enemy regiment on the map), as vanilla's line-group moves do (6953-6956) and shipped #61 did. `UpdateBlockedCrossings` only lists the crossings the enemy covers; the obstacle carving and the delayed `SetWaypoint` (two frames later, `useorderdelay:false`, from `UpdateOrders` 3382) are `BlockedMovingOrder`'s. With no perceived covered point (or a map with no river crossings and no covered fire point) the list is empty and the move is a plain `SetWaypoint`.

**Road march routing (BC10, `Enable Deployment And March Doctrine`):** a Move of at least 400 m whose straight line samples at least 30 % forest or swamp, by a group not in close engagement, is planned by `RoadRoutePlanner` (an A* over a 25 m lattice of the terrain grid, cost = cell length x `terrainspeccost`, river impassable unless frozen). When the route costs at most 0.80 of the way vanilla's pathfinder takes, the intent carries up to two via points (the route's first road entry and last road exit, none within the last 250 m where vanilla skips roads) and the group takes March Column (group formation 4) first; the executor issues each via point as a `SetWaypoint` leg (`newpath: false` after the first) with a leg heading toward the next point. A group not in column 0.1 battle-h after the formation write moves unrouted. One route plan per side per arbiter tick, cached per (group, 50 m target bucket) for 0.25 h. A move that takes `BlockedMovingOrder` is never routed (safety before speed).

**Avenue flags (BC10):** `TacticalVisionRuntimeAdapter` reads `roadAnchored` and `crossingAnchored` from the terrain grid (`AvenueAnchors`): road-anchored needs half the entry-to-target samples on road or bridge, or the entry point within 20 m of a road cell; crossing-anchored needs a ford or bridge sample, or a vanilla `rivercrossingpositions` point within 50 m of the segment. Fewer avenues read road-anchored than before (every entry point with a target used to), so `TacticalDefensiveLineAnchorPlanner` uses its generic intercept and frontage more often.

### Crossing-threat release (BC14)

A Defender's allocator has a third release after BC13's crisis and stagger and BC11's flank release: `DirectChildAllocator.ReleaseReserveToCrossings`. The ledger (`FeasibilityLedger.TryThreat`) lists the uncovered flank crossings the enemy can reach before dark, earliest first, with each direct child's courier-plus-march time to each. For a crossing without a guard, the eligible Reserve child (not an exploit asset, holding no player-owned strength; a BC10 deployed reserve is eligible) with the least time is released when the enemy arrives within 0.25 h of that time and the floor of held reserves allows: role `RefuseLeft` / `RefuseRight`, the crossing's band, and a guard point 100 m behind the crossing on the own bank facing it (`GuardFlank` in the doctrine order). The binding (`ReserveReleaseState.CrossingBinding`) stays while the arrival stays inside the child's time at release plus 0.5 h, so a guard never alternates; an enemy already across is contact and BC13's crisis release answers it. Rows: `TacticalReserveRelease` with `reason=crossing-threat`, `crossing`, `guardX/Z/Heading`, `ownShiftHours`, `enemyArrivalHours`, `held`, `floor`, and a `reverted=true` row when the guard ends.

### Works hold on the stored orders (BC14W)

While the ledger's works hold is on (`FeasibilityLedger.WorksHold`: `hopeless-assault` when the selected plan sends a body at works whose detail term is at least `RiskyPenalty`, `turn-not-struck` while a holding fix faces works and no enveloping wing has struck), `ArmyOrchestrator.UpdateOperationsLedger` guards the stored products in the one store, before any reader of the tick: the copied command states in place (`WorksHoldGuard.ApplyState`: the task becomes `HoldObjective`, reason `works-hold`, the former task kept in `PreGuardTask`), then the orders built from them into the army's own array (`CurrentDoctrineOrders`; the ledger's own list stays as it built it), the leaf map into a cached guarded copy (`CurrentLeafBrigadeMap`, `LeafAssignment.WorksHeld`) and a fallback state built for a group the ledger has no state for (`GuardFallback`). A node is guarded when its child faces works and BC11's maneuver table does not already hold it; an exploit that is not a breach and aims at held works is built as a hold with its followers. `OperationStrength.CountsAsAssigned` counts a guarded node by its pre-guard task so the objective ledger's assigned strength does not drop. The counts of what the guard rewrote are the `TacticalWorksHold` row's `guarded`. The guard pass allocates nothing after warm-up (FEAS-ALLOC-W).

### Works station (BC14W)

A Defender holding works keeps a reserve behind its most threatened focal point. The ledger (`FeasibilityLedger.TryStation`) publishes the station (150 m behind the own focal point the enemy reaches first within 1 h, on own land, facing it) and each direct child's courier-plus-march time to it, NaN for a child with player-owned strength; `DirectChildAllocator.StationReserveBehindFocalPoint` gives the quickest eligible Reserve child (not exploiting) the guard point after the crossing release. It is a station, not a release: the role stays `Reserve` (every later crisis, stagger, flank or crossing release still sees the child first and the station is then chosen anew), the floor of held reserves is not consulted and the release hysteresis skips it. The binding (`ReserveReleaseState.StationBinding`) keeps its child and its place until the child stops being an eligible Reserve or another focal point is reached 0.25 h earlier than the standing one was at choice; `Clear()` (a plan change, a new battle day, a stand-down) ends it, `ClearTrigger()` does not. The child's nodes become guard entries in the maneuver table (task `ReserveWait`, reason `station-works`, the station as target and heading); the ledger gate reads `ArmyOrchestrator.GuardHash` because the role, and so the command-node intents, do not change when a station appears, moves or ends. A stationed child holds no leaf reserve inside itself (a Reserve-role command is not a holding command) and `TacticalReserveCommitGate.TryReleasedDirectChild` is false for it, so the leaf slot and the release producer plan no move for it: the station's guard point is its one placement.

### Main-effort objective and screens (BC14O)

With `Enable Objective Doctrine` the director picks the army's main effort among its objectives by `TacticalOperationDirector.PickMainEffortObjective` instead of the shipped best score: each map objective with a row in the ledger's table brings the row's score (value from vanilla's points, risk for the main body, chain prior, hold term), an infeasible row is out, and any other entry (the enemy line, a recon contact) scores its value times `1 + U`. The director's own terms (ground strength, approach, exposure, enemy line) are added to every entry. The pick is latched per alliance (`ObjectiveLatch`) and changes only for a rival 0.1 ahead; `ParallelObjectives` is not opened (one main effort). `TryPickBestObjective` returns the pick as the operation's primary objective; the shipped contact-elsewhere rule still applies to it (a Defender holds, another stance works the perceived contact), so the operation can follow perceived contact while the main effort, the plan's band, stays on the pick. `StoreDoctrineForCommandStates` keeps the latch and `CurrentObjectivePick` (read by the `TacticalMainEffort` row). After the allocation, `DirectChildAllocator.ScreenSecondaryObjectives` gives at most two secondary objectives one Screen-role child each (`DirectChildIntent.ScreenTarget`; the posture producer moves that child to its target). With the flag off none of this runs.

### Exploit orders and reserve release (BC13)

The commander opportunity tick (`Tactical/Battle/Producers/OpportunityIntentProducer`, plan § BC13) can override the ledger for one asset at a time. An exploit order is a `CommandDoctrineOrder` carrying `ExploitId` (role `SupportingAttack` for a gap, shaken, flank, battery or cluster exploit with an attack task; role `Defender` with the `Counterstroke` task for a Defender's penetration on a second-line reserve). The posture pipeline (`PosturePipeline.Run`, `ExploitOverrideKind`) reads `ExploitTable` before the ledger order: the asset's Moves go out without the order delay, its formation follows the exploit task, and its descendants "follow" (no Move or Formation). The ledger itself is not rewritten: when the exploit ends the asset returns to its ledger assignment, and a cooldown (`AssetCooldownHours`) keeps it from being picked again at once. `DirectChildAllocator.ReleaseReserves` releases reserves one per interval above a floor (`ReserveReleaseState`, `TacticalReserveRelease` rows with `reason=stagger|crisis`, throttled per child and reason); a release and a return are damped by `ReleaseHysteresis` (the trigger must hold 0.03 doctrine-h, a released reserve stays out 0.25 h and until the trigger has been gone 0.05 h, no re-release for 0.1 h after a return); a crisis release of an exploit asset's parent line aborts the exploit (`line-crisis`). Rollback flags: `Enable Commander Opportunity`, `Enable Staggered Reserve Release`.

## Risks

- The posture producers' orders reach vanilla battle state when `Active`; they stay behind the arbiter's battle gate and the executor's write guard, settlement, dedupe, recent-order and contact gates.
- The command system depends on command-tree discovery; missing or unstable command nodes produce no writes.
- `SetWaypoint` calls keep vanilla courier order-delay semantics; broad movement replacement is out of scope.
- Existing config files can preserve old values. Inspect the live BepInEx config before interpreting smoke.
