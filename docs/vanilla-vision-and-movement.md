# Vanilla Vision and Movement (vanilla reference)

> Dated audit (2026-10-14); the vanilla sections stay current. The [Mod usage audit](#mod-usage-audit) table describes the mod before the battle core (stage D, 2026-10-15); [Battle-core status](#battle-core-status-2026-10-15) records how each recommendation was implemented. `BattleChargeGatePatch.cs`, `TacticalRegimentalThreatComputer`, the worker analysis path and `TacticalUnitObservationAggregate` are deleted.

How vanilla handles fog of war, spotting, line of sight, intelligence and AI movement on battle and campaign maps, and where Whiskey reads or bypasses it. Source: `/tmp/gt_src/asm/Assembly-CSharp.decompiled.cs` (build 14703111, mapped 2026-10-14); all bare numbers are lines in that file. Config values are from `<GTCW>/Config/battleprefs.txt` and `unitprefs.txt`. Mod paths are relative to `src/WhiskeyRealism/` in the `orchestrator-worker-thread` worktree (verified at `fe94959`). Living doc: update when the game patches.

## Battle vision

**Two grids, one per side.** `GameController` carries two `FogOfWar` components. `Regiment.CreateFOWObjects` (116505-116518) sets `fow = fows[0]` / `oppositefow = fows[1]` for player-alliance units, reversed for everyone else. So `fows[0]` is the player alliance's vision and `fows[1]` the other side's. `AssignUnitToFOW` (116520-116534) registers every battle regiment as a viewer on its own side's grid. On the campaign map only units with `GetUnitVisibilityStateOnCampaignMap != 0` are viewers (82934: top units, unittyp 15/16/17, garrisons).

**Both grids are maintained.** `BattleController` calls `fows[0].InitFOW()` and `fows[1].InitFOW(_drawtexture:false)` (23886-23890); campaign does the same with `_calledfromcampaign:true` (29916-29920). `InitFOW` sets `initialized=true` either way (100794), and `Update` (100675) runs the state machine `UpdateViewRange → BlurFOW → TransferFOW+ClearFOW → ResetUnitsVariables` (100683-100714) on both. `drawtexture=false` skips only the blur and texture upload (100924, 100937). The `fowvisible` counters and the `concealment` grid are updated by `ClearFOW` (100878-100916) for both grids.

**How a grid is painted.** `UpdateViewRange` (101274-101365) sweeps each viewer through 360° in angle steps. The view radius is `maximumlineofsight[unittyp]` (101296; unitprefs: 200/250/225/250/450/20/150…), times the expert-scouts perk (perk 8), times the battle weather `lineofsightreduction` (campaign: a rain/snow climate term) (101302), times `fow_lineofsightreduction_layndown` 0.3 if lying down (101305). With `GameVars.hqinthesaddle` in battle, any viewer that is not `dlcw_isundercommander` gets radius 0 (101312). Routed or inactive units do not paint (101325).

`DrawVisibility` (101057-101132) marches outward in steps of `distance × fow_mapfactor` (0.1, so 10 samples per ray). Smoke shortens the ray (101087-101096). **There is real terrain line of sight**: each sample is painted only if `Physics.Raycast(startpos+4m, dir, remaining, "Map" layer)` misses (101098). Note the cast length is the *remaining* range, not the distance to the sample, so terrain beyond a sample can hide it; this needs an in-game check. Forest (terrain 14) and high crop (16) above eye height cut the remaining range by 12 / 7 cells (101102-101115; battle only). Each painted cell also gets `100 − terrainconcealment + distance × fow_concealmentreductionperfield (−0.5)`, which is forced to 100 inside `alwaysdiscoverunitstorange` (40) (101116-101127). A cell counts as visible once `fowvisible > 0`, with `fow_hideunhideconfirmations` (5) cycles of hysteresis (100880-100897, 101246-101263).

**`UnitIsHidden(pos, cunning, relStrength, laydown, coverType, coverValue, unittype, reg)`** (101179-101229): W&L incidents 60/61/64 force "visible". Otherwise a unit in fog is hidden. Outside fog it is hidden when `concealment − cunning×100×0.4 − size term − spottingfactor[unittype] − perk 3/8 − coverconcealment×covervalue − layndown(70) < 0`. `GetIntelligenceOnPos` (101231) returns 1 unless the component's `UseIntelligenceMap` is set.

**Who reads the grids.** `Regiment` visibility tick (127209-127239): `iswithinfog` is forced false for every player-alliance unit unless W&L HQ-in-the-saddle applies (127211-127215). Non-player units are tested against `oppositefow`, which is `fows[0]` (127225). So **`iswithinfog` means "hidden from the player"**. Melee always reveals (127216, 127231). The campaign bugle-range branch (127235-127238) assigns `true` inside `if (iswithinfog…)`, so it does nothing. Other `iswithinfog`/`fow` readers are all player-UI or rendering: `HideUnderFOW` (103411), `ObstacleLine` (106219), `SpottedEnemy` (142530+), `CalculateApproximation` (80265), `ShowPathArrows` (137932), and model swaps (116383-116461). **No vanilla decision consumes `fows[1]`.** `SpottedEnemy` calls `oppositefow.UnitIsHidden` (142724, 142800), which is `fows[1]` for player units, but 142804 removes those marks first because their `iswithinfog` is false. So the grid is painted every frame and its result is thrown away.

**AI perception in battle.** `Regiment.UpdateUnitRangeFast` (122545-122937) cycles `completeunitlist`, a few units per call (`raycastcycles`):
- `enemyinrangereg` / `enemyinfirerangereg` (122650, 122699) take an enemy when `!enemy.iswithinfog | enemy.alliance == playeralliance | !usefow` and it is within range. For AI units every enemy is player-alliance, so **the AI's lists are range-only (no fog, no LOS)**. The player alliance's own lists are filtered by `fows[0]`. Range = `max(maximumlineofsight, distancetoenemytocancelinterruption)` in battle and `commanderrange` on the campaign map (122508-122513, 122608).
- `closestenemyunitfar*`, `retreatangle`, `enemystrengthwithinangle[]` (the `aidefensiveslices` used by `MostEnemiesAreOutsideAngle` 4784) and `flankstrength[]` have **no fog filter for either side** (122658-122663, 122719-122733, 122799-122820).
- `enemyinrange` (GameObjects) is distance-sorted (122838), but **`enemyinrangereg` is not** (122840). It stays in scan order.
- `AIBattle.UpdateAIHotspotUnits` (7975-8004) lists every active enemy regiment, plus reinforcements arriving within 12 h. `UpdateBlockedCrossings` (7917) scans every enemy against `rivercrossingpositions`. `DefensivePosition.UpdateEnemyUnits` (2738) uses `enemyinrangereg`.

Net: **vanilla battle AI is omniscient within about 200-450 m, and fully omniscient for hotspots, flank/slice pressure and closest enemy.** Fog is player-only.

**W&L / ai_vs_ai.** W&L sets `ai_vs_ai` (23755-23768, 29787-29793), but `playeralliance` still decides which side `fows[0]` serves, so the asymmetry stays. With HQ-in-the-saddle, only the player's command paints `fows[0]` (101312). Enemies outside that command's view then drop out of *friendly AI* `enemyinrangereg`/`enemyinfirerangereg` (122650/122699 read `fows[0]`-derived `iswithinfog`). This is a code inference; verify in-game. The AI order-delay cheat (91296, `debug_aicheat_noorderdelayinfrontofenemy = true` at 64463) is skipped when `ai_vs_ai`.

**Artillery and LOS.** Target choice uses `enemyinrangereg`/`firerange` by distance only (`UpdateAutoTargets` 118352-118400). There is no pre-fire terrain LOS check. Projectiles raycast along the ballistic path (`Projectile` 108330-108350), so terrain and objects block hits but not target choice.

## Battle movement

- **`BattleUnits.SetWaypoint`** (91225-91640): returns `void`. Several paths return early **silently**: W&L control check (91300), garrison (91331), campaign readiness ≤ 1 on enemy soil (91338-91360), **`onretreat || isrouted`** (91362), prewar chapter, battle monuments. With `useorderdelay`, the move is queued through `AddToOrderQueue` (courier delay) (91483-91510). Regiments (`unittyp ≤ 13`) go to `RegimentSetPath` (91540); groups go to `SetGroupFormation` (91559). `SetWaypoint` sets `doublequick` only on `unittyp ≤ 13` (91541); groups may pick it up elsewhere (120410, 121903, 133413), which was not traced.
- **`SetGroupFormation`** (91822-92110): builds the hierarchy offsets (`BuildGroupOffsetPositions`) and bends the line to a `TerrainShape` that avoids terrains 4,5,6,9-13 (92054). It then calls `RegimentSetPath` for the HQ and each attached unit (92108), and refuses a flank with `SkewedPosition`.
- **`Regiment.RegimentSetPath`** (130791-131270) guards: campaign `CheckIfFinalWaypointIsPassable` (131031, defined at 25608); map-bounds clamp x to [1, sizeX−2] and z to [−sizeZ+2, −1] (131057-131068; the battle z axis is negative); battle `CheckIfFinalWaypointIsOnTerrain(…, 4)` pulls a river endpoint back toward the start (131075, 25574). Roads are skipped for line formations, artillery and routed units, and for **AI units within `maximumdistancetoobjectiveroadmovement` of the target** (131134-131166). It then tries `AddPath` up to 20 times, nudging the start 0.5 m each time (131167-131186). After that come `UpdateWaypointAdjustments` and `CheckIfWaypointEndsOnWater` (131558, which steps river corners back toward the unit). Finally `RepositionWaypoints` (131263 → 138531) **snaps the final waypoint to a cover collider, or to the crest from `CheckTerrainPeak` (27068) when `usehills`**. `usehills` defaults to true for AI units and false for player units (112854-112861).
- **`AddPath`** (130259-130540): clamps the start to the terrain. In battle it sets **Rivers to 500000 (2 if frozen), Roads/Bridges to 1 (2 when skipping roads), Fences/Walls to 2 (500 for some types when `pathinterrupted`)** (130364-130394). It then runs `NavMesh.CalculatePath(start, target, -1, …)` (130473). A `PathPartial`/`PathInvalid` result is discarded until iteration 15, then accepted (130511), so **an unreachable target ends at the nearest reachable point**. The return value is 1 only if the path reaches the target exactly. Area costs are **global NavMesh state**, rewritten on every `AddPath`.
- Terrain ids (battleprefs mesh order): 0 grass, 3 creek, 4 river, 5 river crossing (ford), 6 bridge, 7 road, 8 fences/walls (sea on the campaign map), 9-13 pontoons, 14 forest, 16 high crop (texture specs). `GetCurrentTerrainOnPos` (26727) reads the baked `terrainspecs[x,z]` grid. `GetTerrainHeight` (27002) snaps to the bridge/river NavMesh surface.
- Vanilla's own position picking is terrain-aware only through `RepositionWaypoints` (cover/crest), `CheckIfFlanksAreAnchored` (6599, `CheckTerrainPeak`) and `TerrainShape`.

## Campaign vision

- `AIFaction.SeparateUnits` (10458-10515) splits all visible-state units into own, enemy, neutral, fleets and garrisons at **true position and `groupstrengthdirect`**. There is no fog or intel term. Every garrison lands in `owngarrisons` whatever its alliance (10510-10513). `UpdateHeatmaps` (12318) and `GetStrengthRatio` consume these lists directly. `theaterposition` is the midpoint to `closestenemyunitfarreg` within 5 × `commanderrange` (17063-17067), again unfiltered.
- The player's campaign view is `fows[0]` plus `SpottedEnemy` marks. Marks are placed with intel-scaled position jitter and time delay (142658-142751, 142773-142870); perk 1 adds delay and perk 3 (Bureau of Military Information) reduces it.
- **`fows[1]` exists on the campaign map too.** Its viewers are top units, its radius is `maximumlineofsight × rain/snow` (101302), and it uses the same terrain raycast. No crop or forest reduction applies there.

## Campaign movement

- **`AICampaign.MoveUnitTo`** (14479-14540) returns `false` only when the target's state is neutral or not at war/allied (14493-14494) or the unit is in battle. Otherwise it stacks the target (`GetStackPosition`, 14498). Then:
  - **`movearoundenemyunits:false`**: `SetWaypoint(doublequick:true, useorderdelay:false)` (14514). `SetWaypoint` does not set `doublequick` on the group itself (91541).
  - **`movearoundenemyunits:true`**: it queues a `DefensiveMovingOrder` (14523), which builds NavMesh obstacles around all enemy units (`Retreat.PrepareAllianceObstacles`, 9870+). Orders run one at a time over 2+ frames. If an order is already running it returns **false** (14519). Otherwise it returns **true before any path exists**.
- **Transport**: AI units are initialized `userails=true, usesea=false, userivers=true` (112862-112867). `AddPath` campaign costs come from `terrainspeccost` (130414-130465). Sea costs 500000 unless `usesea`, rails are toggled per `Railroad.SetNavmeshPassable`, and `useseaonly` blocks everything except area 8. Sea invasions set `usesea` (9546). Rail boarding needs spare capacity and rail access (116420-116446). An over-subscribed mode only slows movement (`speedadjustmentduetotransportcap`, 116487-116502).
- **Target validation**: `CheckIfFinalWaypointIsPassable` walks the target back toward the start until `IsTerrainPositionRestricted` (25755) is false (terrain 1, wrong water type for ships, closed state). If nothing qualifies it falls back to the start, which means no move. Distances (`GetClosestAreaDistance` 14835, area scoring) are straight-line XZ, not path cost. Unreachable targets get a partial path (see `AddPath`).
- Forced march: `running|doublequick` cuts supply (114258) and raises sickness (115153).

## Recommended changes

- **R1. Fog-limited AI contact.** In `TacticalFogOfWarContact.IsValidVisibleEnemy`, when `own.alliance != playeralliance && GameVars.usefow`, also require `!own.fow.UnitIsHidden(enemy.GetPosition(), GameVars.commander[enemy.commander].GetCommanderCunning(), (float)enemy.strength / GamePrefs.unittypesize[enemy.unittyp], enemy.liedown, enemy.coverobject, enemy.covervalue, enemy.unittyp, enemy)` (arguments mirror 127225; `Regiment.fow` is public (111350) and is `fows[1]` for AI units, not `oppositefow`). Guard the `commander` index range; vanilla does not. Keep `inmelee > 0` as always-visible (127216). Cost: O(1) grid/array lookups per call, no raycasts (the grid is already painted every frame). Main thread only (`HasPerk`, commander lookup).
- **R1 fallback, required.** `fows[1]` is blank when HQ-in-the-saddle is on (101312: enemy units are never `dlcw_isundercommander` — `IsUnitUnderCommander` 91168-91175) and when `!usefow` (100755). Detect this with `GameVars.hqinthesaddle && !calledfromcampaign`, or with a cached "`fows[1]` painted any cell" probe, and fall back to the current range-only behavior.
- **R2. Capture visibility once.** (Implemented by the battle snapshot, which replaced `TacticalUnitObservationAggregate`; see below.) Originally: in `TacticalUnitObservationAggregate.CaptureUnit`, record `HiddenFromSide[side]` per enemy observation: `fows[1]`-based for the AI side, `!iswithinfog` for the player side. `TacticalRegimentalThreatComputer`, `PlayExecutorRuntime` and the worker path then filter `EnemyIndices` on that flag. Keep last-known positions (with a timestamp) so hidden enemies decay rather than vanish. For worker-thread safety, the flags must be computed on the main thread.
- **R3. Campaign (optional).** Apply the same `fows[1].UnitIsHidden(enemy.GetPosition())` filter to the mod's `enemyunits` consumers (strategic ledgers), with a fallback to vanilla's omniscient list. Vanilla `AICampaign` itself stays omniscient.
- **R4. Validate mod-generated targets.** Before `SetWaypoint`, clamp x to [1, sizeX−2] and z to [−sizeZ+2, −1] (131057-131068). Reject or step back from terrains 4/8 with `BattlefieldSetup.CheckIfFinalWaypointIsOnTerrain(start, target, 4)` (it is public) and `CheckTerrainLine(start, target, {4}, 2f)`. After the call, treat `regimentpaths == 0` as failed and try a rotated bearing. Do not re-implement cover/crest snapping: vanilla `RepositionWaypoints` already does it for AI units (`usehills=true`) unless `skipcover`.
- **R5. NavMesh probes.** In battle every `AddPath` resets Rivers (500000, or 2 when frozen, which vanilla also uses), so the river cost is consistent. What drifts is Roads/Bridges (1 or 2) and Fences (2 or 500), set by the last unit's `skiproads`/`pathinterrupted` (130370-130394). That changes corner counts and so the probe's `roadPreference`/`congestion` scores. Set Roads=1, Bridges=1, Fences=2 explicitly before the `CalculatePath` at :1833, or score with `CheckTerrainLine` instead.

## Battle-core status (2026-10-15)

All five recommendations are implemented on `feat/battle-core` (plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-15-battle-core.md`; runtime proof is the BC7 in-game smoke):

- **R1, fog-limited AI contact.** `BattlePerceptionProbe` tests each enemy with `own.fow.UnitIsHidden(...)` (the 127225 arguments, `commander` and `unittyp` bound-checked, `-1` when out of range), `inmelee > 0` always visible. The fallback is `BattlePerception.DecideMode`: RangeOnly (vanilla's range lists) while `!usefow`, with HQ in the saddle in battle (101312), before `firstrunfinished` (100648) or in the first `FogStartupSeconds` (5 s, the transient from the skipped init-time `ClearFOW`), or with `Enable Battle Fog-Honest Perception` off. A player-alliance AI side (W&L / `ai_vs_ai`) uses `!iswithinfog` (OwnSideWithinFog). `UseIntelligenceMap` is logged once per battle (`[BattlePerception] side grid UseIntelligenceMap=…`). `TacticalFogOfWarContact` keeps its method names and filters vanilla's per-unit candidates by `BattleSnapshot.IsVisible`, sorted by true distance (never by the unsorted `enemyinrangereg` order).
- **R2, capture visibility once.** One pooled `BattleSnapshot` per AI side per doctrine tick holds own units and enemy observations; hidden enemies become Remembered after two hidden probes (vanilla's hide hysteresis analogue) with confidence `max(0.05, 0.5^(minutes/20))`, dropped at 90 minutes. Evidence (`ArmyEvidenceBuilder`, `EnemyVisibleState`), the contact ledger, objective records, the threat inputs, plays (`PlayAnchor`), posture, artillery, charge and #42/#44 read observations; `enemystrengthwithinangle`, `flankstrength`, `closestenemyunitfar*` and the enemy `reinforcementarrivalswithin24hrs` have no Whiskey decision reader left (BC7 grep gate). Reinforcements count only once their entry point is seen.
- **R3, campaign.** Not part of the battle core (optional, unchanged).
- **R4, validate mod-generated targets.** `WaypointValidator` (pure) + `WaypointValidatorRuntime` run inside `OrderExecutor.Plan` for every Whiskey move: the x/z clamp above (`VanillaMapBounds.ClampXZ`, parity test), `CheckIfFinalWaypointIsOnTerrain(start, target, 4)` with up to four 25 m step-backs, `CheckTerrainLine(start, target, {4, 8}, 2f)` for in-contact withdrawals only (the line starts one 2 m step out), friendly footprint, and for artillery the friendly fire line and the 60 m point blank. Battle terrain ids: 4 river, 5 ford, 6 bridge, 8 fences/walls (8 is sea only in the campaign table). After the call the executor judges the move next tick (moving-unit count or vanilla's waypoint stamp; plan D6) and retries once at ±30 degrees before giving up. The artillery straight-line retreats and the veto's re-issue were deleted with their patches.
- **R5, NavMesh probes.** The posture producer's path probe sets the battle area costs explicitly before `CalculatePath` (`PostureNavAreaCosts`: Roads 1, Bridges 1, FencesWalls 2, Rivers 500000 or 2 when frozen; test-pinned).
- **Leaf target via `transform.parent` (:2946 above).** Replaced: `LeafSlotTarget` takes the parent from the snapshot's D5 parent map.
- **Campaign `MoveUnitTo(…, true)` returns queued, not moved** (last table row): unchanged, campaign scope.

## Open questions

- `UseIntelligenceMap` is an inspector field. The battle core logs the AI side grid's value once per battle; the smoke answers whether `GetIntelligenceOnPos` is live in battle (campaign still unlogged).
- The `DrawVisibility` raycast length (101098) is the remaining range, not the distance to the sample. Confirm in-game whether near cells get hidden behind far ridges.
- The W&L HQ-in-the-saddle effect on friendly AI fire/target lists (122650/122699 reading `fows[0]`) is inferred from code and needs smoke confirmation.
- Battle `hqinthesaddle` does not check `dlc_scenarioactive` at 101312. Does a non-W&L battle with the option on blank both grids?
- `fows[1]` skips the init-time `ClearFOW` (100785 is inside `if (drawtexture)`). Its first `ClearFOW` therefore sees alpha 0 and increments every cell, which may give a 1-2 cycle "everything visible" transient at battle start. R1 ignores the first 5 seconds (`BattlePerception.FogStartupSeconds`).
- Not traced: newspaper/telegraph intel paths, entrenchment, and supply as a routing input. `AddPath` costs contain no supply term.
- Settled by the battle core: an enemy hidden for two probes becomes Remembered and decays (not instantly lost).
