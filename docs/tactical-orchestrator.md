# Tactical Orchestrator

Living reference for the Grand Tactician tactical orchestrator. It describes current behaviour; build, deploy, harness count and smoke status live in [`docs/handoff.md`](handoff.md). Slice specs and plans under `docs/superpowers/` are point-in-time artifacts.

## Architecture

The tactical orchestrator is a per-battle brain layered over vanilla `AIBattle` methods:

- `TacticalBattleCoordinator` owns battle lifecycle, the doctrine clock and the two per-side orchestrators.
- `TacticalBattleOrchestrator` owns one side.
- `ArmyOrchestrator` owns the army plan, enemy-intent observation, the command-node tree, direct-child roles, command intent resolution and the operations ledger.
- Generic command-node snapshots model the vanilla `Regiment` hierarchy. There are no `CorpsOrchestrator`, `DivisionOrchestrator` or `BrigadeOrchestrator` classes.
- The battle core (`Tactical/Battle/`, [Battle Core](#battle-core)) is the only path for Whiskey's battle movement, stance, formation, facing and combat-behavior writes: intent producers run in one arbiter tick, and an order executor issues the winners. Patches read orchestrator state; they do not write it. The posture producers' orchestrator inputs (AI pool, leaf brigade map, live commander initiative, reinforcement opportunity) are refreshed from the coordinator tick: `ArmyPostureInputsRuntime.RefreshPickInputs` (stance, live initiative, reinforcement opportunity: first, before any pick, and before the initial pick) and `RefreshPostureCaches` (AI pool, leaf brigade map).

Command nodes come from the side's battle snapshot. The snapshot's command parent map (plan D5) scans each group's `allattachedunits` (every descendant) once per roster change; a unit's direct parent is the containing group with the smallest `allattachedunits.Length`. That reproduces vanilla's own direct relation (`GetAttachedUnitsReg(directonly)` tests `transform.parent == this`) without reading `transform.parent` or allocating a `GetAttachedUnitsReg` list. `DirectChildDiscoveryRuntime` and `CommandTreeRuntime` read `UnitRecord.ParentIndex` / `BattleSnapshot.DirectChildren`; `Regiment.unittyp` and `GamePrefs.commandhierarchyshift` give the echelons.

## Lifecycle

- #35 `TacticalObserverPatch` (Postfix on `AIBattle.CheckGlobalAIStrategy`) drives the coordinator when `Enable Tactical Battle Orchestrator` is on: a battle-identity detector issues Start / End / Restart, then the coordinator `Tick` runs. Battle end is also observed on `BattleUnits.OnDestroy`.
- The W&L feud instance (`isplayeraiorfeud == 2`) never drives lifecycle or `Tick` (`TacticalBattleCoordinator.DrivesOrchestrator`).
- At start the coordinator suppresses one alliance (`ResolveSuppressedAllianceId`): a W&L Commander-in-Chief player's alliance, otherwise the player's alliance outside `ai_vs_ai`. A W&L subordinate career suppresses nothing; its units are protected per unit by `TacticalWriteGuard`.
- `Tick` runs once per vanilla side-stat cycle per battle (the second side's `CheckGlobalAIStrategy` in the same cycle is skipped) and drives each side against that side's own `AIBattle` (`ArmyEvidenceBuilder.ResolveAiBattleForAlliance` / `SelectAiBattleIndexForSide`: `sideofai` match, feud instance skipped). Each `AIBattle.objectivechain` holds only its own side's groups.
- `OnBattleEnd` calls `BattleScope.ResetAll()` and drops the orchestrators.

## Battle Core

Plan and spec: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-15-battle-core.md`, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-battle-core-design.md` (archived after the in-game smoke).

- **Snapshot (D1/D2).** `BattleCoreTick.EnsureSnapshot(AIBattle, alliance)` builds one pooled `BattleSnapshot` per AI side per frame (`BattleSnapshotBuilder`, one pass over `completeunitlist`): own units and enemy records as `UnitRecord`s, the D5 parent map, ownership flags, derived pressure arrays. `TacticalBattleCoordinator.EnsureBattleCoreSnapshots` builds every AI-run side first in the `CheckGlobalAIStrategy` postfix (5866), whatever the orchestrator flag and in every Commander Mode (it writes nothing); #60 builds its side's snapshot at deployment. Patches read it through `BattleCore.TryGetSnapshot`. Two pooled instances per side; the snapshot is the per-tick memo.
- **Perception (R1).** `BattlePerceptionProbe` runs after each build: an enemy is seen when vanilla's own AI-side fog grid shows it (`FogOfWar.UnitIsHidden`, arguments as 127225) or it is in melee; a player-alliance AI side (W&L, `ai_vs_ai`) uses vanilla's `iswithinfog`; while fog is off, with HQ in the saddle (101312) or before the grid is painted the side falls back to vanilla's range lists. `BattlePerception` keeps hidden enemies as Remembered at their last position (confidence halves every 20 battle minutes, dropped at 90) and counts reinforcements only once their entry point is seen. `SlicePressureBuilder` fills slice, flank and closest-visible-enemy arrays from Visible plus confidence-weighted Remembered observations. Evidence, contact ledger, objective records, plays, posture, artillery, charge and feud targets read these, never vanilla's unfiltered enemy fields. Rollback: `Enable Battle Fog-Honest Perception = false` (range-only for every side).
- **Ownership (BC3).** `PlayerOwnership` sets `PlayerOwned` (W&L player's command, its descendants, take-overs, a CIC player's whole alliance; outside W&L the player alliance unless `ai_vs_ai`), the chain flags and `Feud` (a unit's own `ai_feudstance`, propagated from its AI group and command ancestors). `TacticalChainOrderPlanner` fans a group order out to its children when a player-owned descendant is exposed (no detached node between them, 119865), and `TacticalPlayerDispatchRuntime.Emit` sends the player's share of an ancestor's move as one W&L dispatch.
- **Arbiter and executor (D3/D4/D6).** #68 `BattleCoreArbiterPatch` (Postfix on `AdjustGroupFormations` 5870, `Priority.Last`) runs `BattleCoreTick.RunArbiter`: producers submit `BattleOrderIntent`s, `OrderArbiter` keeps one winner per (group, channel) by priority (Safety > Executor > Play > Support > Scorer, ties keep last tick's source), `OrderExecutor` plans each winner (guard, chain, `WaypointValidator`: map clamp, water/fence step-back, friendly footprint, artillery fire line and point blank; `SilentExitPrecheck`; dedupe within 60 m; recent-order, task-hold, courier and failed-write cooldowns) and `OrderExecutorRuntime` issues it (stop, move, stance, formation, facing, combat behavior: a move before any stance order, because vanilla queues each order behind the latest queued processing time). Last tick's moves resolve to Moved, Failed (one ±30 degree retry, then GaveUp) or Superseded. Claims are per (unit, channel), so a group Move whose descendant holds a higher-priority Move claim in the same tick fans out around that subtree (`OrderExecutor.BeginTickClaims`, `TacticalChainOrderPlanner`): vanilla's group formation re-paths every child (92076-92108), and a regiment's Safety withdrawal must survive its brigade's Executor move. The arbiter stands down while the master `Enabled` switch is off or Commander Mode is not Active. Patches that must act inside a vanilla call (#42 feud replay, #56 courier parity) run the same pipeline inline (`TryPlanInline` / `ExecutePlannedInline`), refused under a higher arbiter claim.
- **Producers** (built once from `BattleProducerRegistry.Order`, run in this order): Withdrawal (Safety), ArtilleryDoctrine and ArtilleryPointBlank, GroupStance (Scorer), ChargeDemotion (Safety), Opportunity (BC13; submits no intents, see below), Posture (Executor; #61's ledger posture and leaf brigades), FacingPulse (Scorer), FireControl (Executor), DivisionPlay (Play), Recon (Support, BC9 reconnaissance), ReserveRelease (Support), Charge (Executor, the infantry charge doctrine). D8: the posture executor beats the division play on the same channel, so a play order wins only where the posture producer submitted nothing for that group. Rollback: the producer's own flag in `Plugin.cs` where it has one, otherwise `Tactical Commander Mode = MonitorOnly`/`Off`.
- **Reconnaissance (BC9).** The fog-honest AI goes and looks (`Battle/BattleRecon.cs`, runtime `Producers/ReconIntentProducer.cs`; plan § BC9). *Cavalry recon:* when an enemy-side entry point has not been watched for an hour (the contact ledger keeps per-entry-point watch stamps), a front band's ground is stale (recency < 0.5), or confidence is below the 0.35 gate (straight ahead before any contact, the nearest remembered enemy after it), one standing AI cavalry regiment of a settled brigade (not ordered by posture this tick, not moving, not linked-group-moved) rides to a stand point 150 m short of the target (vanilla `distancetoenemytocancelinterruption`), stops on first sight, observes 0.05 h and rides back to its brigade slot; never toward a visible enemy at least as strong (stand-off or the 100 m corridor of its path); at most 4 starts per side per doctrine hour; advanced only on the executor's Issued outcome (fail closed); another source's move or vanilla's re-lay ends it. Scout companies (type 4) are not used: vanilla re-paths them to their parent's target every update (`CheckScoutMovingTargets` 124616-124660). *Reconnaissance in force:* in the army's Probe phase, a side that is not the Defender gets one AI-pool group per front band with no visible enemy nominated (not linked, no player-owned unit below, not moving); the posture pipeline rewrites it to role Probe / task Probe (screen stance via the task mapper) toward a point one infantry sight depth (200 m) ahead; on first sight within 200 m the recon producer sends one Stop (screen stance is not stopped by vanilla contact, 117253). Once per band per doctrine hour. A probe that ends short of a visible enemy at least as strong (weaker contact, arrival, 0.25 h) leaves its group holding the ground gained: the posture pipeline drops a move more than 60 m back along the probe's advance (`BattleRecon.HoldBlocks`; forward bounds, break-offs and fallbacks go) until the group's posture task differs from the one it was nominated under; a superior contact returns it to its ledger task. A Defender side never probes and never gets a Probe or attack task (the director's `ForDefender`: no offensive shape, Committed → Forming; `TacticalDecisionDoctrine.TaskForStance` where node tasks are planned, in the doctrine assignment, GT recon, the posture pipeline and the leaf cascade; #45's ledger and default-scorer stances capped at defend; stance `ArmyOrchestrator.CurrentBattleStance`, the one stance reader, refreshed every tick whatever the opportunity-doctrine flag) but its cavalry still scouts. A probe hold never overwrites another group's (grow-only store). *Look points:* every Scout/Probe task targets `BattleRecon.LookPoint` (200 m along the front axis, toward the objective and no farther before any sighting) instead of an objective stand-off. *Facing:* Screen and Probe brigades with nothing in sight turn to face the stalest unwatched enemy entry point (`TacticalFacingThreatSource.Entry`, never urgent; `CommandFormationCorrection.ShouldFaceThreat(task, source)` lets the facing pulse submit it, `PostureIntentPlan.SubmitBrigadePulse`); their regiments take the brigade's bearing when the subtree is not clean (on a clean subtree the group facing turns them); GuardFlank turns as before; HoldObjective, ReserveWait, Consolidate, FixEnemy, Delay, HoldChoke and FallBackToLine never take the Entry source and keep their front-axis facing. *Skirmishers:* `SkirmisherScreenDistancePatch` pushes an AI skirmisher company of a screening (or advancing attack-stance) line from vanilla's 30 m link to 100 m while nothing is in its range. Rollback: `Enable Battle Reconnaissance = false`.
- **Commander opportunity (BC13).** Plan § BC13, spec [`superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-battle-opportunity-design.md`](superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-battle-opportunity-design.md). *Personality:* `CommanderPersonalityRuntime.TableFor` derives per-commander aggression, caution, audacity, casualty tolerance and political responsiveness from the historical registry, else from the commander's vanilla stats (initiative, cunning, leadership, traits), else the default; the table feeds the macro/doctrine band and the opportunity decision (`TacticalCommanderPersonality` rows). *Openings:* `OpeningDetector` (pure) finds gap, shaken, flank-in-the-air, battery, isolated-cluster and (Defender) penetration openings from the snapshot's Visible/Remembered enemy rows only; cavalry stands off 60 m. *Override:* `OpportunityIntentProducer` runs the pure `OpportunityTick` and writes the per-alliance `ExploitTable` (at most 8 live entries, 256 bound units); `PostureIntentProducer` asks the table before the ledger order: the asset gets the exploit order (`CommandDoctrineOrder.ExploitId`, no courier delay), its descendants follow (no Move or Formation), and an ancestor keeps its ledger state; `PlayExecutorRuntime` excludes exploit subtrees. *Defender:* a Penetration exploit may carry the bounded `Counterstroke` task (only in `ExploitLifecycle.cs` and `TacticalCommandTaskToStanceMapper.cs`; #45 maps it to -1). *Reserve release:* `ReserveReleaseState` (per `ArmyOrchestrator`) staggers `DirectChildAllocator.ReleaseReserves`: interval 0.15 - 0.1 x aggression h, a floor of max(1, ceil(R0 x floorShare)) reserves kept, crises release one per sector. Flags `Enable Commander Opportunity`, `Enable Defender Counterstroke`, `Enable Staggered Reserve Release`. Six vanilla bug fixes ride with it (#119-#124, [`patch-catalog.md`](patch-catalog.md)). Zero allocation after warm-up (OPP-ALLOC); OPP-TIME measured ~80-130 us a tick for 12 divisions in the harness (informational; the binding figure is smoke item 9).
- **Movement honesty (BC10).** *Terrain grid:* `BattleTerrainGridRuntime` copies vanilla's per-battle terrain-id array once (`BattleTerrainGrid`: `IdAt`, `SpeedCost`, `Passable`, `SampleLine`, the listed river crossings); the pure readers are `CoveredCrossings`, `PathTerrainScorer`, `RoadRoutePlanner` and `AvenueAnchors`. *Perceived flanking:* `PerceivedFlanking.Apply` fills `UnitRecord.Outflanked` from the units the side sees (vanilla's `UpdateFlanking` rules, 122959-123175, over Visible enemy rows and the side's own regiments, on a 60 m bucket grid); the builder no longer copies vanilla's unfogged `outflanked`, so the charge bar and target flank, `PerceivedFlankRisk` and the flank-threat means are fog-honest. *Deployment (#60):* a Prefix/Postfix pair re-plans the objective-path groups vanilla moved from an assumed enemy point (nearest enemy-owned entry point under `RangeOnly`, Visible centroid first under `FogGrid`), places the ones whose choice differs, and sets a third of the eligible direct children back as a reserve echelon; all as immediate-placement executor Moves (`DeploymentPlanRuntime`, `DeploymentDoctrine`); the allocator gives a child the executor placed as a reserve (`DirectChildEvidence.DeployedReserve`) the Reserve role first. *Arrival guard (#112):* record-only sizing of the vanilla entry re-path over executor-ordered members (`TacticalArrivalRepath` rows; step B, the guard, is not built). - **Direct children (army-children fix, 2026-10-17).** `DirectChildDiscovery` lists an army's command-level direct children from the snapshot's parent map (`BattleSnapshotAssembler.IsDirectChild`: not routed, on the field, not a skirmisher or scout company). `permanentlydetached` is not part of the relation: vanilla sets it on the groups the AI engine drives, so excluding it dropped every division or brigade under the army and left one synthetic child per army. The first plan pick registers the discovered children before the forced feasibility evaluation (`PickInitialPlanInOrder`), so the first ledger prices real children. A command in no army (an active division or corps with no parent group, `ParentInstanceId` 0, at or above the minimum command level) is itself a direct child of the side, attached to the side's first army for the log label (the loose-top loop of `DirectChildDiscovery`), so its brigades are commanded and not left to the command tree's default Reserve/Hold. Fixtures for the Union and CSA hierarchies at the W&L depth (shift -1) and the default depth (shift 0): `ArmyChildrenTests`.
- **Crossing move gate (army-children fix, 2026-10-17).** A doctrine Move (source `PostureExecutor`, `DivisionPlay` or `ReserveRelease`, which carry the Screen/Probe moves, the leaf slots, the play and maneuver legs, and the reserve release) whose start and target lie in different land regions of the BC14 `RiverRegions` is checked by `RiverCrossingRule` in the executor's step 8, over BC14's own crossing graph (a link joins up to four regions; a shortest path of links, so a leg over several rivers is routed link by link, the first two crossings being the vias and the group re-orders from where they leave it). Every leg of the move is checked (start to the first via, via to via, last via to the target): a via the move already has (a road via, a play's march or attack via) is kept as the mover chose it, and a leg that needs a crossing gets it ahead of that via (the vias become the crossings and the mover's own, in order, within the executor's two-via bound; a move that would need more is refused, `SkipReason.NoKnownCrossing`). The one exception is a BC10 road route (`IntentFlags.RoadRoute`, set where the posture request carries the road planner's two vias): the leg between its two vias follows the road, which takes its own crossing, so no via is inserted there and only the crossing it passes is checked (the crossings it checks are the ones the road actually takes, `RoadRoute.Link0/Link1`: the BC14 links the road crosses, found where the road is planned by walking its nodes between the vias: at each change of land region (a node on a bridge or ford is on no land) the link joining those two regions that lies nearest the road's own stretch from the last land node to the first node of the new region (a long run along the river band is not summed up by its midpoint; links within a metre of each other are told apart by the stretch's midpoint), in road order, at most two, none when a change has no link joining it; a stretch that changes land region more than twice is no road route (the planner does not use it: the move goes out as an ordinary move, gated leg by leg); a road that leaves its bank and comes back records both crossings, and its leg is checked at them although both ends lie on one bank (the same chain rule; a record that does not chain on one bank leaves the leg unchecked); the gate uses the recorded links only when they chain from the leg's start region to its target region (one link touching both, or two touching them in order and sharing a middle region), otherwise, or with none recorded, the best route over every link; each must pass the rule of a move off the road: Clear or Watched goes, a non-Held crossing the selected plan chose goes, a Held one goes only for an attack-stance task whose selected plan chose that crossing, anything else is refused and an Unknown one posts the look); the legs before the road entry and after the road exit are gated and get vias as above, within the two-via bound. A link carries the move when the side perceives it Clear or Watched, or when the selected plan chose it (`FeasibilityLedger.IsPlannedLink`) and it is not Held; a Held crossing the plan chose carries only an attack task (the tasks that map to the attack stance: attack, support attack, fix, counterstroke): a Screen, a Probe, a march or a release never crosses a Held crossing. The link graph (each link's regions and point, the links that share a region, their distances) is built once per map and shared by both sides (battle state: `BattleScope` drops it with the battle), and the search stops at the first route that cannot be beaten (252 links, three crossings: ~0.3 ms a call in the harness, `INFO RIVER-ROUTE`; the real maps have a few links). Otherwise the move is not made (`SkipReason.NoKnownCrossing`, first-fire `[CrossingGate]` lines) and the first Unknown link of the best route over every link is posted as a look (`FeasibilityLedger.PostGateLook`): the look mechanism of BC14 below publishes it in the order the selected plan's looks, the gate's look, the other candidates' and the Defender's list (at most two requests; an unresolved gate look is not overwritten by a newer refusal, and ends when the crossing is no longer Unknown or after an hour of doctrine time), BC9's recon looks at it, and the move is made once the crossing reads Clear. A Held crossing the plan did not choose is no way across and no look. A withdrawal, a fallback task, an in-contact withdrawal, a scout's look (it exists to make a crossing known) and an immediate placement are not gated; with no river map the gate lets every move through. Rollback: `Tactical / Enable Crossing Move Gate`. Vanilla's own NavMesh already routes a path over a ford or bridge (rivers cost 500000); the gate adds the side's knowledge of which crossing and whether it is covered.
- **Defender reserve inside a holding command (army-children fix).** The leaf cascade of a Defender holds back `DeploymentDoctrine.ReserveShare(N)` (floor(N / 3) from N = 3) of a holding command's N subordinates as Reserve (under a Main, SupportMain or Fix command alike), interior and rearmost first, never the anchor (nor an envelopment's second anchor) or an end flank guard; an exposed end draws the nearest reserve to guard it; rows `TacticalDefenderReserve` (`reserved`, `thin`) carry the numbers and the reason (`thin-numbers`, `no-free-brigade`, `released-to-flank`). *Release:* the held reserves are not held for the whole battle. `LeafReserveRelease` runs the army's own `DirectChildAllocator.ReleaseReserves` (crisis, stagger by interval and floor, threatened wing) over each holding command's subordinates, each command with its own `ReserveReleaseState`; the evidence is the perceived picture only (`EnemyVisibleState`: the sector a subordinate stands in by its live position, the strengths seen there, fire seen, the cascade's flank bucket), so an enemy the side has not perceived releases nothing. A released reserve takes the role the allocator gives (Fix on a crisis, SupportMain on the stagger, RefuseLeft / RefuseRight on a wing), the leaf map is rebuilt with it, and it is a reserve again when the trigger ends. Crossing guards are not carried to a subordinate command (the guard point is read by the play executor for a direct child): a reserve division is released to a crossing by the army's allocation.
  *On the field (`LeafReservePlanner`, run by `ReserveReleaseIntentProducer`; rides `Enable Tactical Orchestrator Reserve Commit Gate`):* a Defender's leaf tasks are all hold tasks, so the role changes alone moved nothing. A held reserve child stands in a slot behind its holding command's line: the centre of the standing (non-reserve) subordinates less `DeploymentDoctrine.DivisionSupportMeters` (200 m, BC16 decision 5's constant) along the front axis, on lanes of at most `ReserveLaneMeters` across it in the order the reserves already stand (the geometry of #60's reserve echelon at division scale). The slot stays in the BC14 land region of the line centre (pulled back toward the line until it is, else no slot), inside the own deployment zone while the deployment phase is on (`Frontline2.GetClosestPointInDeploymentZone`, as #60 clamps), and the executor's validation steps it back from water and fences. A released reserve goes to its trigger point: the standing line of the crisis band, the exposed wing's band or the main effort's band, stood off `ReleaseStandOffMeters` behind it, and returns to its slot when the release ends. ## Scoped State

Per-battle and per-campaign statics register their own reset with `Util/ScopedState.cs`: `BattleScope.Register(name, reset)` / `CampaignScope.Register(name, reset)`, normally from the owner's static constructor. ## Doctrine Clock

`TacticalBattleCoordinator.DoctrineBattleHours` (exposed as `TacticalDoctrineTime.NowBattleSeconds` / `NowBattleHours`) is the one clock for tactical doctrine timers. It follows `GameVars.currenttimefromstart` (battle game hours), samples at most once per frame, never runs backwards, restarts for a new `BattleUnits` instance, is 0 outside a battle, and is frozen while `BattleUnits.eodcycle != 0` (vanilla's end-of-day sequence, where the raw clock jumps about 9 hours).

The night is the coordinator's: while the end-of-day gate reports `Suspended`, `Tick` returns without doctrine work. On `Resumed` it forces a heavy snapshot rebuild for both sides and flags a night replan; on that side's next tick the night replan (`ReplanForNewBattleDay`: re-infer intent, replan) runs instead of the cadence replan, and it counts against `Min Replan Seconds`. Orchestrators, ledgers and plan memory are kept across the night.

Readers: plan age, phase age and the replan cooldown (`ArmyTickCycle.MaybeReplan`), phase budgets and main-effort/exploit windows, opportunity hysteresis, the reinforcement-opportunity update, contact-ledger decay, perception memory (seen/remembered ages), the operations-ledger clock (director commit windows), the order executor's cooldowns (recent order, task hold, courier cadence, failed-write backoff) and outcome stamps, the producers' windows (facing pulse, fire control, main-arrival hold, artillery hold, charge refusal back-off), and `TacticalStanceWriter`'s hold records. Only vanilla's own `lastaistancechangetime` stamp, which the writer sets for `AdjustGroupAIStance`, stays on the raw `currenttimefromstart`, because vanilla compares it with that clock (4264).

## Front Axis And Sectors

`TacticalFrontAxis` is one side's front geometry: forward from the own centroid toward the visible-enemy centroid (or an objective), lateral = forward rotated 90 degrees clockwise, so lower sector ids are the army's left. Sectors are `DefaultBandCount = 5` equal-width lateral bands over the own army's lateral extent (padded to 100 world units; units beyond it clamp into the edge bands), so a sector is ground, not a list position. `Stabilize` keeps the previous heading unless it changes by at least 10 degrees. The axis also records the visible enemy extent for flank overlap, and `FlankExposureBucket` (0-3) combines wing position, enemy overlap and the group's mean vanilla outflanked weight.

## Intel Confidence And Odds

Enemy figures come only from the side's battle-snapshot perception: visible enemies in full, remembered ones weighted by confidence (see [Battle Core](#battle-core) and [`docs/vanilla-vision-and-movement.md`](vanilla-vision-and-movement.md)). `TacticalEnemyContactLedger` keeps the largest enemy strength seen (fading 10% per battle hour without re-sighting; only a visible enemy or fire stamps contact) and:

```
confidence = coverage x entryFactor x contactRecency
coverage      = mean over the current coverage bands of the observation recency of the ground each spans
entryFactor   = 0.5 + 0.5 x (watched share of enemy-owned map entry points; 1 when there are none)
recency(t)    = clamp(1 - t / 2 h, 0, 1)
```

Coverage bands span own units plus known enemy positions; observation stamps are kept on 25 m lateral cells of the front axis (re-based when the heading changes by more than 1 degree), so recency lives on the ground, not on band indices. Enemy size plays no part.

Odds blend monotonically toward unknown (1) with confidence and are capped at `EnemyVisibleSector.MaxKnowableOdds = 10`:

- army: `ArmyEvidenceBuilder.KnownEnemyOdds = 1 + confidence x (min(own / estimate, 10) - 1)`;
- band: `EnemyVisibleState.SectorOdds = 1 + confidence x (bandOdds - 1)`.

Under low confidence army odds therefore read as unknown. `TacticalReinforcementOpportunityDoctrine` returns `NoOpportunity` ("intel-confidence-too-low") below `MinIntelConfidenceForAction = 0.35`, and the probe commit in `TacticalPhaseProgressionDoctrine` uses the same constant. What raises the confidence is looking: the BC9 recon producer sends cavalry to stale bands and unwatched entry points, the Probe phase pushes one group per empty band forward, and the operation director scouts (phase `Scouting`, reason `no-contact-scout`) rather than committing on zero enemy strength at a newly selected objective. (`ArmyIntentInference.ConfidenceFloor = 0.3` is the separate intent-inference confidence.)

## Doctrine Components

- **Reinforcement opportunity** (`TacticalReinforcementOpportunityDoctrine`): `AttackNow`, `WaitAndConsolidate`, `DefensiveHold`, `DelayingDefense`, `WithdrawalToFightLater` or `NoOpportunity`. `AttackNow` needs the ratio over the commander's threshold and either a known enemy below us that will not catch up or, in a Meeting/Attacker battle, parity that holds through the attack window. There is no defeat-in-detail window and no enemy arrival schedule: enemy reinforcements count once they are in range. A `Defender` side (vanilla `GetBattleType`) never gets `AttackNow`.
- **Phase progression** (`TacticalPhaseProgressionDoctrine`, wired into `ArmyTickCycle.MaybeReplan`): Probe, MainEffort, Exploit, Consolidate, Withdraw, from plan/phase age, odds, morale, reserves and sector readiness. In the Probe phase the BC9 recon planner nominates one probe group per empty front band (reconnaissance in force).
- **Sector readiness** (`TacticalSectorReadinessDoctrine`): effective force = strength x (1 - fatigue) x ammo x morale; outcomes PushReady, PushDegraded, HoldForReinforcements, HoldFatigued (below 25% effectiveness).
- **Main-effort shift** (`ArmyOrchestrator.ConsiderMainEffortShift`): moves the main-effort sector only when the candidate's odds beat the current sector by 25%.
- **Offensive replan triggers** (`ArmyReplanTriggers`): `BreakthroughOpportunity` (another sector beats the main effort by 0.5 odds) and `MainEffortLocalBreakthrough` (local odds at 1.35x history or 1.8 absolute).
- **Role cascade** (`TacticalRoleCascade`, `TacticalLeafBrigadeMap`): depth-agnostic role propagation down the command tree, with `DoubleWing` / `DoubleWingEchelon` envelopment modes chosen by commander aggression (0.6 threshold); under envelopment pressure a playbook's reserve-commit trigger odds are lowered to at most 1.1.
- **Playbooks** (`TacticalPlaybookCatalog`, 19 seeded by `BuiltInPlaybooks`, `FocalAssault` last; with the ledger set (BC14) a candidate is skipped when Impossible and loses 0.4 x its feasibility penalty, see Battle Core): stance precondition filter (`StanceAllowsPlaybook`: offensive playbooks never for a Defender, defensive never for an Attacker) plus envelopment and defensive-pressure score biases. Geometric play templates live in `Orchestrator/Plays/` (`Enable Geometric Play Templates`).

Reasons: `first-tick`, `signature-change`, `pending-change`, `max-interval-force`, `throttled-pending`, `stable-under-max`, `realtime-floor`, `clock-rewind`.
- **Heavy path, on Run:** `TacticalBattleSnapshotBuilder.Build` (army evidence, objective records with approach avenues, command tree, direct children) publishes an immutable `TacticalBattleRuntimeSnapshot`, reused by the tick cycle, direct-child cycle and operations ledger. Builds alternate between the sides on consecutive Ticks when both have a cached snapshot.
- **Operations ledger:** re-ticks only on a new snapshot, changed command-node intents, a Commander Mode change, or 60 battle seconds (`ShouldTickOperationsLedger`).
- **Compression:** the review cycle is scaled by `ScaleCycleForCompression` (unchanged up to 4x, linear to 5x at 20x), and a real-time floor bounds heavy builds under compression.
- **Urgent boundary:** the producers never call the gate or the heavy build; they pair the last published ledger state with the per-tick battle snapshot (rebuilt every tick at 5866).

Config (`[TacticalTickOptimization]`): `Enable Tactical Heavy Path Throttling = true`, `Heavy Ledger Review Cycle Hours = 0.003`, `Heavy Ledger Review Min Realtime Seconds = 4`. Setting the throttle false builds every Tick. Gate decisions are `[TacticalHeavyGate]` telemetry rows. Measurement notes: `docs/tactical-tick-optimization-task10-baseline.md`, `docs/tactical-tick-optimization-task11-smoke.md`.
