# Tactical Orchestrator

Living reference for the Grand Tactician tactical orchestrator. It describes current behaviour; build, deploy, harness count and smoke status live in [`docs/handoff.md`](handoff.md). Slice specs and plans under `docs/superpowers/` are point-in-time artifacts.

## Architecture

The tactical orchestrator is a per-battle brain layered over vanilla `AIBattle` methods:

- `TacticalBattleCoordinator` owns battle lifecycle, the doctrine clock and the two per-side orchestrators.
- `TacticalBattleOrchestrator` owns one side.
- `ArmyOrchestrator` owns the army plan, enemy-intent observation, the command-node tree, direct-child roles, command intent resolution and the operations ledger.
- Generic command-node snapshots model the vanilla `Regiment` hierarchy. There are no `CorpsOrchestrator`, `DivisionOrchestrator` or `BrigadeOrchestrator` classes.
- The battle core (`Tactical/Battle/`, [Battle Core](#battle-core)) is the only path for Whiskey's battle movement, stance, formation, facing and combat-behavior writes: intent producers run in one arbiter tick, and an order executor issues the winners. Patches read orchestrator state; they do not write it. The posture producers' orchestrator inputs (AI pool, leaf brigade map, live commander initiative, reinforcement opportunity) are refreshed by `ArmyPostureInputsRuntime.Refresh` from the coordinator tick.

Command nodes come from the side's battle snapshot. The snapshot's command parent map (plan D5) scans each group's `allattachedunits` (every descendant) once per roster change; a unit's direct parent is the containing group with the smallest `allattachedunits.Length`. That reproduces vanilla's own direct relation (`GetAttachedUnitsReg(directonly)` tests `transform.parent == this`) without reading `transform.parent` or allocating a `GetAttachedUnitsReg` list. `DirectChildDiscoveryRuntime` and `CommandTreeRuntime` read `UnitRecord.ParentIndex` / `BattleSnapshot.DirectChildren`; `Regiment.unittyp` and `GamePrefs.commandhierarchyshift` give the echelons.

## Lifecycle

- #35 `TacticalObserverPatch` (Postfix on `AIBattle.CheckGlobalAIStrategy`) drives the coordinator when `Enable Tactical Battle Orchestrator` is on: a battle-identity detector issues Start / End / Restart, then the coordinator `Tick` runs. Battle end is also observed on `BattleUnits.OnDestroy`.
- The W&L feud instance (`isplayeraiorfeud == 2`) never drives lifecycle or `Tick` (`TacticalBattleCoordinator.DrivesOrchestrator`).
- At start the coordinator suppresses one alliance (`ResolveSuppressedAllianceId`): a W&L Commander-in-Chief player's alliance, otherwise the player's alliance outside `ai_vs_ai`. A W&L subordinate career suppresses nothing; its units are protected per unit by `TacticalWriteGuard`.
- `Tick` runs once per vanilla side-stat cycle per battle (the second side's `CheckGlobalAIStrategy` in the same cycle is skipped) and drives each side against that side's own `AIBattle` (`ArmyEvidenceBuilder.ResolveAiBattleForAlliance` / `SelectAiBattleIndexForSide`: `sideofai` match, feud instance skipped). Each `AIBattle.objectivechain` holds only its own side's groups.
- `OnBattleEnd` calls `BattleScope.ResetAll()` and drops the orchestrators.

## Battle Core

Plan and spec: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-15-battle-core.md`, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-battle-core-design.md` (archived after the in-game smoke).

- **Snapshot (D1/D2).** `BattleCoreTick.EnsureSnapshot(AIBattle, alliance)` builds one pooled `BattleSnapshot` per AI side per frame (`BattleSnapshotBuilder`, one pass over `completeunitlist`): own units and enemy records as `UnitRecord`s, the D5 parent map, ownership flags, derived pressure arrays. `TacticalBattleCoordinator.EnsureBattleCoreSnapshots` builds every AI-run side first in the `CheckGlobalAIStrategy` postfix (5866), whatever the orchestrator flag and in every Commander Mode (it writes nothing); #60 builds its side's snapshot at deployment. Patches read it through `BattleCore.TryGetSnapshot`. Two pooled instances per side; the snapshot is the per-tick memo.
- **Perception (R1).** `BattlePerceptionProbe` runs after each build: an enemy is seen when vanilla's own AI-side fog grid shows it (`FogOfWar.UnitIsHidden`, arguments as 127225) or it is in melee; a player-alliance AI side (W&L, `ai_vs_ai`) uses vanilla's `iswithinfog`; while fog is off, with HQ in the saddle (101312) or before the grid is painted the side falls back to vanilla's range lists. `BattlePerception` keeps hidden enemies as Remembered at their last position (confidence halves every 20 battle minutes, dropped at 90) and counts reinforcements only once their entry point is seen. `SlicePressureBuilder` fills slice, flank and closest-visible-enemy arrays from Visible plus confidence-weighted Remembered observations. Evidence, contact ledger, objective records, plays, posture, artillery, charge and feud targets read these, never vanilla's unfiltered enemy fields. Rollback: `Enable Battle Fog-Honest Perception = false` (range-only for every side).
- **Ownership (BC3).** `PlayerOwnership` sets `PlayerOwned` (W&L player's command, its descendants, take-overs, a CIC player's whole alliance; outside W&L the player alliance unless `ai_vs_ai`), the chain flags and `Feud` (a unit's own `ai_feudstance`, propagated from its AI group and command ancestors). `TacticalChainOrderPlanner` fans a group order out to its children when a player-owned descendant is exposed (no detached node between them, 119865), and `TacticalPlayerDispatchRuntime.Emit` sends the player's share of an ancestor's move as one W&L dispatch.
- **Arbiter and executor (D3/D4/D6).** #68 `BattleCoreArbiterPatch` (Postfix on `AdjustGroupFormations` 5870, `Priority.Last`) runs `BattleCoreTick.RunArbiter`: producers submit `BattleOrderIntent`s, `OrderArbiter` keeps one winner per (group, channel) by priority (Safety > Executor > Play > Support > Scorer, ties keep last tick's source), `OrderExecutor` plans each winner (guard, chain, `WaypointValidator`: map clamp, water/fence step-back, friendly footprint, artillery fire line and point blank; `SilentExitPrecheck`; dedupe within 60 m; recent-order, task-hold, courier and failed-write cooldowns) and `OrderExecutorRuntime` issues it (stance before move before formation). Last tick's moves resolve to Moved, Failed (one ±30 degree retry, then GaveUp) or Superseded. Claims are per (unit, channel), so a group Move whose descendant holds a higher-priority Move claim in the same tick fans out around that subtree (`OrderExecutor.BeginTickClaims`, `TacticalChainOrderPlanner`): vanilla's group formation re-paths every child (92076-92108), and a regiment's Safety withdrawal must survive its brigade's Executor move. The arbiter stands down while the master `Enabled` switch is off or Commander Mode is not Active. Patches that must act inside a vanilla call (#42 feud replay, #56 courier parity) run the same pipeline inline (`TryPlanInline` / `ExecutePlannedInline`), refused under a higher arbiter claim.
- **Producers** (built once from `BattleProducerRegistry.Order`, run in this order): Withdrawal (Safety), ArtilleryDoctrine and ArtilleryPointBlank, GroupStance (Scorer), ChargeDemotion (Safety), Posture (Executor; #61's ledger posture and leaf brigades), FacingPulse (Scorer), FireControl (Executor), DivisionPlay (Play), Recon (Support, BC9 reconnaissance), ReserveRelease (Support), Charge (Executor, the infantry charge doctrine). D8: the posture executor beats the division play on the same channel, so a play order wins only where the posture producer submitted nothing for that group. Rollback: the producer's own flag in `Plugin.cs` where it has one, otherwise `Tactical Commander Mode = MonitorOnly`/`Off`.
- **Reconnaissance (BC9).** The fog-honest AI goes and looks (`Battle/BattleRecon.cs`, runtime `Producers/ReconIntentProducer.cs`; plan § BC9). *Cavalry recon:* when an enemy-side entry point has not been watched for an hour (the contact ledger keeps per-entry-point watch stamps), a front band's ground is stale (recency < 0.5), or confidence is below the 0.35 gate (straight ahead before any contact, the nearest remembered enemy after it), one standing AI cavalry regiment of a settled brigade (not ordered by posture this tick, not moving, not linked-group-moved) rides to a stand point 150 m short of the target (vanilla `distancetoenemytocancelinterruption`), stops on first sight, observes 0.05 h and rides back to its brigade slot; never toward a visible enemy at least as strong (stand-off or the 100 m corridor of its path); at most 4 starts per side per doctrine hour; advanced only on the executor's Issued outcome (fail closed); another source's move or vanilla's re-lay ends it. Scout companies (type 4) are not used: vanilla re-paths them to their parent's target every update (`CheckScoutMovingTargets` 124616-124660). *Reconnaissance in force:* in the army's Probe phase, a side that is not the Defender gets one AI-pool group per front band with no visible enemy nominated (not linked, no player-owned unit below, not moving); the posture pipeline rewrites it to role Probe / task Probe (screen stance via the task mapper) toward a point one infantry sight depth (200 m) ahead; on first sight within 200 m the recon producer sends one Stop (screen stance is not stopped by vanilla contact, 117253). Once per band per doctrine hour. A probe that ends short of a visible enemy at least as strong (weaker contact, arrival, 0.25 h) leaves its group holding the ground gained: the posture pipeline drops a move more than 60 m back along the probe's advance (`BattleRecon.HoldBlocks`; forward bounds, break-offs and fallbacks go) until the group's posture task differs from the one it was nominated under; a superior contact returns it to its ledger task. A Defender side never probes and never gets Probe tasks (the director's `defender-screen`, GT recon's Defender screen) but its cavalry still scouts. *Look points:* every Scout/Probe task targets `BattleRecon.LookPoint` (200 m along the front axis, toward the objective and no farther before any sighting) instead of an objective stand-off. *Facing:* screens/probes/flank guards with nothing in sight face the stalest unwatched entry point. *Skirmishers:* `SkirmisherScreenDistancePatch` pushes an AI skirmisher company of a screening (or advancing attack-stance) line from vanilla's 30 m link to 100 m while nothing is in its range. Rollback: `Enable Battle Reconnaissance = false`.
- **Telemetry.** `[BattleCore] producers count=12 order=…` once per battle; `[OrderArbiter] alliance=… intents=… winners=… issued=… moved=… failed=… retried=… gaveUp=… skipped=… deduped=… superseded=… topSkip=…` every 30 s real time per side; `[OrderArbiter] first vanilla order issued channel=… source=…` once per battle; `[BattlePerception] alliance=… mode=… reason=… visible=… remembered=…` once per mode per battle; `[BattleRecon] first reconnaissance: …` / `first reconnaissance in force: …` / `first skirmisher screen pushed out: …` once per battle and `TacticalRecon` Decision rows (mission start/end, probe nomination). Scopes: `tactical.snapshot.build`, `tactical.perception`, `tactical.arbiter.resolve`, `tactical.arbiter.execute`, `tactical.producer.*`, `tactical.patch.battle-core-arbiter`.

## Scoped State

Per-battle and per-campaign statics register their own reset with `Util/ScopedState.cs`: `BattleScope.Register(name, reset)` / `CampaignScope.Register(name, reset)`, normally from the owner's static constructor. Battle teardown calls `BattleScope.ResetAll()` and `StrategicCoordinator.ResetAll` calls `CampaignScope.ResetAll()`. Each reset runs in its own try/catch and a failure logs one `[ScopedState]` warning per name. No teardown path hand-wires individual resets.

## Doctrine Clock

`TacticalBattleCoordinator.DoctrineBattleHours` (exposed as `TacticalDoctrineTime.NowBattleSeconds` / `NowBattleHours`) is the one clock for tactical doctrine timers. It follows `GameVars.currenttimefromstart` (battle game hours), samples at most once per frame, never runs backwards, restarts for a new `BattleUnits` instance, is 0 outside a battle, and is frozen while `BattleUnits.eodcycle != 0` (vanilla's end-of-day sequence, where the raw clock jumps about 9 hours).

The night is the coordinator's: while the end-of-day gate reports `Suspended`, `Tick` returns without doctrine work. On `Resumed` it forces a heavy snapshot rebuild for both sides and flags a night replan; on that side's next tick the night replan (`ReplanForNewBattleDay`: re-infer intent, replan) runs instead of the cadence replan, and it counts against `Min Replan Seconds`. Orchestrators, ledgers and plan memory are kept across the night.

Readers: plan age, phase age and the replan cooldown (`ArmyTickCycle.MaybeReplan`), phase budgets and main-effort/exploit windows, opportunity hysteresis, the reinforcement-opportunity update, contact-ledger decay, perception memory (seen/remembered ages), the operations-ledger clock (director commit windows), the order executor's cooldowns (recent order, task hold, courier cadence, failed-write backoff) and outcome stamps, the producers' windows (facing pulse, fire control, main-arrival hold, artillery hold, charge refusal back-off), and `TacticalStanceWriter`'s hold records. Only vanilla's own `lastaistancechangetime` stamp, which the writer sets for `AdjustGroupAIStance`, stays on the raw `currenttimefromstart`, because vanilla compares it with that clock (4264).

## Front Axis And Sectors

`TacticalFrontAxis` is one side's front geometry: forward from the own centroid toward the visible-enemy centroid (or an objective), lateral = forward rotated 90 degrees clockwise, so lower sector ids are the army's left. Sectors are `DefaultBandCount = 5` equal-width lateral bands over the own army's lateral extent (padded to 100 world units; units beyond it clamp into the edge bands), so a sector is ground, not a list position. `Stabilize` keeps the previous heading unless it changes by at least 10 degrees. The axis also records the visible enemy extent for flank overlap, and `FlankExposureBucket` (0-3) combines wing position, enemy overlap and the group's mean vanilla outflanked weight.

## Intel Confidence And Odds

Enemy figures come only from the side's battle-snapshot perception: visible enemies in full, remembered ones weighted by confidence (see [Battle Core](#battle-core) and [`docs/vanilla-vision-and-movement.md`](vanilla-vision-and-movement.md)). `TacticalEnemyContactLedger` keeps the largest enemy strength seen (fading 10% per battle hour without re-sighting; only a visible enemy or fire stamps contact) and:

```
confidence = coverage x entryFactor x contactRecency
coverage      = mean over the current coverage bands of the observation recency of the ground each spans
entryFactor   = 0.5 + 0.5 x (watched share of enemy-owned map entry points; 1 when there are none)
recency(t)    = clamp(1 - t / 2 h, 0, 1)
```

Coverage bands span own units plus known enemy positions; observation stamps are kept on 25 m lateral cells of the front axis (re-based when the heading changes by more than 1 degree), so recency lives on the ground, not on band indices. Enemy size plays no part.

Odds blend monotonically toward unknown (1) with confidence and are capped at `EnemyVisibleSector.MaxKnowableOdds = 10`:

- army: `ArmyEvidenceBuilder.KnownEnemyOdds = 1 + confidence x (min(own / estimate, 10) - 1)`;
- band: `EnemyVisibleState.SectorOdds = 1 + confidence x (bandOdds - 1)`.

Under low confidence army odds therefore read as unknown. `TacticalReinforcementOpportunityDoctrine` returns `NoOpportunity` ("intel-confidence-too-low") below `MinIntelConfidenceForAction = 0.35`, and the probe commit in `TacticalPhaseProgressionDoctrine` uses the same constant. What raises the confidence is looking: the BC9 recon producer sends cavalry to stale bands and unwatched entry points, the Probe phase pushes one group per empty band forward, and the operation director scouts (phase `Scouting`, reason `no-contact-scout`) rather than committing on zero enemy strength at a newly selected objective. (`ArmyIntentInference.ConfidenceFloor = 0.3` is the separate intent-inference confidence.)

## Doctrine Components

- **Reinforcement opportunity** (`TacticalReinforcementOpportunityDoctrine`): `AttackNow`, `WaitAndConsolidate`, `DefensiveHold`, `DelayingDefense`, `WithdrawalToFightLater` or `NoOpportunity`. `AttackNow` needs the ratio over the commander's threshold and either a known enemy below us that will not catch up or, in a Meeting/Attacker battle, parity that holds through the attack window. There is no defeat-in-detail window and no enemy arrival schedule: enemy reinforcements count once they are in range. A `Defender` side (vanilla `GetBattleType`) never gets `AttackNow`.
- **Phase progression** (`TacticalPhaseProgressionDoctrine`, wired into `ArmyTickCycle.MaybeReplan`): Probe, MainEffort, Exploit, Consolidate, Withdraw, from plan/phase age, odds, morale, reserves and sector readiness. In the Probe phase the BC9 recon planner nominates one probe group per empty front band (reconnaissance in force).
- **Sector readiness** (`TacticalSectorReadinessDoctrine`): effective force = strength x (1 - fatigue) x ammo x morale; outcomes PushReady, PushDegraded, HoldForReinforcements, HoldFatigued (below 25% effectiveness).
- **Main-effort shift** (`ArmyOrchestrator.ConsiderMainEffortShift`): moves the main-effort sector only when the candidate's odds beat the current sector by 25%.
- **Offensive replan triggers** (`ArmyReplanTriggers`): `BreakthroughOpportunity` (another sector beats the main effort by 0.5 odds) and `MainEffortLocalBreakthrough` (local odds at 1.35x history or 1.8 absolute).
- **Role cascade** (`TacticalRoleCascade`, `TacticalLeafBrigadeMap`): depth-agnostic role propagation down the command tree, with `DoubleWing` / `DoubleWingEchelon` envelopment modes chosen by commander aggression (0.6 threshold); under envelopment pressure a playbook's reserve-commit trigger odds are lowered to at most 1.1.
- **Playbooks** (`TacticalPlaybookCatalog`, 18 seeded by `BuiltInPlaybooks`): stance precondition filter (`StanceAllowsPlaybook`: offensive playbooks never for a Defender, defensive never for an Attacker) plus envelopment and defensive-pressure score biases. Geometric play templates live in `Orchestrator/Plays/` (`Enable Geometric Play Templates`).

## Battle Write Ownership

- **Write path:** every Whiskey movement, stance, formation, facing and combat-behavior write goes through the battle-core order executor (producers via #68, #42/#56 inline). The remaining writers are #59's reserve rollback, #60's deployment `SetGroupFormation`, #42's no-snapshot fallback and #44's `macroai`.
- **Write guard:** every write passes `TacticalWriteGuard`, a thin reader of `PlayerOwnership`: `CanWrite(in UnitRecord)` for the executor's plan, and the `Regiment` overloads (`CanWrite`, `CanWrite(unit, feudReplay)`, `IsPlayerProtected`) for live re-checks and writers that cannot fan out, which also refuse a group with an exposed player-owned descendant. PlayerOwned is never written; Feud is refused except for #42's own feud replay. Any exception means "do not write". Battle-level writers also skip the feud instance (`IsFeudInstance`).
- **Commander Mode** (`Tactical Commander Mode`, default `Active`): `Active` runs the ledger and allows writes; `MonitorOnly` runs the snapshot, perception, ledger and telemetry with no writes (the arbiter stands down); `Off` also stops the ledger (rollback).
- **ai_stance:** written only by `Util/TacticalStanceWriter` (the executor's Stance channel), on AI pool groups only (`unittyp > 13`, in `aibattle[0]/[1].unitsused`), never 0 or 4. Rules are in `Tactical/TacticalStanceOwnership.cs`: a write or agreeing claim stamps vanilla's `lastaistancechangetime` so `AdjustGroupAIStance` leaves the group alone for `HoldHours = 0.25`; the executor's sources (posture, withdrawal, …) outrank the group-stance scorer, and the same source waits `MinSelfChangeHours = 0.05` before changing its own opinion. Whiskey yields (releases the stamp and drops its record) to vanilla's per-group weak-group defend/screen, to vanilla's assault escalation only when Whiskey's own target is attack, and to army retreat (`macroai 3`, `TacticalStanceWriter.ReleaseForRetreat` from the arbiter gate). A lower-priority source cannot yield a higher-priority hold. Vanilla's side-level lines (4248 `macroai 2` defend-all, 4256 outnumbered screen) are outside the yield: the side-level answer belongs to #44.
- **Charge denial:** vanilla assault (stance 4) is replaced only by the ChargeDemotion producer (Safety, `StanceWriteSource.ChargeDemotion`, which precedes the yields) from the coordinator's cached `TacticalChargeDenial` decision: doctrine Deny demotes to defend (2), doctrine Allow keeps the assault, and with the orchestrator army off a legacy local-reaction DenyCharge demotes to attack (3). Behind `Enable Tactical Charge Denial`. The infantry charge doctrine reverts its charges once the group leaves assault.
- **macroai:** #44 `BattleMacroStrategyPatch` is the single owner of the instance `AIBattle.macroai` (Postfix on `CheckGlobalAIStrategy`). It never writes retreat (3), defers to scripted `GameVars.aistrategy` / `sideinformation.macroai`, an end-of-battle state and the feud instance, and takes no opinion from an army in Commander Mode `Off` or a missing side orchestrator. The operations-ledger opinion wins over the fallback (reinforcement-opportunity mapping behind `Enable Vanilla Macro AI Sync`, or the legacy scorer when the orchestrator army is off). On the W&L player side only a ledger opinion that differs from vanilla's current and pending value is written, never for a Commander-in-Chief player. A non-blocking, value-restoring Prefix puts vanilla's own value back before vanilla runs, so vanilla's timers never see Whiskey's value.

## Tactical Tick Optimization

The coordinator gates its heavy work per side (design and plan archived under `docs/superpowers/{specs,plans}/archive/2026-05-1[67]-tactical-tick-optimization-*`):

- **Cheap path, every Tick:** `TacticalBattleSnapshotBuilder.ExtractCurrentSignature` + `TacticalHeavyPathGate.Decide`, evaluated once per side per Tick (`EnsureSideSnapshot`). Reasons: `first-tick`, `signature-change`, `pending-change`, `max-interval-force`, `throttled-pending`, `stable-under-max`, `realtime-floor`, `clock-rewind`.
- **Heavy path, on Run:** `TacticalBattleSnapshotBuilder.Build` (army evidence, objective records with approach avenues, command tree, direct children) publishes an immutable `TacticalBattleRuntimeSnapshot`, reused by the tick cycle, direct-child cycle and operations ledger. Builds alternate between the sides on consecutive Ticks when both have a cached snapshot.
- **Operations ledger:** re-ticks only on a new snapshot, changed command-node intents, a Commander Mode change, or 60 battle seconds (`ShouldTickOperationsLedger`).
- **Compression:** the review cycle is scaled by `ScaleCycleForCompression` (unchanged up to 4x, linear to 5x at 20x), and a real-time floor bounds heavy builds under compression.
- **Urgent boundary:** the producers never call the gate or the heavy build; they pair the last published ledger state with the per-tick battle snapshot (rebuilt every tick at 5866).

Config (`[TacticalTickOptimization]`): `Enable Tactical Heavy Path Throttling = true`, `Heavy Ledger Review Cycle Hours = 0.003`, `Heavy Ledger Review Min Realtime Seconds = 4`. Setting the throttle false builds every Tick. Gate decisions are `[TacticalHeavyGate]` telemetry rows. Measurement notes: `docs/tactical-tick-optimization-task10-baseline.md`, `docs/tactical-tick-optimization-task11-smoke.md`.
