# Live Theater Campaign Planner — Design

**Status:** approved direction (user, 2026-10-14). Decisions:
- History is a **weight**; live scoring decides.
- Commander **personality is a modifier** on live scoring, taken from the real vanilla commanders (no hardcoded Lincoln/Davis).
- **Succession = both:** the ~12 historical succession events stay as a prior that fires when its historical conditions hold; every other appointment is by competence through vanilla APIs.
- **W&L:** Whiskey may move the player's parent formations the way vanilla does (never the player's own command) and writes deduped W&L dispatches (see `2026-10-14-wl-chain-of-command-design.md`).
- **Fog:** the planner and mod ledgers are fog-honest; vanilla's internal force sums stay as they are, but the targets the planner hands vanilla come from the honest picture.

This **supersedes Slice A locked choices #3-#6** (personality stack → modifier; scripted succession → prior + competence; one phased plan per side → per-theater plan; adjust-by-default/replan-on-invalidation → daily re-scoring with hysteresis). #1 and #2 unchanged. AGENTS.md, `docs/wl-dispatch-objective-bridge.md` and `docs/wl-player-order-doctrine.md` are updated in the same slice.

**Investigation (2026-10-15, planning phase).** Every vanilla claim below was re-read against the decompile (build 14703111) before the implementation plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-15-campaign-planner.md`) was written. The approved decisions stand. Where the code contradicted the earlier text, the text was corrected; the corrections that change how a decision is carried out are listed in [Corrections to the approved design](#corrections-to-the-approved-design-2026-10-15). The open points (C1, C11, C15, and the BUG-WL-004 intent, C19) were decided on 2026-10-15 and are recorded in the table.

## Vanilla and W&L model

All numbers are lines in `/tmp/gt_src/asm/Assembly-CSharp.decompiled.cs` (build 14703111). Config values come from the installed `<GTCW>/Config/*.txt|dat`.

### Campaign AI cadence (`AICampaign.Update` 11159, `UpdateUnitAI` 11304-11631)

- `Update` returns on `debug_turnoffcampaignai` (11163), runs `InitializeAI` until `initialized` (11167), then `UpdateUnitAI` `max(1, floor(gamespeed^0.5))` times (11195-11199: 1 pass/frame at 1x, 4 at 20x), then `UpdateCompanyFoundationList` and `CBuilding.WorkDownConstructionWishes` (11200-11201). There is no pause or battle gate; pause is implicit through campaign-date deltas (`CheckOffensiveMovements` returns on `timediff <= 0`, 14200; defensive ops 13569-13574). Whiskey's #26 governor replaces the loop at ≥ 20x.
- **Per pass, in order:** (1) one `CheckOffensiveMovements` for `ownunits[currentoffensiveunitrun]` of faction `currentfactionoffensiverun` once `importancevaluesruns > 0` (11311-11338); the round-robin advances faction only when that faction's unit list wraps and `currentfactionupdatecycles == 2` (set by job 36, 11608-11611); (2) `AdjustSpeedOfDefensiveOperations` and `AdjustOffensiveOperationsSpeed` for every faction (11339-11352); `CheckSelectionSingleBrigadePerk` (11353); (3) lazy `aifaction` build (11354-11365); (4) the W&L pause (11368, below); (5) one step of the per-faction job machine; `UpdateImportanceValues` runs every pass from job 2 on (11394) and publishes only when its IIP/building/town sweeps all wrap (14982-14995).
- **Job machine** (one job per pass for `currentfaction`; the whole cycle for one faction, then the next): 0 `SeparateUnits` + `UpdateHeatmaps`; 1 `UpdateAreaPoints`; 3 `UpdatePorts`; 4 `CalculateMostValueableAIZones` + `UpdateSupplyCapacity`, **one AIArea per pass** (`zonearearun`, 11408-11428); 5 `RelieveUnitsInOperations` (reached only while `airuns == 0` or `importancevaluesruns == 0`, i.e. startup); 6 `UpdateMicroMovementInOffensive`; 7 `CheckFleetMovements`; 8+9 `CalculateZoneRecruitingProbs` + `ZoneRecruiting` (same pass); 10 `UpdatePortBlockRatios`; 11 `RemoveConstructedVessels`; 12 `CheckShipConstruction`; 13 `CheckStanceSetting`; 14 `CheckPerkSelection`; 15 `CheckForDefensiveOperations`; 16 `CheckForEndOfDefensiveOperations` (17 does not exist); 18 `AssignUnitToDefendCapital`; 19 `CheckSupplyDepotConstruction`; 20 `CheckSiegeAssaults`; 21 `CheckFortConstruction` (one own unit per pass until it returns true); 22 `CheckForSeaInvasion` (only with `debug_seainvasionsactive`); 23 `LinkToFightingUnits`; 24 `CheckAICommanderReplacements`; 25 `UpdateCampaignTheaters`; 26 `CheckCombinationOfUnits`; 27 `CheckTransferOfUnits`; 28 `CheckAssignmentOfPoolShips`; 29 `CheckOccupationOfLostCities`; 30 `CheckCreationOfRaidForces`; 31 `CheckCombinationOfBrigades`; 32 `CheckArmyGroupManagement`; 33 `PickCampaignObjective`; 34 `CheckFinalPositions`; 35 `RollUpEnemyObjectivesInZone` (a mover); 36 next faction, `airuns++` on wrap, skipping the W&L CIC player's alliance (11601-11628). A faction cycle is about `32 + aiarea.Count + ownunits.Count` passes; divide by passes per frame.
- **Factions.** `aifaction` holds one `AIFaction` per alliance with `(k != playeralliance) | ai_vs_ai` (11357-11364), so alliances 2-4 (Europe) are factions and, under W&L's forced `ai_vs_ai`, so is the player's alliance. `AIArea` per-faction arrays are sized by alliance count (10679-10692) but indexed by **faction index**; map alliance → faction with `AIFaction.GetAIFactionIDFromAllianceID` (10413; `AIFaction` and `aifaction` are private, 10299/11097). Many jobs skip `allianceid >= 2` (12307, 12321, 12451, 13084, 17128, 17242, 17336, 17572, 17771); `recruitingzonesupdated` is set only in 13145, so **Europe never launches an offensive**.
- `ownunits` holds campaign-visible own groups only (visibility state ≠ 0, no fleets, no garrisons; `SeparateUnits` 10458-10515); every garrison of any alliance lands in `owngarrisons` (10510-10513).

### Offensive lever (jobs 4, 6, round-robin)

- `AIArea.CalculateMostValueableAIZones(f)` (10964-11009) stores, **per origin area**, `mostvalueableaiareaclose[f]` = argmax over other areas of `importancevalues[f] × k + points[f] + distancepoints[f]` (`k = zoneimportancefactornoenemy` when close enemy < own close strength, else 1; 10980-10982), gated by `GetClosestAreaDistance <= aimaximumdistancetosearchforunitrelocations × rangefactoroffensivetargets`, `ownpoints <= supplycapacity`, and (points ≥ 0 or higher distance points or higher importance than the origin) (10984). A second field, `mostvaluealbeaiareaclosedistanceonly[f]`, is the highest-distancepoints friendly area with no close enemy (10989-11006). The **only reader** is `CheckOffensiveMovements` (14206, 14219-14227). Job 4 runs before job 8, so it scores with the previous cycle's close-strength fields.
- `CheckOffensiveMovements` (14166-14459): returns early on no units, `timediff <= 0`, inactive unit, `!recruitingzonesupdated`, or a unit already in the offensive/defensive/depot lists (14200), or no target (14206). Target area = the distance-only area when the origin has no close enemy, else `mostvalueableaiareaclose` (14215-14228). Enemy strength = target close strength × **own-zone** troop morale factor × `aiimportanceenemyunits` × weapon factor ÷ the national-morale ratio (14229-14230; own morale, a vanilla quirk). It gathers the triggering unit's group (`UnitBelongsToGroup`, 14232-14243), then any own unit within relocation range of the unit or the target, the W&L player's campaign group first (14252-14272), until own points ≥ enemy × `aidominanceforoffensiveoperations` × `1 + (1 − initiative) × commanderinfluenceinitiativeforoperations`, with initiative from `GameVars.commander[GetCommanderInChiefOfUnits(list)].GetCommanderInitiative()` (highest rank×100+exp in the list, 89046; 14249-14250 and 14280-14281), then trims surplus (14283-14298). An aggression roll applies when the objective is in enemy territory (14336-14353): `aiagressiveness[chapter] × timediff × 10 × aiagressivenessoffensiveoperationfaction[alliance] × usedcampaignagressiveness × winter + clamp01(idle³)`, multiplied by `dlcwl_careerincreasedaggressiveness` (2.0, every faction in a career; again if the player's group is in the list; 14341-14347). Each unit moves to `GetClosestObjectivePosOfArea(pos, target, _aifaction: -1)` (no owner filter: towns weighted, capital preferred, else IIPs/buildings, else `averageposition`; 14587-14632) through `MoveUnitTo(…, movearoundenemyunits: true)` and joins `unitsinoffensiveoperations` on `true` (14383-14385). W&L player-owned units get order 5 or 16 instead (14451, 14455) and are not listed. **At most one offensive per faction per job cycle**: 14458 clears `recruitingzonesupdated` whether or not a unit moved; only job 8 re-arms it (13145).
- `UpdateMicroMovementInOffensive` (13968-14061, job 6) moves idle listed units (`GetLastTransmittedPath() < 0`, 14004) to the closest enemy objective of their current area while morale, readiness and list membership allow (14023-14036; its supply check is dead, 14009-14018); W&L player units get order 6 (14052). A unit leaves the list when `MoveUnitTo` fails (14038), on low morale / no enemy objective in its area / capital or depot list (14057), when capital defense (11815) or a defensive op (13716, 13773) takes it, or at startup (job 5, 14074).
- `IsUnitAvailableForOffensiveOperations(int, Regiment, Vector3, bool, bool)` (14080-14158, public static) is the shared availability gate: readiness ≥ 2, not raid, strength/morale minima, not in offensive/defensive lists (when checked), not capital defense, not depot builder, not fort builder, not sea invasion, a fighting force, inside its operations theater box, weather line clear, not taking a town.
- `followedcampaignobjective` (`PickCampaignObjective` 17769-17789, alliances 0-1 only) keeps its value until `IDAccomplished` resets it, and is re-picked at random from `GetAvailableObjectives` only when < 0. Readers: the importance bonus `aitownweightfactorobjective` on objective IIPs (14922) and towns (14961), the recruiting heatmap's enemy weight near the objective town (12337), save/load (16549, 16697). Nothing else.

### Defensive operations, capital defense, transfers, theaters

- `CheckForDefensiveOperations` (13505-13780, job 15): per faction per cycle at most one op. Enemies sorted by min(distance to their closest enemy, distance to our capital / 3); an enemy qualifies when its **destination** is in our state and no op exists within 50. Candidates must pass `IsUnitAvailableForDefensiveOperation` (13460, private static), supply in zone, weather, distance, theater box; units accumulate to `enemy × higherstrengthdefensiveoperations`, then a probability roll; dispatch is a direct `DefensiveMovingOrder` (13713) and the unit moves from the offensive to the defensive list. A second branch attacks an enemy in enemy territory with its own roll and adds to the defensive list as well (13721-13779). (Vanilla bug: 13610 tests availability against `enemyunits[j]`, the unsorted list, while iterating the sorted `list4`.)
- `CheckForEndOfDefensiveOperations` (13782-13848, job 16) removes listed units with no path / retreating / in battle, and otherwise **calls `StopRegiment`** (skipped for W&L player units, 13840) and removes the unit when no enemy unit or enemy-connected depot is within its `buglerange` of its **destination**. `DefensiveOperation.UpdateDefensiveOperation` (10146-10252) also `StopRegiment`s on collapse (10197-10207). **Never put a planner-moved unit in `unitsindefensiveoperations`.**
- `AssignUnitToDefendCapital` (11668-11869, job 18): requirement = `aiminimumstrengthformovement` + Σ over enemies within relocation range of `min(str, str×morale) / max(10, d)^0.3 × 0.5` − own str×morale closer than the nearest enemy (11712-11741; the garrison loop 11742-11750 is dead). It prunes weak, low-morale, retreating and `IsMovedByPlayer` members (11751-11759) and **releases the farthest member when the requirement is met** (11760-11781); it adds the single closest qualifying unit per call (11791) and strips it from every other list (11810-11817); members are moved behind the capital toward the nearest enemy (11819-11867; W&L player units get order 8, and vanilla still sets their `running`/`doublequick`, 11865-11866). Members are excluded from offensives (14118), down-weighted in the recruiting heatmap (12328) and preferred fort builders (16362).
- `CheckTransferOfUnits` (17232-17332, job 27, alliances 0-1): uses `positionsurplus`/`positiondeficit` that `ZoneRecruiting` builds from the 20×20 own/enemy heatmaps over own-town cells (12467-12520); capped by `maximumunittransferstrength × troopsfielded` minus strength in transit; moves **one brigade per call** by `BattleUnits.MoveUnit` (93091, reparenting; `SetTransfer` 93147 sets an abstract `transfertime = distance / speedforunittransfers`, 139051) — not a rail move. **In a W&L career it does nothing for any faction:** 17328 calls `MoveUnit` without `skipdlccheck`, and `MoveUnit` returns unless both units are player-owned (93099), while 17327 has already appended the unit to `unitstobetransfered` (BUG-WL-003). `CheckCombinationOfUnits` passes `skipdlccheck: true` (17158) and works.
- `Regiment.theaterposition` (public, saved; 110494) — job 25 (`UpdateCampaignTheaters` 17034-17110) sets it to the midpoint between the unit and its closest enemy when unset and that enemy is within `commanderrange × 5`, and **clears it when the unit is outside the box** (17070-17084). `IsWithinOperationsTheater` (17090-17110) is an axis-aligned box of half-extent `GamePrefs.theatersize` (true when unset or `debug_usecampaigntheaters` is false). It **confines** vanilla's offensive targets (14138), defensive candidates (13610), capital candidates (11791), the counter-attack (13744), combination (17134, 17215) and army groups (17729-17751); it does not attract a unit anywhere. There is no theater id on `AIArea`.
- `ArmyGroup` (75022-75065) has no objective field; `GetGroupBonus` (75783) is a command bonus only. `AppointCommander(ArmyGroup, int, bool usefeudmechanism = false, bool resetcoordinationtime = true, bool ignoreprestige = false)` (75559). Job 32 (`CheckArmyGroupManagement` 17705-17767) adds top units (> 1000 strength, with a theater) to an in-range group or creates one; it can adopt the W&L player's top unit (17734, `ArmyGroup.AddUnit` 75345 has no guard), and it returns for **every** faction when the W&L player is CIC (17723, BUG-WL-001).
- Other movers: `LinkToFightingUnits` (16940, job 23) links non-fighting units to the closest fighting force (followers only); `CheckCombinationOfUnits` (17112, job 26) reparents a corps under an army HQ (Grand Army project 90); `CheckFinalPositions` (17898, job 34) moves idle units off water; `RollUpEnemyObjectivesInZone` (13883, job 35) moves one idle offensive-stance unit to the closest enemy objective in its own area; `CheckStanceSetting` (job 13, 11893-11930) sets `campaignstance` from area strength ratio × `(1 + (initiative − 0.5) × 0.6)` (11913-11914).

### Movement (`MoveUnitTo` 14479-14540)

`MoveUnitTo` returns false for a null unit, a neutral destination state or a unit in battle, then applies a stacking offset. `movearoundenemyunits: false` → `SetWaypoint` at once (14514). `movearoundenemyunits: true` → **returns false when an order is already queued**, else enqueues a `DefensiveMovingOrder` and returns true: *queued*, not moved. The queue is drained by `BattleUnits.Update` (79295 → 9965-10052), one order per ~2 frames, globally serialized and blocked while `Retreat.retreatorder` is non-empty, so a burst of Whiskey moves waits behind vanilla. There is no W&L guard inside (callers check `dlcw_isundercommander`). Rails are implicit: AI units are created with `userails = true, usesea = false, userivers = true` (112862-112867) and `SetWaypoint` allows the rail area when transport capacity is under `greyoutmeansoftransportcap` (91603-91635). `CheckIfFinalWaypointIsPassable` (25608-25671) walks the target back toward the start until `IsTerrainPositionRestricted` (25755-25806: terrain 1, wrong water type, state out of range, neutral state) is false, else returns the start (no move).

### Supply and reachability

- `AIArea.UpdateSupplyCapacity` (10772-10802) = Σ non-trade-node visible IIP `GetTransportCapacity() × 2` + per depot the mean of `stock / 10 × Condition`; it ignores the owner of both. `SupplyInZoneAvailable(pos, f, from)` (10941-10962) is true in the same zone, with no zone, or when `ownpoints[f] < supplycapacity` (`ownpoints` = Σ |`GetUnitPoints`| of own units by **destination**, 10571-10580): a crude stacking cap.
- **Per-unit supply** is `Regiment.UpdateIIPSupplyLink` (114074-114500, slow cadence 112258): candidates are IIPs and buildings within the unit's own campaign `commanderrange` (`UpdateIIPsInRange` 113997-114010; range = `commandrange[unittyp] × (1 + readiness and initiative terms)`, 125830-125838). The best own-owned IIP by `transportcap / dist / avgPrice` (114272-114295) or an own/allied depot in range (114296-114306) supplies it; with neither, or while `running|doublequick` (114258-114266), `supplystate = 0`. Supply speed interpolates over distance 0..2000 and the enemy-soil factor (114317-114345). The AI's per-group gate is `GetGroupSupplyState` (14063-14066) = `max(avg(groupsupplystate[0..3]) × 0.5, supplystate × 0.5)`: both inputs are 0..1, so the value **never exceeds 0.5**. Vanilla's 0.25 thresholds (job-19 depot trigger 14715, fort builders) therefore mean 50 % real supply, and its 0.75 `minimumsupplyoffensivetargets` gates (raid `SearchObjective` 9135, the micro-move gate 14019) can never pass: vanilla raid forces probably never pick a target (being resolved by `fix/campaign-logistics-bugs`). **The AI never checks supply before or during an offensive:** `IsUnitAvailableForOffensiveOperations` (14080-14158) has no supply term, job 6's supply gate is dead (`flag` is always true, 14009-14019), job 6 re-targets listed units on morale/readiness only (14023-14030), and `RelieveUnitsInOperations` (14068-14078) removes only retreating or low-morale units from the operation lists. Zone supply capacity freezes after the second AI run: `AIArea.iips/cbuildings/towns` are only filled while `airuns <= 1` (14927, 14946, 14965), so later depots never count (being fixed by `fix/campaign-logistics-bugs`). Job 19 also admits an unguarded enemy depot as a supply source (14740) and skips a unit with any own-started depot in range, built or not (14683-14715); same fix slice. **There is no route or rail-connectivity model**: rail only multiplies an IIP's transport capacity when a connected railroad is complete (104652-104668).
- Campaign NavMesh: `AddPath` sets global area costs (130395-130466; sea 500000 unless `usesea`, rivers by `userivers`, rails 1 when built) and calls `CalculatePath(start, target, -1)` (130473). "Closed" is a cost, not impassability, and the costs are global state left by the last unit; a planner probe would have to set them itself and inspect corners. The cheap target gate is `IsTerrainPositionRestricted` / `CheckIfFinalWaypointIsPassable`.

### Construction

- `CBuilding.AddConstructionWish(id, pos, iip, alliance, overridealreadyconstructing)` (97479-97536): returns the IIP's `currentlyunderconstruction` building when one exists (pending economy wishes set it, 15031/15336), null for a null IIP, otherwise `Place(…, newlycreated: true)` at once — **cost is charged at wish time** (96211-96257) — and queues an `AIPlacement`. `WorkDownConstructionWishes` (97538-97591, every `Update`) spirals around the wish for a `CheckIfTerrainOK` site and removes the building without refund when none is found within `buildingsitemaxradius`. Building ids (95083-95121): supply depot 22, fort 7, telegraph 23, entrenchments 26, siege fortification 27. Railroads are not buildings.
- **Builders must stay.** For fort, telegraph and depot types, a friendly unit of the building alliance must be within its own `buglerange` and not retreating or progress reverses, and the site is removed after `timetoremovemilitarybuildings` (`IncreaseCondition` 96626-96655).
- **AI depots** (`CheckSupplyDepotConstruction` 14659-14779, job 19): per own unit with supply below `supplystatedepotconstruction`, idle, no own depot under construction within `commanderrange`: move to an own town or depot (Condition ≥ 0.75) within range but outside bugle range (14762-14768), else **wish a depot at the unit's own position** when it has `closestiipforsupply` and no depot within range (14770-14775). One depot wish per faction pass. `unitsconstructingsupplydepots` is rebuilt (Clear + reassign) every job 19 (14683-14712, 14778).
- **AI forts** (`CheckFortConstruction` 16347-16405, job 21): one fort order per alliance at a time (16368); sites only from `fortconstructionsites` (scenario forts not built at start, 81931-81943), spacing `maxdistancetootherfort` (×0.6 near the capital), within `rangefortconstructionsite`, near the own frontline; `FortConstructionOrder` moves the builder and wishes fort level 1 on arrival (9636-9675, 9857-9865). #27 already filters the site list.
- **AI railroads** (`UpdateRailroadConstruction` 16052-16074, from the alliance tick 81741): for alliances 0-1 (the player's only under `ai_vs_ai`), **each predefined line is rolled independently** at `(probrailroadconstructionperyear + per-industry-policy bonus) × timediff` and `StartConstruction(alliance)` (77818-77845: needs `AllianceOwnsAllIIPs` and `IsPermitted`, costs nothing). Progress is monthly (77859-77893). Choosing *which* line starts has no Postfix lever (a filtered list lowers the rate); see the railroad user decision.

### Campaign fog

- Both grids run on the campaign map: `fow[0].InitFOW(true, calledfromcampaign: true)`, `fow[1].InitFOW(false, true)` (29916-29920); `FogOfWar.Update` (100675-100714) every frame (no blur on `fows[1]`). `CreateFOWObjects` (116505-116517): player-alliance units `fow = fows[0]`, every other alliance `fow = fows[1]`. Viewers (`AssignUnitToFOW` 116520-116534) are units with campaign visibility state ≠ 0 (82934-82974: garrisons, corps, top-unit divisions, armies, fleets), garrison dummies (82108) and detachments (90478); inactive or routed viewers are skipped (101325). Radius = `maximumlineofsight[unittyp]` (campaign groups: division, corps and army all 50, `unitprefs.txt` 645/689/733) × a perk-8 factor (101298-101301; battle units only once BUG-AI-003 is fixed, see below) × (1 − climate layer 105 × `influenceofrainsnowonvisibilitycampaign`) (101296-101302) × lie-down and W&L incident 63 terms (101303-101307); the terrain raycast applies (101098); crop/forest reductions do not (101102). **Concealment is painted on campaign** (101116-101128, campaign values from `campaignprefs.txt`).
- `UnitIsHidden` (101179-101229) on campaign is called from `CheckRegimentVisibility` every frame (111994 → 127225/127229) with `(GetPosition(), commander.GetCommanderCunning(), strength / unittypesize[unittyp], liedown, coverobject, covervalue, unittyp, this)`. Cover search is battle-only (116274), so the cover term is 0. Hidden = outside vision, or cell concealment − cunning term − size term − `spottingfactor[unittyp]` − perks 3/8 < 0. W&L incidents 60/61/64 make everything visible (101183-101186). The `relativestrength` argument uses the top unit's own `strength` field; the campaign aggregation sums only unittyp ≤ 13 into `groupstrength` (120284-120288), so for HQs it is probably small (inferred from the code, not measured; vanilla's own behaviour either way).
- `iswithinfog` is "hidden from the player alliance": forced false for player-alliance units on campaign (127211-127214), `fows[0].UnitIsHidden(...)` for every other unit (127223-127225). `hqinthesaddle` is battle-only (101312, 127211), so the campaign `fows[0]` is never blanked.
- **There is no AI-side campaign fog in vanilla.** `SeparateUnits` fills `enemyunits` from every at-war campaign group at true position (10458-10515); `UpdateAreaPoints` even uses the enemy's *destination* (`GetLastTransmittedPathPos(ignoreorderdelay: true)`, 10535, 10582-10594). `SpottedEnemy` (142644-142941) is player-only; `groupintelligencegathering` (120417-120620) only feeds readiness; there is no newspaper, telegraph or cavalry-scout detection path. `GetIntelligenceOnPos` returns 1 unless the inspector-only `UseIntelligenceMap` is set (100594).
- **Perk-8 bug (BUG-AI-003, fixed by `fix/campaign-perk-ids`):** vanilla `UpdateViewRange` multiplies the radius by `perk_expertscoutsvisibilityrangefactor` when `HasPerk(8)` (101298-101301) with no campaign guard, and `HasPerk` (132765) does not remap ids; on the campaign map perk 8 is Ambulance Corps (`PerkTooltipsArmyCampaign.txt`:100) and Expert Scouts is 16. The fix (flag `Fix Perk Id Mismatches`, default on) makes the perk table follow the unit type (≤ 13 battle, 17 fleet, 14-16 and 18 campaign), so the painted radius becomes `maximumlineofsight[unittyp] × (unittyp ≤ 13 && HasPerk(8) ? perk_expertscoutsvisibilityrangefactor[GetPerkLevel(8)] : 1) × weather` (campaign weather `1 − climate(105) × influenceofrainsnowonvisibilitycampaign`, battle `lineofsightreduction`), then × `fow_lineofsightreduction_layndown` when lying down and × the W&L incident-63 multiplier for the player's command. Campaign viewers are top units, so no campaign perk (Expert Scouts 16 included) changes campaign sight. Whiskey's `SightRadiusFormula` computes that radius, takes the flag as an input and keeps the old factor only when the flag is off, so the viewer check always matches what paints `fows[1]`.
- **Every non-player alliance shares `fows[1]`** (116513-116516). European units, including a European power at war with the AI side (Spain allied with the Union vs the CSA, 15740-15745), paint the non-player AI's grid.
- `GameVars.usefow` (default true, 64636; prefs 199481, options 193645, save 216608/69253) off → `InitFOW` deactivates (100755-100759), every `iswithinfog` is false, and **`UnitIsHidden` dereferences the unallocated `concealment` grid at 101188 (NullReferenceException)**: gate on `usefow` before calling it.
- Campaign group fields: position `GetPosition()` (140398-140409); strength `groupstrengthdirect` (combat regiments whose nearest campaign ancestor is the group, 120391-120407, 83022-83051; no double count, unlike `groupstrength` 120288); enumeration `BattleUnits.campaignunitlist` (79974-79994). `UnitIsHidden` is O(1) per call (two grid lookups, a perk scan, a commander getter).

### Reconnaissance, intelligence and march (vanilla AI)

- **The campaign AI does no scouting.** Campaign groups have three cavalry orders, 1 Scouting, 2 Raiding, 3 Guarding (`cavalryorders` 110590; status text `unitstatustextcamp` 65018-65022, shown by `UpdateUnitStatusCampaign` 119914-119935; icons 121092-121100; setter `SetCavalryOrders` 122074-122082). ## Architecture

```
daily review (per alliance, alliances 0/1; skipped when the W&L player is CIC; paused on W&L hierarchy −1 like vanilla's job machine 11368)
  CampaignSnapshotRuntime ──► CampaignSnapshot (immutable)
  EnemyArmyModel.Update(snapshot detections) ──► believed enemy armies (persisted)
  CommanderProfiles (vanilla commanders → personality modifiers)
  TheaterPlanner.Plan(snapshot, beliefs, priors, profiles, state, rng) ──► TheaterPlan
  LogisticsGate.Evaluate(plan, snapshot) ──► reachable targets + depot tasks
  ForceAllocator.Allocate(plan, snapshot, reliability) ──► Allocation
  CommanderAppointmentPlanner (competence + historical succession prior) ──► appointment intents
  Executors (idempotent, rate-limited, W&L-guarded) ──► vanilla hooks
```

### Components

1. **`CampaignSnapshotRuntime`** (runtime) → `CampaignSnapshot` (pure data): formations (position `GetPosition()`, last waypoint, `groupstrengthdirect`, morale, raw supply (`max(avg(groupsupplystate[0..3]), supplystate)`, 0..1 — vanilla's `GetGroupSupplyState` without its halving, C20), readiness step, battle/retreat flags, `theaterposition`, player ownership `dlcw_isundercommander`, map-group flag, `IsMovedByPlayer`, membership of every vanilla busy list, commander id, `commanderrange`, `buglerange`), areas (importance, points, supply capacity, own/enemy close strengths, average position, edges), objectives (live positions from `CampaignObjective.objectives` only — the hand-coded `ObjectiveCatalog` coordinates are removed), capitals, depots/IIPs (owner, transport capacity, condition), railroads (line, progress, startable), `CampaignMapLedger` assets, battle history, the player's echelon (`GetPlayerCommandHierarchy`) and map-group status (8271), raw enemy groups (read **only** by the detection step). One build per review; replaces the scattered per-runtime reflection.
2. **`EnemyArmyModel`** (pure, persisted): an enemy campaign group is *detected* on a review day when (a) `GameVars.usefow` is off (omniscient, tagged `mode=NoFog` in telemetry), or (b) for an AI side that is not the player alliance, `!ownUnit.fow.UnitIsHidden(enemy.GetPosition(), <127225 arguments>)` on `fows[1]` **and** an own or allied viewer is within its campaign sight radius (101296-101302) of the enemy, or (c) for the W&L player-alliance AI side, `!enemy.iswithinfog`, or (d) contact: the enemy is in battle with the side, or within an own unit's `buglerange`. It stores last-seen position, strength (`groupstrengthdirect`), unit type and day; uncertainty radius grows with age; entries expire. Enemy-held objective towns imply garrisons. Enemy-vs-enemy cases with no grid (a European enemy of a non-player AI side) use (d) only. It never reads enemy positions except through this filter; `ContactEvidenceLedger` (battle-history contact classes) is adopted as an input in the same step.
3. **`CommanderProfiles`** (pure + runtime reader): personality modifiers derived from the live vanilla commander record of the alliance CIC (`GameVars.alliance[a].commanderinchief`) and each army-group/army/corps commander: `GetCommanderInitiative/Leadership/Administration/Cunning()` (61134-61251, experience multiplier included), `exp`, `fame`, `rank`, `veteran/westpoint/political`. Outputs per theater: required-odds shift (initiative, as vanilla's own 14250 factor), hold-vs-attack bias (initiative vs leadership), risk tolerance (cunning), supply-reach slack (administration). Era and faction remain priors.
4. **`TheaterPlanner`** (pure): sub-theater sectors built from AI areas (not the current 6 whole-theater buckets; the sector model is the planner's own, see C15). Per objective/sector live score = value (importance, objective role, capital/rail/port role) + believed force balance near it + supply reach (from `LogisticsGate`) − march distance for the candidate force − exposure + threat to own capital (believed enemy strength over distance, streak counter) + `λ·prior` (historical catalog as time-decayed weight: 1 inside its window, fading over months; `Priority` sets magnitude; λ tuned so the prior breaks near-ties but cannot overturn a live margin above a fixed bound — harness-tested). Personality modifiers shift thresholds. **Hysteresis:** switch main effort only if `best − current > θ` for M days or a trigger fires (decisive battle, capital threat, objective taken/lost). **Bounded randomness:** softmax within ε of the best with a deterministic seed (campaign id ⊕ alliance ⊕ decision serial) persisted in the sidecar. Output `TheaterPlan`: main-effort theater + objective + required ratio; per-theater posture (MainEffort / Hold / EconomyOfForce / Screen / Recover); capital-defense floor; depot tasks; commander-need hints; per-theater axis (for stage H).
4b. **Reconnaissance** (`ReconPlanner` + `ReconMissionLedger`, pure; executor runtime) — added 2026-10-15 because beliefs only decay without it. Screens (cavalry-majority campaign groups, with vanilla's scouting order through `ChangeCampaignOrders(reg, 121)`) go toward the oldest / most uncertain believed armies and along approaches to the own capital and the main-effort axis that no own viewer covers, held at a standoff of 0.8 × their sight radius from the nearest belief and inside supply reach, recalled on contact or low supply; ≤ 2 screens per theater, and the main effort always keeps its own screen. Pickets hold unwatched major towns on threatened axes without taking a sector's final reserve; one advance guard holds a sight radius ahead of a main effort with spare strength. Belief-driven probes replace the omniscient `OperationalProbe*` layer: a small group goes to the standoff short of a main-effort objective whose beliefs are absent or stale, and `ContactEvidenceLedger` (on beliefs) decides escalate or withdraw. Missions use whole existing campaign groups (vanilla has no AI detach API; `DetachUnitToIndependentCorps` 178375 is UI-only). `TheaterPosture.Screen` sectors always get a screen; the `Screen` formation directive means "on a recon mission"; the unread `RaidAllowed`, the `RaidSupport` directive and `FrontSectorLedger`'s `ArmyRole` are deleted. W&L: never the player's own command; a below-map-level player's parent group may screen (F's `MoveUnitTo` Postfix dispatches); a map-group player is order-only and never the sole screen. Adopted from `docs/strategic-recon-commitment.md` § Advance Guard And Picket Sandbox and the archived advance-guard sandbox spec (supply-base attack/patrol missions are left to vanilla raid forces — which depends on `fix/campaign-logistics-bugs` repairing the raid search gate (9135, unreachable on the halved supply metric); until it lands vanilla raids probably never pick a target).
5. **`LogisticsGate`** (pure): a target is in reach when an own-owned IIP (not invisible) or an own/allied depot (Condition ≥ 0.75, built) lies within the moving formation's campaign `commanderrange` of it (vanilla's own supply rule, 114272-114306), weighted by transport capacity and the 0..2000 distance curve; the target zone's `supplycapacity` vs `ownpoints` is a stacking check only. It refuses advance targets beyond reach and emits depot tasks (site, builder need) along the axis instead ("don't outrun supply"). Administration from `CommanderProfiles` widens the slack.
6. **`ForceAllocator`** (pure): maps each AI formation to (theater, role ∈ {MainEffort, Support, Hold, Reserve, CapitalGuard, DepotBuilder, Recover, Recon}, target). Hold theaters get `max(holdRatio × believed enemy, minimum garrison)`; the remainder goes to the main effort. Corps-level redeployment uses `MoveUnitTo` with rails (vanilla's own `userails`/capacity rule). Replacement flow uses vanilla's deficit/surplus brigade transfer (with the C1 career fix), and the allocation lists **shortfalls** (formations below their role's need) and **surpluses**: transfers move a brigade from a surplus to a shortfall formation, and new recruits are routed to the nearest shortfall formation by an argument edit on `GrabExistingGroup` inside `ZoneRecruiting` only (C23). A **Recover** formation (raw supply below 0.4 or low morale) is withheld from vanilla's new operations and removed from its operation lists, as `RelieveUnitsInOperations` does, then moved toward supply (C21). **Player handling** (C4): the player's own command is never allocated a move; a below-map-level player's parent campaign group is a movable AI asset (F's `MoveUnitTo` Postfix emits the dispatch); a map-group player's formation is an *unreliable executor* (reliability from F's `PlayerComplianceLedger`): order-only, never the sole holder of a must-hold role (capital guard, sole main effort), discounted in allocation, reassigned around after N days of non-compliance. CIC player → planner stands down for that side.
7. **`CommanderAppointmentPlanner`** (pure + vanilla executor): scores available commanders (vanilla `CanBePickedForAIReplacement` pool, `!refreshcommand`, `CheckSuitableRank`) on `GetAverageAttributeValue` and the live getters against theater importance (vanilla's job 24 already swaps best-for-worst by rank; the planner adds *where*: the best available commanders go to the main effort); historical succession events (existing scheduler conditions) add a prior bonus so the historical swap happens when its conditions hold. Executes in the daily review via `ArmyGroup.AppointCommander` (army groups, reseating the stranded commander first, as today) or the vanilla recipe `ReplaceCommanderOfUnit(appointee.currentcommand)` → `AssignCommando` → `DoCommanderPromotion` (corps/army HQs), and protects its picks for a window through the #6 guard (C16). Never touches the player, the player's command or commanders serving under the player (`IsServingUnderPlayer` 60695). `GeneralInChief` events stay deferred (C18).
8. **Executors** (runtime; one concern each, `dlcw`/`IsMovedByPlayer`-guarded, idempotent, ≤ K writes/day):
   - **Objective:** the daily review writes `followedcampaignobjective` (C6); no patch.
   - `ImportanceValuesPatch` (rewritten): per source-area pointers (both fields, C7) to that area's theater target, only for MainEffort/Support theaters and within vanilla's own distance/supply gates.
   - `theaterposition` writer (replaces #15's hardcoded anchors), set only for a formation inside its theater box (C8).
   - Availability filters: Postfixes on `IsUnitAvailableForOffensiveOperations` (14080) and `IsUnitAvailableForDefensiveOperation` (13460) withhold Reserve, CapitalGuard, DepotBuilder and in-transit formations.
   - Main-effort mover: folds `OperationalProbeRuntime` + `CoordinatedOperationRuntime` commit/rollback and #38; uses `MoveUnitTo` + the offensive list (never the defensive list). `MoveUnitTo(…, movearoundenemyunits:true)` returning true means *queued* (14519-14523) and the queue is global and serialized (9965-10052); the mover confirms the move on the next review (path or position change) and retries/reassigns on failure. Targets pass vanilla's `CheckIfFinalWaypointIsPassable` semantics (25608) before dispatch.
   - Construction steering: supply depots (builder tasks, C10), forts (preferred sites on planned axes and threatened sectors through #27's site filter) and railroads (C11) along planned main-effort axes and threatened sectors; each with its own flag, default on (C12).
   - Capital floor: rewritten #4 Prefix pre-pledging the planner's chosen units into `groupstodefendcapital` up to vanilla's need; the excess as `CapitalGuard` (C9).
   - Replacement flow: `CheckTransferOfUnits` steering (rewritten #3) + the career fix (C1) + recruit routing (C23).
   - Recover relief: withheld by the availability Postfixes and removed from `unitsinoffensiveoperations`/`unitsindefensiveoperations` (C21).
   - W&L dispatch emitter: from the chain-of-command spec (stage F).

9. **Garrison manning beyond the capital** (stage G8, after the main slices): own objective towns, rail hubs, ports, depots and forts in held or threatened sectors get a small field formation posted when the believed threat (the capital-need formula on beliefs) exceeds the existing garrison (a fort's garrison dummy plus formations already there); never the capital (the capital floor owns it), never at the main effort's expense below its required ratio, released after a quiet fortnight; vanilla's reactive recapture stays (C24).

### Cadence & performance

One snapshot per daily review; scoring O(objectives × sectors + formations × sectors); alternate alliances across frames if a review exceeds ~4 ms; executor writes ≤ K/day, idempotent. Planner execution must not rely on vanilla job throughput beyond what the (fixed) governor guarantees: vanilla launches at most one offensive per faction per job cycle (14458) and serializes `MoveUnitTo` orders globally.

## Testing

Pure units harness-tested with fixtures: both alliances; live margin beats prior / prior breaks tie; hysteresis prevents flapping; seeded RNG reproducible; enemy near capital raises the floor and pulls the main effort; out-of-supply target yields a depot task not an advance; economy-of-force theaters keep hold ratio; personality shifts thresholds (high initiative attacks at worse odds than low); historical succession prior fires when conditions hold, competence otherwise; no vertical swap on the W&L player's side in a career; raw supply metric (raw 0.45 not Recover); Recover relieved from vanilla operation lists; recruits routed to shortfall formations; invasion/raid threats only from seen forces; garrison posts from beliefs, never the capital; W&L below-map-level career (parent movable, player command untouched); W&L map-group career (player discounted, never sole must-hold, reassigned after N days); CIC player → stand down; hierarchy −1 → pause; fog (unseen enemy excluded, last-seen decays, fog off = omniscient, European-painted cell without an own viewer stays unseen); recon (screens target the oldest beliefs and unwatched approaches, keep the standoff and supply reach, recall on contact, never strip the main effort's screen, belief-driven probe escalates or withdraws); faction index bounds (alliances 2-4); empty snapshot; sidecar migration from v1/v2 plans.
