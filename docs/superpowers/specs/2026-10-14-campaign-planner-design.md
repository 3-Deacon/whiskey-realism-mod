- History is a **weight**; live scoring decides.

### Campaign AI cadence (`AICampaign.Update` 11159, `UpdateUnitAI` 11304-11631)

- `Update` returns on `debug_turnoffcampaignai` (11163), runs `InitializeAI` until `initialized` (11167), then `UpdateUnitAI` `max(1, floor(gamespeed^0.5))` times per frame (11195-11199; one pass per frame at 1x, four passes per frame at 20x), then `UpdateCompanyFoundationList` and `CBuilding.WorkDownConstructionWishes` (11200-11201). There is no pause or battle gate; pause is implicit through campaign-date deltas (`CheckOffensiveMovements` returns while `timediff` ≤ 0, 14200; defensive ops 13569-13574). Whiskey's #26 governor replaces the loop at ≥ 20x.
- **Per pass, in order:** (1) one `CheckOffensiveMovements` for `ownunits[currentoffensiveunitrun]` of faction `currentfactionoffensiverun` once `importancevaluesruns > 0` (11311-11338); the round-robin advances faction only when that faction's unit list wraps and `currentfactionupdatecycles == 2` (set by job 36, 11608-11611); (2) `AdjustSpeedOfDefensiveOperations` and `AdjustOffensiveOperationsSpeed` for every faction (11339-11352); `CheckSelectionSingleBrigadePerk` (11353); (3) lazy `aifaction` build (11354-11365); (4) the W&L pause (11368, below); (5) one step of the per-faction job machine; `UpdateImportanceValues` runs every pass from job 2 on (11394) and publishes only when its IIP/building/town sweeps all wrap (14982-14995).
- **Job machine** (one job per pass for `currentfaction`; the whole cycle for one faction, then the next). Job 0 `SeparateUnits` + `UpdateHeatmaps`; 1 `UpdateAreaPoints`; 3 `UpdatePorts`; 4 `CalculateMostValueableAIZones` + `UpdateSupplyCapacity`, **one AIArea per pass** (`zonearearun`, 11408-11428); 5 `RelieveUnitsInOperations` (reached only while `airuns` or `importancevaluesruns` is still zero, i.e. at startup); 6 `UpdateMicroMovementInOffensive`; 7 `CheckFleetMovements`; 8+9 `CalculateZoneRecruitingProbs` + `ZoneRecruiting` (same pass); 10 `UpdatePortBlockRatios`; 11 `RemoveConstructedVessels`; 12 `CheckShipConstruction`; 13 `CheckStanceSetting`; 14 `CheckPerkSelection`; 15 `CheckForDefensiveOperations`; 16 `CheckForEndOfDefensiveOperations` (17 does not exist); 18 `AssignUnitToDefendCapital`; 19 `CheckSupplyDepotConstruction`; 20 `CheckSiegeAssaults`; 21 `CheckFortConstruction` (one own unit per pass until it returns true); 22 `CheckForSeaInvasion` (only with `debug_seainvasionsactive`); 23 `LinkToFightingUnits`; 24 `CheckAICommanderReplacements`; 25 `UpdateCampaignTheaters`; 26 `CheckCombinationOfUnits`; 27 `CheckTransferOfUnits`; 28 `CheckAssignmentOfPoolShips`; 29 `CheckOccupationOfLostCities`; 30 `CheckCreationOfRaidForces`; 31 `CheckCombinationOfBrigades`; 32 `CheckArmyGroupManagement`; 33 `PickCampaignObjective`; 34 `CheckFinalPositions`; 35 `RollUpEnemyObjectivesInZone` (a mover); 36 next faction, `airuns++` on wrap, skipping the W&L CIC player's alliance (11601-11628). A faction cycle is about `32 + aiarea.Count + ownunits.Count` passes (one per job, one per area in job 4, one per own unit in job 21 at most); divide by passes per frame for frames.
- **Factions.** `aifaction` holds one `AIFaction` per alliance with `(k != playeralliance) | ai_vs_ai` (11357-11364), so alliances 2-4 (Europe) are factions and, under W&L's forced `ai_vs_ai`, so is the player's alliance. `AIArea` per-faction arrays are sized by alliance count (10679-10692) but indexed by **faction index**; map alliance → faction with `AIFaction.GetAIFactionIDFromAllianceID` (10413; `AIFaction` and `aifaction` are private, 10299/11097). Many jobs skip alliance ids of 2 and above (12307, 12321, 12451, 13084, 17128, 17242, 17336, 17572, 17771); `recruitingzonesupdated` is set only in 13145, so **Europe never launches an offensive**.
- `ownunits` holds campaign-visible own groups only (visibility state ≠ 0, no fleets, no garrisons; `SeparateUnits` 10458-10515); every garrison of any alliance lands in `owngarrisons` (10510-10513).

### Offensive lever (jobs 4, 6, round-robin)

- `AIArea.CalculateMostValueableAIZones(f)` (10964-11009) stores, **per origin area**, `mostvalueableaiareaclose[f]` = argmax over other areas of `importancevalues[f] × k + points[f] + distancepoints[f]` (`k = zoneimportancefactornoenemy` when close enemy < own close strength, else 1; 10980-10982), gated by `GetClosestAreaDistance <= aimaximumdistancetosearchforunitrelocations × rangefactoroffensivetargets`, `ownpoints <= supplycapacity`, and (points ≥ 0 or higher distance points or higher importance than the origin) (10984). A second field, `mostvaluealbeaiareaclosedistanceonly[f]`, is the highest-distancepoints friendly area with no close enemy (10989-11006). The **only reader** is `CheckOffensiveMovements` (14206, 14219-14227). Job 4 runs before job 8, so it scores with the previous cycle's close-strength fields.
- `CheckOffensiveMovements` (14166-14459): returns early on no units, `timediff` ≤ 0, an inactive unit, `!recruitingzonesupdated`, or a unit already in the offensive/defensive/depot lists (14200), or no target (14206). Target area = the distance-only area when the origin has no close enemy, else `mostvalueableaiareaclose` (14215-14228). Enemy strength = target close strength × **own-zone** troop morale factor × `aiimportanceenemyunits` × weapon factor ÷ the national-morale ratio (14229-14230; own morale, a vanilla quirk). It gathers the triggering unit's group (`UnitBelongsToGroup`, 14232-14243), then any own unit within relocation range of the unit or the target, the W&L player's campaign group first (14252-14272), until own points ≥ enemy × `aidominanceforoffensiveoperations` × `1 + (1 − initiative) × commanderinfluenceinitiativeforoperations`, with initiative from `GameVars.commander[GetCommanderInChiefOfUnits(list)].GetCommanderInitiative()` (highest rank×100+exp in the list, 89046; 14249-14250 and 14280-14281), then trims surplus (14283-14298). An aggression roll applies when the objective is in enemy territory (14336-14353): `aiagressiveness[chapter] × timediff × 10 × aiagressivenessoffensiveoperationfaction[alliance] × usedcampaignagressiveness × winter + clamp01(idle³)`, multiplied by `dlcwl_careerincreasedaggressiveness` (2.0, every faction in a career; again if the player's group is in the list; 14341-14347). Each unit moves to `GetClosestObjectivePosOfArea(pos, target, _aifaction: -1)` (no owner filter: towns weighted, capital preferred, else IIPs/buildings, else `averageposition`; 14587-14632) through `MoveUnitTo(…, movearoundenemyunits: true)` and joins `unitsinoffensiveoperations` on `true` (14383-14385). W&L player-owned units get order 5 or 16 instead (14451, 14455) and are not listed. **At most one offensive per faction per job cycle** — 14458 clears `recruitingzonesupdated` whether or not a unit moved; only job 8 re-arms it (13145).
- `UpdateMicroMovementInOffensive` (13968-14061, job 6) moves idle listed units (`GetLastTransmittedPath() < 0`, 14004) to the closest enemy objective of their current area while morale, readiness and list membership allow (14023-14036; its supply check is dead, 14009-14018); W&L player units get order 6 (14052). A unit leaves the list when `MoveUnitTo` fails (14038), on low morale / no enemy objective in its area / capital or depot list (14057), when capital defense (11815) or a defensive op (13716, 13773) takes it, or at startup (job 5, 14074).
- `IsUnitAvailableForOffensiveOperations(int, Regiment, Vector3, bool, bool)` (14080-14158, public static) is the shared availability gate: readiness ≥ 2, not raid, strength/morale minima, not in offensive/defensive lists (when checked), not capital defense, not depot builder, not fort builder, not sea invasion, a fighting force, inside its operations theater box, weather line clear, not taking a town.
- `followedcampaignobjective` (`PickCampaignObjective` 17769-17789, alliances 0-1 only) keeps its value until `IDAccomplished` resets it, and is re-picked at random from `GetAvailableObjectives` only when < 0. Readers: the importance bonus `aitownweightfactorobjective` on objective IIPs (14922) and towns (14961), the recruiting heatmap's enemy weight near the objective town (12337), save/load (16549, 16697). Nothing else.

### Defensive operations, capital defense, transfers, theaters

- `CheckForDefensiveOperations` (13505-13780, job 15): per faction per cycle at most one op. Enemies sorted by min(distance to their closest enemy, distance to our capital / 3); an enemy qualifies when its **destination** is in our state and no op exists within 50. Candidates must pass `IsUnitAvailableForDefensiveOperation` (13460, private static), supply in zone, weather, distance, theater box; units accumulate to `enemy × higherstrengthdefensiveoperations`, then a probability roll; dispatch is a direct `DefensiveMovingOrder` (13713) and the unit moves from the offensive to the defensive list. A second branch attacks an enemy in enemy territory with its own roll and adds to the defensive list as well (13721-13779). (Vanilla bug: 13610 tests availability against `enemyunits[j]`, the unsorted list, while iterating the sorted `list4`.)
- `CheckForEndOfDefensiveOperations` (13782-13848, job 16) removes listed units with no path / retreating / in battle, and otherwise **calls `StopRegiment`** (skipped for W&L player units, 13840) and removes the unit when no enemy unit or enemy-connected depot is within its `buglerange` of its **destination**. `DefensiveOperation.UpdateDefensiveOperation` (10146-10252) also `StopRegiment`s on collapse (10197-10207). **Never put a planner-moved unit in `unitsindefensiveoperations`.**
- `AssignUnitToDefendCapital` (11668-11869, job 18): requirement = `aiminimumstrengthformovement` + Σ over enemies within relocation range of `min(str, str×morale) / max(10, d)^0.3 × 0.5` − own str×morale closer than the nearest enemy (11712-11741; the garrison loop 11742-11750 is dead). It prunes weak, low-morale, retreating and `IsMovedByPlayer` members (11751-11759) and **releases the farthest member when the requirement is met** (11760-11781); it adds the single closest qualifying unit per call (11791) and strips it from every other list (11810-11817); members are moved behind the capital toward the nearest enemy (11819-11867; W&L player units get order 8, and vanilla still sets their `running`/`doublequick`, 11865-11866). Members are excluded from offensives (14118), down-weighted in the recruiting heatmap (12328) and preferred fort builders (16362).
- `CheckTransferOfUnits` (17232-17332, job 27, alliances 0-1): uses `positionsurplus`/`positiondeficit` that `ZoneRecruiting` builds from the 20×20 own/enemy heatmaps over own-town cells (12467-12520); capped by `maximumunittransferstrength × troopsfielded` minus strength in transit; moves **one brigade per call** by `BattleUnits.MoveUnit` (93091, reparenting; `SetTransfer` 93147 sets an abstract `transfertime = distance / speedforunittransfers`, 139051) — not a rail move. **In a W&L career it does nothing for any faction:** 17328 calls `MoveUnit` without `skipdlccheck`, and `MoveUnit` returns unless both units are player-owned (93099), while 17327 has already appended the unit to `unitstobetransfered` (BUG-WL-013). `CheckCombinationOfUnits` passes `skipdlccheck: true` (17158) and works.
- `Regiment.theaterposition` (public, saved; 110494) — job 25 (`UpdateCampaignTheaters` 17034-17110) sets it to the midpoint between the unit and its closest enemy when unset and that enemy is within `commanderrange × 5`, and **clears it when the unit is outside the box** (17070-17084). `IsWithinOperationsTheater` (17090-17110) is an axis-aligned box of half-extent `GamePrefs.theatersize` (true when unset or `debug_usecampaigntheaters` is false). It **confines** vanilla's offensive targets (14138), defensive candidates (13610), capital candidates (11791), the counter-attack (13744), combination (17134, 17215) and army groups (17729-17751); it does not attract a unit anywhere. There is no theater id on `AIArea`.
- `ArmyGroup` (75022-75065) has no objective field; `GetGroupBonus` (75783) is a command bonus only. `AppointCommander(ArmyGroup, int, bool usefeudmechanism = false, bool resetcoordinationtime = true, bool ignoreprestige = false)` (75559). Job 32 (`CheckArmyGroupManagement` 17705-17767) adds top units (> 1000 strength, with a theater) to an in-range group or creates one; it can adopt the W&L player's top unit (17734, `ArmyGroup.AddUnit` 75345 has no guard), and it returns for **every** faction when the W&L player is CIC (17723, BUG-WL-001).
- Other movers: `LinkToFightingUnits` (16940, job 23) links non-fighting units to the closest fighting force (followers only); `CheckCombinationOfUnits` (17112, job 26) reparents a corps under an army HQ (Grand Army project 90); `CheckFinalPositions` (17898, job 34) moves idle units off water; `RollUpEnemyObjectivesInZone` (13883, job 35) moves one idle offensive-stance unit to the closest enemy objective in its own area; `CheckStanceSetting` (job 13, 11893-11930) sets `campaignstance` from area strength ratio × `(1 + (initiative − 0.5) × 0.6)` (11913-11914).

### Movement (`MoveUnitTo` 14479-14540)

`MoveUnitTo` returns false for a null unit, a neutral destination state or a unit in battle, then applies a stacking offset. `movearoundenemyunits: false` → `SetWaypoint` at once (14514). `movearoundenemyunits: true` → **returns false when an order is already queued**, else enqueues a `DefensiveMovingOrder` and returns true: *queued*, not moved. The queue is drained by `BattleUnits.Update` (79295, then 9965-10052), about one order per two frames, globally serialized and blocked while `Retreat.retreatorder` is non-empty, so a burst of Whiskey moves waits behind vanilla. There is no W&L guard inside (callers check `dlcw_isundercommander`). Rails are implicit: units of non-player alliances (`alliance != playeralliance`, 112862) are created with `userails` and `userivers` on and `usesea` off (112862-112867), while the player's alliance (including the W&L player's AI side under `ai_vs_ai`) keeps the values its units already carry; and `SetWaypoint` allows the rail area when transport capacity is under `greyoutmeansoftransportcap` (91603-91635). `CheckIfFinalWaypointIsPassable` (25608-25671) walks the target back toward the start until `IsTerrainPositionRestricted` (25755-25806: terrain 1, wrong water type, state out of range, neutral state) is false, else returns the start (no move).

### Supply and reachability

- `AIArea.UpdateSupplyCapacity` (10772-10802) = Σ non-trade-node visible IIP `GetTransportCapacity() × 2` + per depot the mean of `stock / 10 × Condition`; it ignores the owner of both. `SupplyInZoneAvailable(pos, f, from)` (10941-10962) is true in the same zone, with no zone, or when `ownpoints[f] < supplycapacity` (`ownpoints` = Σ |`GetUnitPoints`| of own units by **destination**, 10571-10580): a crude stacking cap.
- **Per-unit supply** is `Regiment.UpdateIIPSupplyLink` (114074-114500, slow cadence 112258): candidates are IIPs and buildings within the unit's own campaign `commanderrange` (`UpdateIIPsInRange` 113997-114010; range = `commandrange[unittyp] × (1 + readiness and initiative terms)`, 125830-125838). The best own-owned IIP by `transportcap / dist / avgPrice` (114272-114295) or an own/allied depot in range (114296-114306) supplies it; with neither, or while `running|doublequick` (114258-114266), `supplystate` is set to zero. Supply speed interpolates over distance 0..2000 and the enemy-soil factor (114317-114345). The AI's per-group gate is `GetGroupSupplyState` (14063-14066) = `max(avg(groupsupplystate[0..3]) × 0.5, supplystate × 0.5)`: both inputs are 0..1, so the value **never exceeds 0.5**. Vanilla's 0.25 thresholds (job-19 depot trigger 14715, fort builders) therefore mean 50 % real supply, and its 0.75 `minimumsupplyoffensivetargets` gates (raid `SearchObjective` 9135, the micro-move gate 14019) can never pass: vanilla raid forces probably never pick a target (being resolved by `fix/campaign-logistics-bugs`). **The AI never checks supply before or during an offensive:** `IsUnitAvailableForOffensiveOperations` (14080-14158) has no supply term, job 6's supply gate is dead (`flag` is always true, 14009-14019), job 6 re-targets listed units on morale/readiness only (14023-14030), and `RelieveUnitsInOperations` (14068-14078) removes only retreating or low-morale units from the operation lists. Zone supply capacity freezes after the second AI run: `AIArea.iips/cbuildings/towns` are only filled while `airuns` ≤ 1 (14927, 14946, 14965), so later depots never count (being fixed by `fix/campaign-logistics-bugs`). Job 19 also admits an unguarded enemy depot as a supply source (14740) and skips a unit with any own-started depot in range, built or not (14683-14715); same fix slice. **There is no route or rail-connectivity model**: rail only multiplies an IIP's transport capacity when a connected railroad is complete (104652-104668).
- Campaign NavMesh: `AddPath` sets global area costs (130395-130466; sea 500000 unless `usesea`, rivers by `userivers`, rails 1 when built) and calls `CalculatePath(start, target, -1)` (130473). "Closed" is a cost, not impassability, and the costs are global state left by the last unit; a planner probe would have to set them itself and inspect corners. The cheap target gate is `IsTerrainPositionRestricted` / `CheckIfFinalWaypointIsPassable`.

### Construction

- `CBuilding.AddConstructionWish(id, pos, iip, alliance, overridealreadyconstructing)` (97479-97536): returns the IIP's `currentlyunderconstruction` building when one exists (pending economy wishes set it, 15031/15336), null for a null IIP, otherwise `Place(…, newlycreated: true)` at once — **cost is charged at wish time** (96211-96257) — and queues an `AIPlacement`. `WorkDownConstructionWishes` (97538-97591, every `Update`) spirals around the wish for a `CheckIfTerrainOK` site and removes the building without refund when none is found within `buildingsitemaxradius`. Building ids (95083-95121): supply depot 22, fort 7, telegraph 23, entrenchments 26, siege fortification 27. Railroads are not buildings.
- **Builders must stay.** For fort, telegraph and depot types, a friendly unit of the building alliance must be within its own `buglerange` and not retreating or progress reverses, and the site is removed after `timetoremovemilitarybuildings` (`IncreaseCondition` 96626-96655).
- **AI depots** (`CheckSupplyDepotConstruction` 14659-14779, job 19): per own unit with supply below `supplystatedepotconstruction`, idle, no own depot under construction within `commanderrange`: move to an own town or depot (Condition ≥ 0.75) within range but outside bugle range (14762-14768), else **wish a depot at the unit's own position** when it has `closestiipforsupply` and no depot within range (14770-14775). One depot wish per faction pass. `unitsconstructingsupplydepots` is rebuilt (Clear + reassign) every job 19 (14683-14712, 14778).
- **AI forts** (`CheckFortConstruction` 16347-16405, job 21): one fort order per alliance at a time (16368); sites only from `fortconstructionsites` (scenario forts not built at start, 81931-81943), spacing `maxdistancetootherfort` (×0.6 near the capital), within `rangefortconstructionsite`, near the own frontline; `FortConstructionOrder` moves the builder and wishes fort level 1 on arrival (9636-9675, 9857-9865). #27 already filters the site list.
- **AI railroads** (`UpdateRailroadConstruction` 16052-16074, from the alliance tick 81741): for alliances 0-1 (the player's only under `ai_vs_ai`), **each predefined line is rolled independently** at `(probrailroadconstructionperyear + per-industry-policy bonus) × timediff` and `StartConstruction(alliance)` (77818-77845: needs `AllianceOwnsAllIIPs` and `IsPermitted`, costs nothing). Progress is monthly (77859-77893). Choosing *which* line starts has no Postfix lever (a filtered list lowers the rate); see the railroad user decision.

### Campaign fog

- Both grids run on the campaign map: `fow[0].InitFOW(true, calledfromcampaign: true)`, `fow[1].InitFOW(false, true)` (29916-29920); `FogOfWar.Update` (100675-100714) every frame (no blur on `fows[1]`). `CreateFOWObjects` (116505-116517): player-alliance units `fow = fows[0]`, every other alliance `fow = fows[1]`. Viewers (`AssignUnitToFOW` 116520-116534) are units with campaign visibility state ≠ 0 (82934-82974: garrisons, corps, top-unit divisions, armies, fleets), garrison dummies (82108) and detachments (90478); inactive or routed viewers are skipped (101325). Radius = `maximumlineofsight[unittyp]` (campaign groups: division, corps and army all 50, `unitprefs.txt` 645/689/733) × a perk-8 factor (101298-101301; battle units only once BUG-AI-003 is fixed, see below) × (1 − climate layer 105 × `influenceofrainsnowonvisibilitycampaign`) (101296-101302) × lie-down and W&L incident 63 terms (101303-101307); the terrain raycast applies (101098); crop/forest reductions do not (101102). **Concealment is painted on campaign** (101116-101128, campaign values from `campaignprefs.txt`).
- `UnitIsHidden` (101179-101229) on campaign is called from `CheckRegimentVisibility` every frame (111994 → 127225/127229) with `(GetPosition(), commander.GetCommanderCunning(), strength / unittypesize[unittyp], liedown, coverobject, covervalue, unittyp, this)`. Cover search is battle-only (116274), so the cover term is 0. Hidden = outside vision, or cell concealment − cunning term − size term − `spottingfactor[unittyp]` − perks 3/8 < 0. W&L incidents 60/61/64 make everything visible (101183-101186). The `relativestrength` argument uses the top unit's own `strength` field; the campaign aggregation sums only unittyp ≤ 13 into `groupstrength` (120284-120288), so for HQs it is probably small (inferred from the code, not measured; vanilla's own behaviour either way).
- `iswithinfog` is "hidden from the player alliance": forced false for player-alliance units on campaign (127211-127214), `fows[0].UnitIsHidden(...)` for every other unit (127223-127225). `hqinthesaddle` is battle-only (101312, 127211), so the campaign `fows[0]` is never blanked.
- **There is no AI-side campaign fog in vanilla.** `SeparateUnits` fills `enemyunits` from every at-war campaign group at true position (10458-10515); `UpdateAreaPoints` even uses the enemy's *destination* (`GetLastTransmittedPathPos(ignoreorderdelay: true)`, 10535, 10582-10594). `SpottedEnemy` (142644-142941) is player-only; `groupintelligencegathering` (120417-120620) only feeds readiness; there is no newspaper, telegraph or cavalry-scout detection path. `GetIntelligenceOnPos` returns 1 unless the inspector-only `UseIntelligenceMap` is set (100594).
- **Perk-8 bug (BUG-AI-003, fixed by `fix/campaign-perk-ids`):** vanilla `UpdateViewRange` multiplies the radius by `perk_expertscoutsvisibilityrangefactor` when `HasPerk(8)` (101298-101301) with no campaign guard, and `HasPerk` (132765) does not remap ids; on the campaign map perk 8 is Ambulance Corps (`PerkTooltipsArmyCampaign.txt`:100) and Expert Scouts is 16. The fix (flag `Fix Perk Id Mismatches`, default on) makes the perk table follow the unit type (≤ 13 battle, 17 fleet, 14-16 and 18 campaign), so the painted radius becomes `maximumlineofsight[unittyp] × (unittyp ≤ 13 && HasPerk(8) ? perk_expertscoutsvisibilityrangefactor[GetPerkLevel(8)] : 1) × weather` (campaign weather `1 − climate(105) × influenceofrainsnowonvisibilitycampaign`, battle `lineofsightreduction`), then × `fow_lineofsightreduction_layndown` when lying down and × the W&L incident-63 multiplier for the player's command. Campaign viewers are top units, so no campaign perk (Expert Scouts 16 included) changes campaign sight. Whiskey's `SightRadiusFormula` computes that radius, takes the flag as an input and keeps the old factor only when the flag is off, so the viewer check always matches what paints `fows[1]`.
- **Every non-player alliance shares `fows[1]`** (116513-116516). European units, including a European power at war with the AI side (Spain allied with the Union vs the CSA, 15740-15745), paint the non-player AI's grid.
- **Fort garrisons (decided 2026-10-15, G0 landing F2):** a fort garrison is a `CBuilding.garrisonunit` campaign group (81985-82093); towns have no garrison link. The side knows the exact strength of its own and allied forts' garrisons only. An enemy fort garrison enters the planner only as a belief from the G1 detection rule (seen on the grid or in contact), like any other enemy group; the town's ownership stays public. A harness source pin keeps every `garrisonunit` read in the snapshot runtime behind the own-or-allied check.
- `GameVars.usefow` (default true, 64636; prefs 199481, options 193645, save 216608/69253) off → `InitFOW` deactivates (100755-100759), every `iswithinfog` is false, and **`UnitIsHidden` dereferences the unallocated `concealment` grid at 101188 (NullReferenceException)**: gate on `usefow` before calling it.

### Reconnaissance, intelligence and march (vanilla AI)

- `GameVars.Commander` (58926-59026): identity (`name`, `combinedname`, `alliance`, `stateid`), `rank` (1 Lt … 6 BG, 7 MG, 8 LtG, 9 Gen), `status` (0 active, 2 POW, 3 WIA, 4 KIA; the W&L player is never killed or captured, 126777-126801), `exp`, `fame`, `leadership`, `initiative`, `administration`, `cunning` (+ histories), `veteran`, `westpoint`, `political`, `historicalbranch` (2 artillery, 4 navy), `usedummyphoto` (generated officer), `dateofbirth`, `dateofpromotion[]`, `dateofunavailability`, `currentcommand`, `refreshcommand`, `campaignranking`. **There is no aggression, personality, trait or "available from" field**; personality exists only for the W&L player. - **Experience.** `ResetExperience` (60840-60864) caps `exp` at the alliance `militaryexperience` (+ veteran bonus); service adds 0.2/year up to that cap (`IncreaseExperienceThroughService` 59191-59207, called at 75155 and 114997); battle casualties add exp up the chain without the cap (126949-126975). `Alliance.militaryexperience` loads from `nations.dat` (66069), grows from battles (70301-70305) and military schools (81815), capped at 1.

Leadership and administration are not used inside `AICampaign` (administration drives desertion 86329/115192, supply line 117799, fatigue 132644; initiative command range 125829).
- **Replacement.** (a) A fallen commander (`status > 0`, command not in battle) is replaced through `ReplaceCommanderOfUnit` (80022-80028), offering the command to the W&L player first (`OfferNewCommand(1, …)`). (b) **Job 24** `CheckAICommanderReplacements` (17008-17032): per rank, when `alliance.bestcommanderidperlevel[r]` (idle, `CanBePickedForAIReplacement`) beats `worstcommanderidperlevel[r]` by `expdifferenceforverticalpromotion` (0.1) on `GetAverageAttributeValue`, the best takes the worst's command (`AssignCommando` 17024, `DoCommanderPromotion` 17025, `refreshcommand = true` 17026). Best/worst come from `UpdateCommanderRankingsCampaign` (84424-84487), which excludes navy and commanders of player-owned units (84449). (c) `CheckVerticalReplacement` (17662-17687) lets a subordinate replace his superior; it returns for every commander of `playeralliance` with no `ai_vs_ai` escape, so in W&L it runs for the enemy only. `ReplaceCommanderOfUnit` (81389-81486) prefers the best direct subordinate, else `GetSuitableCommander` (66459-66558: `CanBePickedForAIReplacement` 60748-60779, `!refreshcommand`, `CheckSuitableRank` 60556-60628), else a dummy. `campaignranking = cmdLevel × 100000 + rank × 10000 + exp × 1000 + fame × 100` (84445).
- **Promotion.** AI: `CheckCommanderPromotion` (17884-17896, both sides in W&L, never the player or commanders of player-owned units) promotes +1 when `exp × 0.6 + fame × 0.525 + seniority × 0.5 > 1`. **Prefix-skip justification (C11; user consent 2026-10-15, as AGENTS.md requires for a new Prefix-skip).** `UpdateRailroadConstruction` (16052-16074) is 22 lines with no side effect besides `Random.Range` per line and `StartConstruction`. The replacement runs vanilla's own guards (16054), computes vanilla's probability (16058-16066), and before any start takes a snapshot `eligible[j] = constructionprogress ≤ 0 && StartConstruction(alliance, checkonly: true)` for every line (vanilla starts only the line it is rolling, so at line `j`'s roll the state of `j` equals the state at entry). It then rolls once per line in vanilla's order (one `Random.Range` per line, as 16069 evaluates the roll first). A success on a line that is not `eligible` starts nothing, exactly as in vanilla. A success on an `eligible` line starts the highest-scored line that is `eligible` and not yet started in this call (line `j` itself when none scores higher). Each such success therefore starts exactly one line, every started line is one vanilla could have started, and the number and timing of starts equal vanilla's for the same rolls; only the choice changes. The harness pins it against a replica of vanilla's loop with unstartable lines in the fixture. No Postfix can redirect an independent per-line roll, and filtering `BattleUnits.railroad` would lower the start rate. The patch stands down (returns true) for the W&L CIC player's alliance, alliances ≥ 2, and with its flag off.

## Architecture

```
daily review (per alliance, alliances 0/1; skipped for the W&L CIC player's alliance; on vanilla's job-machine predicate 11368 the executors of every alliance pause and only bookkeeping runs)
  CampaignSnapshotRuntime ──► CampaignSnapshot (immutable)
  EnemyArmyModel.Update(snapshot detections) ──► believed enemy armies (persisted)
  CommanderProfiles (vanilla commanders → personality modifiers)
  TheaterPlanner.Plan(snapshot, beliefs, priors, profiles, state, rng) ──► TheaterPlan
  LogisticsGate.Evaluate(plan, snapshot) ──► reachable targets + depot tasks
  ForceAllocator.Allocate(plan, snapshot, reliability) ──► Allocation
  CommanderAppointmentPlanner (competence + historical succession prior) ──► appointment intents
  Executors (idempotent, rate-limited, W&L-guarded) ──► vanilla hooks
```

### Components

1. **`CampaignSnapshotRuntime`** (runtime) → `CampaignSnapshot` (pure data): formations (position `GetPosition()`, last waypoint, `groupstrengthdirect`, morale, raw supply (`max(avg(groupsupplystate[0..3]), supplystate)`, 0..1 — vanilla's `GetGroupSupplyState` without its halving, C20), readiness step, battle/retreat flags, `theaterposition`, the W&L chain class from stage F's `WlStrategicOrderBridge.ChainClassOf` (which folds `dlcw_isundercommander`, `IsMovedByPlayer`, the map-group test and the carrying group into one class), membership of every vanilla busy list, the persisted identity `unitKey` (`name:commander`, the sidecar's existing convention), commander id, `commanderrange`, `buglerange`), areas (importance, own points, supply capacity, average position, zone edges — never vanilla's enemy aggregates `points`, `distancepoints`, `enemycampaignunitsstrength*` or `enemyavgcampaignunits`, which `UpdateAreaPoints` builds from every enemy at its destination, 10535-10552, 10582-10594, 10642), objectives (live positions from `CampaignObjective.objectives` only — the hand-coded `ObjectiveCatalog` coordinates are removed), capitals, depots/IIPs (owner, transport capacity, condition), railroads (line, progress, startable), `CampaignMapLedger` assets, battle history, the player's echelon (`GetPlayerCommandHierarchy`) and map-group status (8271), raw enemy groups (read **only** by the detection step). One build per review; replaces the scattered per-runtime reflection.
2. **`EnemyArmyModel`** (pure, persisted): an enemy campaign group is *detected* on a review day when (a) `GameVars.usefow` is off (omniscient, tagged `mode=NoFog` in telemetry), or (b) for an AI side that is not the player alliance, `!ownUnit.fow.UnitIsHidden(enemy.GetPosition(), <127225 arguments>)` on `fows[1]` **and** an own or allied viewer is within its campaign sight radius (101296-101302) of the enemy, or (c) for the W&L player-alliance AI side, `!enemy.iswithinfog`, or (d) contact: the enemy is in battle with the side, or within an own unit's `buglerange`. It stores last-seen position, strength (`groupstrengthdirect`), unit type and day, keyed by the sidecar's stable `unitKey` (`name:commander`), never a Unity instance id (instance ids do not survive save/load); a belief whose key is not seen again is re-keyed when a detection with the same unit name and type appears (a commander change), so a reload or an appointment never counts one army twice; uncertainty radius grows with age; entries expire. An enemy-held objective town (public ownership) yields a garrison belief only once the side has seen or contacted that town's fort garrison (decided 2026-10-15 at the G0 landing: the snapshot carries `ObjectiveRecord.GarrisonStrength` for own and allied forts only, never an enemy fort's `garrisonunit` strength). Enemy-vs-enemy cases with no grid (a European enemy of a non-player AI side) use (d) only. It never reads enemy positions except through this filter; `ContactEvidenceLedger` (battle-history contact classes) is adopted as an input in the same step.
3. **`CommanderProfiles`** (pure + runtime reader): personality modifiers derived from the live vanilla commander record of the alliance CIC (`GameVars.alliance[a].commanderinchief`) and each army-group/army/corps commander: `GetCommanderInitiative/Leadership/Administration()` (61134-61251, experience multiplier included), `rank`, and vanilla's own competence metric `GetAverageAttributeValue` (59175, which already averages administration, cunning, fame, leadership, initiative and exp). Outputs per theater: required-odds shift (initiative, as vanilla's own 14250 factor), hold-vs-attack bias (initiative vs leadership), supply-reach slack (administration). Cunning has no planner output (vanilla's campaign AI uses it only for burning depots, 17381). The historical prior stays a date-windowed weight (component 4); the director's tempo (`DirectorPosture`) scales the required ratio and the hold ratio.
4. **`TheaterPlanner`** (pure): sub-theater sectors built from AI areas (not the current 6 whole-theater buckets; the sector model is the planner's own, see C15). Per objective/sector live score = value (importance, objective role, capital/rail/port role) + believed force balance near it + supply reach (from `LogisticsGate`) − march distance for the candidate force − exposure + threat to own capital (believed enemy strength over distance, streak counter) + `λ·prior` (historical catalog as time-decayed weight: 1 inside its window, fading over months; `Priority` sets magnitude; λ tuned so the prior breaks near-ties but cannot overturn a live margin above a fixed bound — harness-tested). Personality modifiers shift thresholds. **Hysteresis:** switch main effort only if `best − current > θ` for M days or a trigger fires (decisive battle, capital threat, objective taken/lost). **Bounded randomness:** softmax within ε of the best with a deterministic seed (campaign id ⊕ alliance ⊕ decision serial) persisted in the sidecar. Output `TheaterPlan`: main-effort theater + objective + required ratio; per-theater posture (MainEffort / Hold / EconomyOfForce / Screen / Recover); capital-defense floor; depot tasks; commander-need hints. (Stage H adds its own axis fields when its plan names a reader.)
4b. **Reconnaissance** (`ReconPlanner` + `ReconMissionLedger`, pure; executor runtime) — added 2026-10-15 because beliefs only decay without it. ## Smoke amendments 2026-10-16

The first in-game smoke (W&L career, 1861-06-02, both alliances AI, the player below map level; tuning log `20261016-205617-615-p190172`) showed the planner marching an unscouted mass at infinite odds: every `CampaignPlan:trace` candidate read `odds=inf/0.82` with `e=0.00` (`TheaterPlanner.Evaluate`: `cand.Ratio = enemy > 0f ? own / enemy : (cand.Force >= 0 ? float.MaxValue : 0f)`, line 241, and `cand.Eligible = cand.Force >= 0 && cand.Ratio >= cand.Required`, line 266), the Union's main effort took 10 of 15 formations (`mass=67669`, `hold:1 eof:3`), the CSA's 6 of 7, the Union's moves failed and were given up (`failed=4 retried=4`, then `gaveUp=4`), no reconnaissance mission existed (`screens=0 pickets=0 advanceGuards=0 probes=0` against `unwatchedApproaches=3`), and the user saw "armies going all over the place and exposing weaknesses in their own lines". **Root causes (evidence).**

- *Infinite odds.* A target no belief speaks for read as "no enemy": `enemy = 0` gave the ratio `float.MaxValue`, every candidate with an own force was eligible, `MassFor` took every massable formation within 30 march-days, and G4 gave the whole mass `MainEffort` and the remainder of the main and Support sectors `Support`. A hold existed only in a sector with a believed enemy (`ReserveRules.Hold` skips `need <= 0`), so a quiet-looking front was stripped.
- *Failed and given-up moves, and the main effort that flipped 31 -> 28 -> -1 (D4, D5).* `Regiment.SetWaypoint` returns without a waypoint, and without a message for the AI, when the unit's readiness step is 0 or 1 and the target lies on enemy soil outside regroupment range (decompile 91336-91355; `GetReadinessStep` 173418: step <= 1 is readiness <= 0.5), and vanilla's own offensive availability asks step >= 2 (`IsUnitAvailableForOffensiveOperations`, 14091). A give-up removes a formation from the mass of that objective only (P8, by design: a refusal follows the target's state), so the other objective's mass still counted it: the main effort flipped to it, the formations failed there too three days later, and when every objective had been given up the main effort ended at -1. Three flips in nine days is exactly the issue / fail / retry / give-up cycle of three reviews. The earlier save that showed the flips (`Campaigns/001/B/Save`) no longer exists; the chain is reproduced from the code and the smoke numbers (`ScoutFirstTests.GaveUpFlipChainNeedsTheMassToBeRefusedFirst`, `LowReadinessFormationsAreNeverMassed`), and the new `ready`-aware scout pool in the `[Recon]` row (`pool=`) is how the next smoke confirms the readiness of the start. The fix is the root cause, not the hysteresis: formations vanilla would refuse to march are neither massed nor scouts.
- *No reconnaissance (D7).* `PlanTasks` plans a task only if some formation can take it (`TaskRanges` over `CanMove`: readiness >= 2, strength and morale above vanilla's offensive minima, not busy), and `PickFormation` takes only formations the allocator has not already given a role. With the mass full of low-readiness formations and the few ready ones already Hold, depot or capital, the Union had tasks (`unwatchedApproaches=3`) and nobody to send; the CSA had no unwatched approach. Reproduced on the pre-fix code with a Union-shaped fixture (twelve corps, a Union-sized army, one objective in reach): at readiness 1 for all, no task is planned, none is picked and the old mass still holds ten MainEffort formations; at readiness 3 and no cavalry the same army gets a probe, an advance guard and two screens. A second defect hid behind it: the main-axis screen and the probe both end at the standoff short of the main objective (same sector, same point), and `ReconMissionLedger.TaskFor` paired an assignment with a task by sector and point alone, so the probe's formation was bound to the screen's task (a second Screen mission, never a Probe). The cavalry hypothesis does not hold: `PickFormation` takes the smallest ready fighting group when no cavalry-majority group exists (tier 1), so 1861's lack of cavalry is not what blocked the screens.
- *Vanilla scouting.* The campaign AI never orders scouting: the only AI caller of `SetCavalryOrders` is the raid group (`raidgroup.SetCavalryOrders(2)`, 9177); 20336 and 20419 reset raiding units to 0; 40667 and 46313 are W&L player paths; every other `ChangeCampaignOrders(…, 120 + n)` call is the UI (205970-206084). `CheckCreationOfRaidForces` (17568-17612, alliances 0-1) creates a new cavalry group at an own town near the front (`new RaidForce`, 8920-8935; probability `probabilityofraidforces`, at most `maxairaidforces`) whose job is to raid an undefended depot or subsidy building (`SearchObjective` 9130-9185), not to look; no job reads `cavalryorders` 1 or 3 for a decision. There is no vanilla scouting rule to mirror; Whiskey mirrors vanilla's availability minima (14091-14106) for screens and pickets and its SetWaypoint readiness rule for every march.

**D1 Scout first.** A target is *scouted* when a fresh land belief (confidence >= `ContactEvidenceLedger.MinEscalationConfidence` 0.5, afloat armies excluded) lies within the relocation range of it, or an own formation's sight radius covers it (`TheaterPlanner.IsScouted`; fog-honest: own beliefs and viewers only). An unscouted target's odds are judged on `max(believed, presumed)` (D2's presumed threat), so they are finite, and its main effort may still be chosen. The balance term ranks by the believed enemy only (the presumption gates and sizes, it does not rank). An unscouted main effort is *staged* (`TheaterPlan.MainStaged`): `ForceAllocator` gives every mass member the plan's start line as its target, the forward-most mass member's position (`StartLineX/Z`; no formation is sent past one of its own side, and it stands in the supply that member stands in), the mover does not gate that gathering on the chapter tempo while the line is in own or neutral ground and does not evaluate the tempo roll (the roll is the march's), and the sector's target stays the objective so the recon planner's main-axis screen, probe and advance guard aim at it. The march is released when the target is scouted and the candidate is still eligible (`Ratio >= Required`): the plan reads `MainStaged = false`, the allocator gives the objective as target, and the mover evaluates the tempo roll as for any launch. The staged -> scouted step is neither a decision nor a switch (`DecisionSerial`, hysteresis and the odds debt are untouched); a scouted candidate whose odds then fall below the required ratio goes through the existing odds deadband and `odds` trigger.

**D1 addendum (verify round 1, F1): released stays released.** A decision whose target has been scouted stays scouted for that decision (`PlannerState.ScoutedDecision == DecisionSerial`, saved as `scoutedDecision`): a belief that decays below 0.5 later (the verifier's day-8 case, 0.45) does not re-stage a march that was released on a fresh picture and does not pull the corps back to the start line. The candidate's own estimate stays honest (it falls back to the presumption), only the staging is latched; the `[CampaignPlan]` row reads `scouted=latched`. A new decision starts unscouted.

**D2 Presumed threat and the hold of a front.** A sector *faces* enemy ground when an enemy-held objective lies within its radius plus the relocation range, or a land army is believed there. A facing sector keeps `HoldNeed = max(HoldRatio' x believed, MinGarrisonStrength, presumed)` where `HoldRatio'` is `HoldRatio` plus the director's modifier and, with nothing fresh believed within that reach, `presumed(x, z, r) = max(MinGarrisonStrength x (1 + I), S)`: `MinGarrisonStrength` 5000 is the smallest force vanilla's AI moves at all (`aiminimumstrengthformovement`, campaignprefs 1396-1397), `I` the largest `Importance01` of an enemy-held objective within `r` (public ownership and importance: a more valuable objective is presumed better held, up to twice the floor), and `S` the strongest army the side's own beliefs remember there below the freshness bar, at its last-seen strength (an aging sighting does not shrink the enemy). `aidominanceforoffensiveoperations` enters through `RequiredRatio`, not the hold. A sector with no enemy ground in reach owes nothing. `ReserveRules.Hold` reserves the nearest massable formations until the sector's need is met, as before; a facing sector with own formations and nothing believed is Hold (own >= need) or Screen.

**D3 Push only what it needs.** `Candidate.Mass` is the *capacity*: the massable formations of the target's department (D6 detail) plus the one reinforcement, within the march horizon, net of the capital reserve and every sector's hold. Eligibility is capacity over the estimate against `RequiredRatio`, as before. The selected mass (`TheaterPlan.MassInstanceIds`, `MassStrength`) is the nearest armies of the capacity until `Need = max(MinGarrisonStrength, estimate x RequiredRatio x (1 + margin))` is met, `margin = 0.25 x (1 - 0.5 x AttackBias)` (a bold commander pushes lean: 12.5 %, a cautious one 37.5 %; `NeedFor`). `ForceAllocator` gives MainEffort to exactly that set; every other formation keeps its own front's role (Hold in place or the hold reserve, Recover, depot builder, recon), the main and Support sectors included: nothing is "the remainder to the main effort". `FormationRole.Support` is now the single reinforcement from another department, marching to the same point as the mass. The map-group player's group is never in the mass and so keeps its role: his Support order path (`IssuePlayerOrder`, `OrderPlayerSupport`) is deleted. The `[Allocation]` row shows `mass=`, `need=`, `cap=` and `staged=`; the `CampaignPlan:trace` rows show each candidate `scouted|unscouted est= pres=`.

**D4 Hysteresis.** The switch margin (0.12 for three reviews) and the odds deadband are unchanged and hold. What changed the main effort three times in nine days was D5's silent refusals chained through the per-objective give-up exclusion; with formations vanilla would refuse no longer massed, a give-up is a real refusal of a target (a state at peace, restricted ground) and the P8 design applies as intended. A staged main effort turning scouted is not a switch (D1).

**D5 Failed and given-up moves.** Root cause above: readiness. `TheaterPlanner.MassEligible` asks `ReadinessStep >= MinReadinessStep (2)`, so such formations are neither massed nor reserved as capital guards; they hold in place and recover readiness. The scout pool counts only ready formations. Start-line points are formation positions, always reachable and inside the arrival radius of a formation already there (no move is issued).

**D6 Armies are territorial, in detail.** *(a)* Tests: `AnArmysCorpsGoTogether` (two corps of one army were split between the push and the front they came from), `AFormationTwentyFiveMarchDaysAwayIsNeverPulledIn` (a corps 24 march-days away joined any push inside the old 30-day horizon), `TheMainEffortDrawsFromItsOwnDepartmentFirst`. *(b)* Each formation has a *home department*, a `Theater`, kept in `PlannerState` by unit key and saved in the sidecar (`PlannerStateDto.departments`, additive; a file without it re-derives): the theater of the sector the formation stands in at the first review that sees it, kept until a deliberate transfer (`PlannerState.Observe`; an appointment re-keys a formation, `Observe` carries the department over by instance id inside a session). The department of a sector is its theater (`SectorModel.TheaterOf`). *(c)* The main effort draws from its own department: the target sector's theater (`MassFor`); `ForceAllocator.HoldKey` discounts a formation's distance to a sector's army by `HomeDepartmentDiscount` 0.25 when its department is the sector's theater (was a 0.05 historical tie-break; `HomeTheaterPrior`, which only knew some army names, is deleted: the department replaces it). *(d)* A formation of another department joins only as the one *reinforcement* of a main-effort decision: the candidate that can be at the target soonest within `ReinforcementMaxDays` 10 (`MarchTime.EstimateDays`, which credits a rail leg where `userails` and a train allow it), only while the losing department's D2 hold is still met without it (the sum of `SectorFacts.Need` over the sectors of its theater against what its other formations hold), at most one per decision (`PlannerState.ReinforcementKey/Decision`, saved); the transfer changes its department to the objective's (`PlannerState.Transfer`) and it is that decision's Support. *(e)* An army's corps go together: the selected mass takes armies (formations sharing a `ParentInstanceId`) nearest first and every capacity member of an army once it is entered; a corps its own department's hold needs is not in the capacity at all, so it stays. *(f)* #3's transfer routing is untouched (a separate follow-up). *(g, verify round 1)* A department whose unit key has not named a formation for `PruneAfterMissedReviews` (10) reviews in a row is pruned (`PlannerState.BeginObservation` / `PruneUnobserved`: a formation missing from one snapshot, inactive or briefly invisible, keeps its home; an empty snapshot, a failed read, neither prunes nor counts). Just before the sidecar is written every known formation is re-keyed from its live name and commander (`PlannerState.RekeyFromLive`, called by `CampaignPlannerRuntime.Export`), so a commander changed since the last review by vanilla (job 24, a death) is saved under the key the next load computes. An appointment that changes a commander (and so the unit key) re-keys the department at once (`PlannerState.RekeyInstance`, called by `CommanderAppointmentRuntime.AppointToUnit` for the target and the appointee's former command), so a save between the appointment and the next review keeps it. Telemetry: `[CampaignPlan] … depts=East:9,West:5 xdept=<name>:<from>><to>|none`.

**D7 Real scouts.** An advance guard or a probe is a *detachment* of the main body, not an offensive operation, so `ReconPlanner.CanMove(detachment: true)` asks readiness (SetWaypoint's own rule) and morale but not vanilla's 5000-man minimum of a whole operation (14099): a brigade or division detached forward leads the advance, as many 1861 advances were led; a screen or picket keeps all three minima. A cavalry-majority formation takes the screens first and gets vanilla's scouting order 121 (`ReconExecutor`, unchanged); without one the staged main effort's advance guard and probe come from its own mass. Missions are recalled on contact (a belief within bugle range, the standoff for an advance guard, battle), on low raw supply (< 0.5, vanilla's raid abort at 9092), and when the question is answered: the probe's task is not planned once `ProbeWanted` is false (a fresh belief near the objective or a viewer's sight on it, the facts `TheaterPlan.MainScouted` reports in the `[CampaignPlan]` row), so its mission stands down (`unassigned`). `TaskFor` pairs by sector, point and reason. The `[Recon]` row adds `pool=<n>`, the scout pool, so a day of unwatched approaches and no mission is explained by `pool=0`.

**D8 Secession (Kentucky, Missouri).** Vanilla gives the CSA two states it can win by secession: objective 15 (state 14, Kentucky, Louisville) and 16 (state 22, Missouri, St.Louis). `CampaignObjective.IsDeactivated` (178966-178973) switches each off while the state already belongs to the CSA or `nation.support[1]` (the CSA's support there) is below 0.45; `CheckIfAccomplished` (179259-179288) accomplishes it when the CSA owns the town and `nation.alliancetroops[0]` is false, which is true when no visible Union campaign group (`unittyp` > 13, not a fleet) stands in the state (81654-81660). The flag is set by the alliance-data pass (every 0.5 days per alliance, campaignprefs.txt:3319-3320, decompile 81492) for every Union unit with `unittyp` > 13 and not a fleet whose *map-symbol* visibility (`GetUnitVisibilityStateOnCampaignMap`, which returns 2 for a garrison unit with its `garrisonreference`, 82940-82943) is above 0 and whose point lies in the state: garrisons count, field armies count, and "visible" is the symbol state, not campaign fog. When the mass is the only presence (no flag, nobody else inside), one replacement formation is posted from outside at once through the ordinary garrison path (never from the mass, capped at one), so there is no unguarded gap between the mass leaving and the next review; that review, with the mass gone, finds the replacement inside and pledges it. The formations inside, nearest the town first (the mass excluded), the formations already standing in it, nearest the town first, until their strength meets D2's hold need at the town (`max(HoldRatio' x believed near the town, MinGarrisonStrength, the presumption while nothing fresh is believed there)`, the very `HoldNeedAt` the sector facts use, so the garrison is small when no enemy is believed near; the first formation always counts, because presence is what denies); with nobody present (no flag, no formation inside), the single nearest ready formation is posted there (presence is what denies; it is not sized to the threat); when the flag alone shows the Union present (a town garrison, no own formation inside), the flag says the state is *held*, not that it is *safe*: the need is the believed threat's hold less what is already kept against that same army and less the garrison's own strength (`HoldRatio' x the land threat believed within the relocation range of the town`, minus the sector hold reserves whose reference belief lies within that range, `TheaterPlanner.HoldFacing`, minus the strength of the side's own garrison units standing in the state, `SecessionRecord.GarrisonStrength`, read for the Union only), with neither the floor nor the presumption; the nearest free formations march to the town as `Garrison` (the mover, `MayMove`) until it is met, and nothing is posted when no enemy is believed near. ### Requirements

#### Enemy picture (root cause 1; decisions 2, 3)

- **requirement-enemy-total.** The snapshot carries `EnemyTroopsFielded` = `GameVars.alliance[e].troopsfielded` of the opposing major alliance `e = 1 - alliance` (alliances 0 and 1 only; Europe is not counted) and the enemy capital `EnemyCapitalX/Z`, `HasEnemyCapital` (`GameVars.alliance[e].capital`). Read by `EnemyPicture.Build` (pure, new). A value that is not finite or below 0, or an unreadable alliance, is *unknown*: the picture then spreads nothing (U = 0 below) and the row says `total=unknown`.
- **requirement-picture-partition.** `EnemyPicture.Build(snapshot, isScouted)` places every man of the enemy total exactly once, in three layers. `TheaterPlanner.Plan` builds it **once per review, before the first `Evaluate`**, into `Inputs.Picture` (displacement is judged on straight distances and needs no terrain probe, so both passes of a terrain-filling review see one picture; a bare `Evaluate` in a test builds it when `Inputs.Picture` is null), and hands it to the runtime through an optional `out EnemyPicture picture` parameter of `Plan` for the `[EnemyPicture]` row; `TheaterPlan.CapitalReach`, `CapitalWeighted` and `CapitalThreat01` carry the `[CapitalThreat]` row's numbers (read by `CampaignPlannerRuntime`, censused). *Limit:* `EnemyBelief` has no alliance field, so a belief about a European group at war with the side is subtracted from the major enemy's total too; named in the code, tagging beliefs by alliance is G1's and out of scope.
  1. *Believed:* every non-expired belief at its live strength (`ReserveRules.Live`, strength x confidence), as today.
  2. *Faded:* every non-expired belief's faded mass `strength - live` at its last-seen point. A belief's quantity is kept while its location ages; expiry (30 days, `EnemyArmyModel.ExpireDays`) returns its whole strength to the unaccounted pool.
  3. *Unaccounted:* `U = max(0, EnemyTroopsFielded - sum of every non-expired belief's last-seen strength)` (land and afloat beliefs alike: `troopsfielded` counts embarked men), spread over the *anchors*.

  **Anchors** are the enemy capital and every enemy-held objective record (`ObjectiveRecord.HolderAlliance` neither the side nor -1, not accomplished), records within `TheaterPlanner.SameTargetRadius` (1, the per-axis box of `SamePoint`) of an earlier anchor merged into it keeping the largest `Importance01` (one pass: the capital first, then the records in snapshot order); the capital anchor's `Importance01` is always 1, merged record or not (it is a capital, `Role01`). An anchor is **displaced** while it is scouted by D1's rule (`TheaterPlanner.IsScouted`: a fresh land belief within the relocation range of the anchor, or an own formation's sight radius covering it), with mark beliefs excluded (requirement-mark-beliefs). U goes `CapitalShare` (0.4) to the capital anchor when it is not displaced, and the rest to the undisplaced objective anchors in proportion to `Importance01` (equal shares when every weight is 0); with no undisplaced objective anchor the capital takes all of U; with the capital displaced the objective anchors take all of U; with every anchor displaced the capital anchor takes U anyway (where vanilla keeps its AI's capital defenders, 11668-11869). With no enemy capital the objective anchors take all of U by importance, displaced or not when every one is; with no anchor at all `Unaccounted` is 0 and `Unplaced` = U (`unplaced=`), so `believed + faded + unaccounted + unplaced` equals the total. `CapitalShare` (0.4) is the investigation's suggested value and is the share in force; the build also computes the **proportional** alternative (U spread over the undisplaced anchors in proportion to `Importance01`, the capital at 1, no floor share) and reports it beside it (`[EnemyPicture] capitalShareProp=`, `[CoveringForce] needProp=`), so the user can pick at the gate after F4 (`question-capital-share`).
  - *Elements:* the picture is a list of elements of five kinds, `Live`, `Faded`, `AfloatLive`, `AfloatFaded` and `Anchor` (an anchor with a share above 0), each a mass at a point. The faded sum of the row counts both faded kinds.
  - *Presumed(x, z, r)* = the `Faded` and `Anchor` elements within straight distance `r`, each weighted `min(1, r / RiverLines.EffectiveDistance)` exactly as `TheaterPlanner.LandThreatNear` weights a belief. Afloat beliefs add no faded land mass.
  - *Estimate(x, z, r)* = `LandThreatNear(x, z, r) + Presumed(x, z, r)`: the layers are disjoint, so they add. **This supersedes D1's `max(believed, presumed)`** and D1's "a scouted target's presumption is 0": a scouted target still carries the shares of anchors within its range that are themselves unscouted and the faded masses near it. The balance term still ranks by the believed layer only (D1 unchanged there).
  - *Floor(x, z, r)* = `MinGarrisonStrength x (1 + PresumedImportanceGain x I)`, `I` the largest `Importance01` of an **undisplaced** anchor within `r` (0 when there is none, so a place whose anchors are all scouted floors at the plain minimum), with the live `MinGarrisonStrength` of requirement-live-min-strength. One rule for candidates and sectors: a candidate's enemy is `max(Estimate(target, RelocationRange), Floor(target, RelocationRange))`, scouted or not (the scouted flag still drives D1's staging); `cand.Presumed` (`pres=`) reports `Presumed` whether or not the target is scouted.
  - D2's `S` term (the strongest aged belief at last-seen strength) is deleted: the faded layer carries that mass where it was last seen, once.
- **requirement-hold-need.** A facing sector's need is `max(HoldRatio' x (threat + Assigned(sector)), Floor(centroid, reach))`, reach = sector radius + relocation range; a sector that does not face enemy ground keeps `HoldRatio' x threat`. **Each presumed element counts in one sector only:** `Assigned(sector)` is the sum of the `Faded` and `Anchor` elements *assigned* to it, each weighted `min(1, reach / EffectiveDistance)` from that sector's centroid; an element is assigned to the nearest facing sector by centroid among the sectors whose reach holds it, the lower sector id on a tie. The believed `threat` keeps the shipped `LandThreatNear` overlap (a seen army near two sectors counts in both, as since G3); that overlap is deliberate and small next to the presumption's. A sector *faces* when its threat is above 0, an enemy-held objective lies within its reach (`EnemyGroundWithin`, D2) or an `Anchor` element does (the enemy capital need not be one of the side's objective records). **This supersedes D2's `max(HoldRatio' x believed, MinGarrison, presumed)`:** unseen men are held against at the same ratio as seen ones. `SectorPlan.BelievedHold` stays `HoldRatio' x threat`; D9's test becomes "belief-driven when `BelievedHold` is above the minimum and at least `HoldNeed - BelievedHold`" (the believed part of the sum is at least the presumed part), `ForceAllocator.SectorMayGiveScout`. **Departments (amends D6 (d)):** `DepartmentHoldMet`'s need is `DepartmentNeed(d) = max(sum of Floor over d's facing sectors, HoldRatio' x the land elements (`Live`, `Faded`, `Anchor`) assigned, by the same nearest-facing-sector rule, to d's sectors)`, so each element counts in one department once and the sector needs of a department never hold one army twice over; afloat elements are excluded, as from every hold need. With no such formation the reach is the relocation range. `CorpsRoadPerDay` = `MarchTime.SpeedPerDay(Speed, 15, Speed.MountedBaseSpeed[15], Speed.RoadCost)` (a null or short array gives 0 and the reach falls back to the relocation range; the index sits outside `SpeedPerDay`'s own guard), a corps' road rate at vanilla's group-speed ceiling (`unitbasicspeed[15,1]`, 120179; chain 133350-133354, 133535, 134083). Rails are not credited for the enemy (an enemy train is not observable). The `max` with the relocation range keeps every element vanilla's own capital defence counts. `TheaterPlan.CapitalReach` carries the reach in map units to the `[CapitalThreat]` row (`reach=`, `relief=`).
- **requirement-capital-floor.** `CapitalFloorStrength = max(MinGarrisonStrength + CapitalFloorRatio x weighted, CapitalFloorPolicy.VanillaNeed(...))` (vanilla's own baseline, 11712; `VanillaNeed` computed on the snapshot's beliefs and own formations, because it measures straight distances where `weighted` measures river-effective ones, so the second term is what makes the inequality below hold); `CapitalThreat01 = clamp01(CapitalFloorRatio x weighted / max(1, own within RelocationRange))`, the baseline **excluded**, so the 0.6 capital trigger (P4) never fires on an empty map. **This amends P7** (floor = ratio x weighted, no baseline). The floor is therefore never below #4's `CapitalFloorPolicy.VanillaNeed` on the same picture, so the `[CapitalFloor]` row's `floor=` and `vanillaNeed=` can no longer contradict each other; the row adds `reach=`. #4's own rule (pledge only while vanilla's need is positive) is unchanged.
- *Worked example (002/A, CSA, 1861-06-27, the investigation's geometry, **without marks** (before F4); the strengths are illustrative until the smoke after F4 reports the measured `[EnemyPicture]`):* the CSA's East department reaches from Richmond to the Valley (Winchester, 482 away); at the investigation's 140-190 units a day `ReliefDays` is about 2.5-3.5 and the reach about 480. A Washington anchor of 36,000 at 389 contributes `36000 / 389^0.3 x 0.5 = 3,010`; the Army of Occupation in the West (948 away) lies beyond the reach and adds nothing. Floor = 2,000 + 3,010 = 5,010. `CapitalThreat01 = 3,010 / own within 200 of Richmond`: the 0.6 trigger fires at the 002/A start only if that own strength is below 5,017; `CapitalTriggerAt002AStart` pins both sides of that line and the first smoke's `[CapitalThreat] threat=` reports the real start. **With marks (after F4):** the believed layer holds nearly every Union group within 25 units, U falls to garrisons and HQ residue, the Washington anchor shrinks accordingly, and the weight moves from the anchor to the believed Union corps near Washington; the floor stays of the same order whenever the Union army stands near Washington.

#### Covering force (decision 4; investigation (c), (h))

- **requirement-covering-post.** Both sides, each review, when the side and the enemy both have a capital. The *axis* is the segment own capital -> enemy capital. Two points on it come from vanilla's own capital-defence radius, the relocation range `R` (`AssignUnitToDefendCapital` counts an enemy only inside it, 11719-11727): the **forward point** at `R` from the enemy capital (the nearest the force can stand without entering the enemy capital's defence radius, where vanilla's own capital defence gathers against it) and the **rear point** at `R` from the own capital (the edge of the radius inside which vanilla's own capital defence already acts). Each post is the own-held **town** (`BattleUnits.towns`, `Town.Owner == alliance`) nearest its point within `R` of it (vanilla's offensive aims at towns first, `GetClosestObjectivePosOfArea` 14587-14632, so a town is where an advance on the capital is met), straight distances, the lower place index on a tie. When both points give the same town, or only one point gives a town, there is one post and no verdict hysteresis. No town within `R` of either point: no covering force (`reason=no-corridor-place`); the capital reach still counts the threat. Small towns, rail lines and any distance fitted to a town name are not part of the rule: **the posts are whatever it gives**. In 002/A the axis is 389 long, about `2R`, so both points fall near (1306, -821) and the rule is expected to give one post for each side: for the CSA the nearest own-held town (Fredericksburg, 63 from the point, if King George C.H. 21 from it is a small town as the save's type field suggests), for the Union its nearest own-held town (Alexandria, 161 from the point). Decision 4 named Manassas Junction forward and Fredericksburg as the fallback; Manassas Junction is a small town 116 from Washington, inside the enemy capital's radius, so the rule does not reproduce it; that is `question-corridor-posts`, recommended: accept the rule's posts. The snapshot carries the towns (`CampaignSnapshot.Places` of `PlaceRecord { Name, X, Z, Owner }`, declared in `CampaignPlannerContracts.cs` so the read census sees it; public map facts) in `BattleUnits.towns` order; the place index is that order.
- **requirement-covering-size.** The covering force is the **axis's own hold**, posted forward. The *axis sectors* are the facing sectors whose centroid lies within `R` of the axis segment (the same vanilla gather radius) and projects strictly between the capitals. `CoveringNeed = min(max(HoldRatio' x Estimate(enemy capital, R), MinGarrisonStrength), sum of HoldNeed over the axis sectors)`: the threat out of the enemy capital, capped at what the axis sectors already hold against (requirement-hold-need assigns Washington's presumed army to the axis sector nearest it, so the cap carries it once). The covering force is never a reserve on top of the sector holds, and it never draws from another front. *Example (illustrative, without marks):* 36,000 presumed at Washington and the 6,600 division within 200 of it give `0.6 x 42,600 = 25,560`, against the CSA's 19,400-man Army of the Potomac: the force is reported `short`, the verdict is rear (which in 002/A is the same post), and the CSA's eastern offensives stay ineligible while the presumption stands. After F4 the presumption mostly turns into believed Union corps near Washington and the need follows them; the gate after F4 shows the measured need (`question-capital-share`).
- **requirement-covering-members.** The members are chosen **inside `ReserveRules.Hold`, in the same pass as the sector holds**, so nothing is stripped to build the force: for each axis sector the hold reserve orders its massable formations by straight distance to the post (incumbents first: `PlannerState.CoveringKeys`, persisted; then distance times `1 - HomeDepartmentDiscount` for the post sector's theater, as `ForceAllocator.HoldKey`; then instance id) instead of by the nearest belief, and the first of the axis sectors' reserved formations, axis sector by axis sector in sector id order, are the covering members until their counted strength reaches `CoveringNeed`. A member stays claimed by its own sector's hold (claimant `Hold`, sector its own, reason `covering-force`, requirement-claims): it counts in its own sector's hold wherever on the axis it stands, and it was never available to any other front. Counted toward `CoveringNeed`: each member's strength, plus every own formation within `R` of the post (by `ReserveRules.HoldPoint`) that counts toward coverage, is not recovering and is not massable (the busy, the unready, the map-group player at strength x morale). What the axis sectors cannot give is the force's `short`. **W&L:** a `CarryingGroup` may be a member; the player's own command and a CIC side are never members and count nothing; the map-group player counts but is **never the only holder**: when the pick took no member and he is counted, the first reserved formation of the axis sectors is added even though the need is met.
- **requirement-covering-post-choice.** With two posts, the force stands at the forward post when its counted strength there reaches `CoveringNeed`, else at the rear post (the investigation's "too small"). The verdict changes only after `TheaterPlanner.SwitchDays` (3, D4's approved hysteresis, reused as its source) consecutive reviews of the other verdict (`PlannerState.CoveringForward`, `CoveringVerdictDays`, `CoveringVerdictKnown`, `CoveringPostKey` = the forward and rear town names with their rounded positions, `none` for a missing post; persisted). On the first review, or when `CoveringPostKey` changes (a town changes hands, a capital moves), the verdict is the raw one and the count restarts at 0; otherwise a raw verdict equal to the stored one resets the count, and a different one adds a day and flips the verdict at `SwitchDays` (the `ChallengerDays` pattern). On a `Kind = None` day: for `main-effort-enemy-capital` the covering state is left untouched (the incumbents survive the push); for `no-capital`, `no-corridor-place`, `flag-off` and `cic-side` `CoveringPostKey = "none|none"` is written, so a post that appears later is a key change and takes the raw verdict. Every `PlannerState` write here is applied once in `Plan` after `Decide`.
- **requirement-covering-hold.** In G4 (`ForceAllocator`, reading the claim table) a member within `max(BugleRange, CampaignMover.ArrivalRadius)` of the post is `Hold`, reason `covering-force`, withheld; any other member is `Redeploy` (requirement-redeploy-role), reason `covering-force`, target the post, withheld. **Defence:** a covering member is available to vanilla's defence when `TheaterPlan.Covering.ThreatNear` is set: at plan time a live land belief of the side's own lies within `R` of the post. `CampaignWithholdView.WithheldFromDefense` reads only that flag (`Read` also returns the plan; the clause sits after the shipped in-transit, held-builder and `!Withheld` tests and applies to a `Hold` or `Redeploy` member alike); it never reads the enemy position vanilla passes, which is `enemyunits[j]` from the unsorted list while vanilla iterates the sorted `list4[j]` (13600-13610, BUG-AI-002, recorded in the base spec; not fixed here) and is omniscient. A covering member stays withheld from every offensive. `MassFor` leaves covering members out of every candidate's capacity **except** a candidate whose target is the enemy capital (its record within `SameTargetRadius` of `EnemyCapitalX/Z`): `HoldThreatAtTarget` already releases an axis sector whose every element lies within `R` of that target. D9's scout draw, the depot builder, the garrison posts and the department reinforcement never take a covering member (requirement-claims).
- **requirement-covering-lifecycle.** Created each review inside the hold pass when both capitals and a post exist; changed by the incumbent rule and the post choice; unbound when a capital is lost, no town is within `R` of either point, the side's alliance is a CIC side (empty allocation), the flag is off, or the main effort targets the enemy capital (the axis holds are then released into that candidate's capacity by `HoldThreatAtTarget`; `TheaterPlan.Covering` reads kind `None`, reason `main-effort-enemy-capital`, and the members the mass did not take keep their hold claims). `TheaterPlan.Covering` is never null after `Plan`: `CoveringPlan { PostName, PostX, PostZ, Kind (None|Forward|Rear), Need, NeedProp, Held, Radius, ThreatNear, InstanceIds, Reason }` (`NeedProp`: the same need under the importance-proportional capital share, for the `[CoveringForce]` row and `question-capital-share` only), reason one of `no-capital`, `no-corridor-place`, `flag-off`, `cic-side`, `main-effort-enemy-capital` when Kind is None; rebuilt daily; `CoveringKeys` keeps the last members. In-flight members whose assignment changes get the new role at the next review; the mover's tracker judges or forgets their move as for any role change (P8).

#### One claim table (G3 and G4 agree by construction)

- **requirement-claims.** `TheaterPlanner.Plan` computes, once per review, which step holds each own formation, and publishes it as `TheaterPlan.Claims`: one `Claim { InstanceId, Claimant, SectorId, TargetX, TargetZ, Reason, Share }` per formation, `Claimant` one of `None`, `PinnedBuilder`, `Immobile`, `Capital`, `Hold`, `Committed`, `Returning`, `DepartmentMove`, `Mass`, `Garrison`, and per sector `TheaterPlan.SectorHeld` (what `ReserveRules.Hold` sized the reserve on, the D9 tally). The order is the one the reserves already use: before the candidates (inside `Evaluate`, against `PlannerState` as it stands) pinned builders (the depot ledger's Wished builders), immobile, capital, the sector holds with the covering members among them, committed, returning, department moves in flight; after `Decide` the mass (taking committed and returning formations first, as requirement-commitment and requirement-return-home say), D8's posts, the new department pick, the return moves. A formation is claimed once; a later step sees it claimed. G4 (`ForceAllocator`) reads the table instead of re-running `ReserveRules.Capital` and `ReserveRules.Hold` and instead of the separate masks (covering, committed, returning, in flight): its roles are the claims plus its own later steps (recover, garrison posts, depot, recon, reserve split, remainder), which take only unclaimed formations or the ones the claim rules release (a Reserve surplus, a hold reserve's post incumbent, D9's one scout). `Assignment` keeps its shape; the census names `CampaignPlannerRuntime` and `ForceAllocator` as the table's readers. This replaces the per-step masks of the earlier draft, the gap class every dry run found ("G3 and G4 must agree on which formation a mask holds").

#### The Redeploy role

- **requirement-redeploy-role.** `FormationRole.Redeploy = 9`: a formation the mover sends to a hold point of its own side (a covering post, a short department's sector, its sortie origin); within `max(BugleRange, ArrivalRadius)` of the point it is an ordinary `Hold` at the next review. The mover serves it with no tempo gate (own or neutral ground, like `CapitalGuard`) and in this order of (role, reason) under the daily cap of `MoveTracker.MaxMovesPerDay`: `CapitalGuard`; `Redeploy covering-force`; `MainEffort`; `Garrison`; `Redeploy department-reinforcement`; `Recover`; `DepotBuilder`; `Support`; `Redeploy return-home`. Home defence goes first and a corps going home never takes a move the push needs. Withholding: a `covering-force` member is `Withheld` (both pools, with requirement-covering-hold's exception); a `department-reinforcement` or `return-home` formation is withheld from vanilla's offensives only (`CampaignWithholdView.WithheldFromOffense` adds `Role == Redeploy`; `Withheld` stays false), so vanilla's defence may still use it on the way; while its move is issued and unjudged the tracker's in-transit rule withholds both pools, as for every role. The mover's priority becomes a list of (role, reason) pairs (a null reason matches any); `CampaignMover.MovesRole` (the recon executor's ownership test) and `ForceAllocator.Marches` (the give-up fallback to `Hold gave-up`) include `Redeploy`. The `[Allocation]` row adds `redeploy=<n>` (by role) and `covering=<n>` (members of either role, reason `covering-force`; a member at its post also counts in `hold=`). The read census names its readers (`CampaignMover`, `CampaignWithholdView`, `ForceAllocator.Summary`).

#### Department shortfall reinforcement (decision 5; investigation (g))

- **requirement-department-reinforcement.** After the mass (as D8's `PostFromOutside`), `TheaterPlanner.DepartmentReinforcement` ranks the departments whose hold is short (`DepartmentNeed(d)` of requirement-hold-need against `HoldStrength` of its formations) by `(need - have) / need`, ties to the lower `Theater`, skipping `Theater.Unknown` and any department with a record in flight, and gives the first one that has a donor **one** formation of another department that the donor's own hold does not need (`DepartmentHoldMet(donor, excluding it)`), that `MassEligible` admits, that no reserve, mass, garrison post, covering force or held builder holds, and that can be at the short department's neediest sector (largest `Need - held` of the sector's standing formations) soonest within `ReinforcementMaxDays` (10) by `MarchTime.EstimateDays` (rail leg credited), the lower instance id on a tie. **One in flight per receiving department and at most one new pick per review** (`PlannerState.DepartmentInFlight`: receiving theater -> unit key, donor theater, pick day, target fixed at the pick; persisted): a department with a formation in flight gets no second one, and the others may still get theirs. While the record stands the plan re-lists the formation in `TheaterPlan.DepartmentMoves` each review and its `DepartmentMove` claim keeps it out of the covering pick, the hold reserves, the mass, the garrison posts and the depot builders; the capital reserve may still take it (home defence first; the record is cleared, the department stays transferred). The pick transfers the formation's department at once (`PlannerState.Transfer`, as D6), so the receiver's hold counts it on the way and the donor's no longer does. G4 assigns it `Redeploy`, reason `department-reinforcement`, target the neediest sector's centroid (the lower sector id on a tie), withheld from offense only (requirement-redeploy-role). The in-flight record is cleared on arrival (within `max(BugleRange, ArrivalRadius)` of the target: it holds there by the ordinary rules), on a give-up (P8; the department is transferred back to the donor), or when the formation is pruned (D6 (g)). Fixes the West's lack of any reinforcement path (the existing cross-department path, D6's `ReinforcementPick`, serves only the main effort; #125 routes recruits within 1.5 x the relocation range of the recruiting cell only). *Pool and sums:* a department's `have` counts what `ReserveRules.Hold` counts as holding (no recovering, returning, committed or mass formation), and the donor pool excludes returning, committed, covering and in-flight formations; the neediest sector is a facing sector of the receiving department. *End of a record:* besides arrival, give-up and prune, the record is cleared the first review the formation's claim is anything other than `DepartmentMove` (it went Recover, is busy in battle or in a vanilla defensive operation, or a capital reserve took it): the claim table says so, no clock. Taken by the capital reserve, the department stays transferred (home defence of its new department); any other end before arrival transfers it back to the donor (row `state=Ended`).

#### Main effort commitment and return home (root cause 4)

- **requirement-commitment.** The mover records a *sortie* for each formation it dispatches as `MainEffort`, as `Support` with reason `reinforcement` (D6's one reinforcement), or as `DepotBuilder`, on any outcome that writes a tracker record (`Issued`, `Retried`, `Queued`; a refusal records nothing): `PlannerState.Sorties`, unit key -> objective id (-1 for a builder), role, origin = the formation's position when the first sortie of that objective was recorded (a re-issue, a retarget within the objective or a staged gather keeps it); persisted, additive. `MoveTracker` cannot carry this: a re-issue replaces its record and `FromX/Z` is not persisted. A formation is *committed* to objective `O` while it has a sortie of `O` and `O` is the state's `CurrentMainObjective` as `Evaluate` reads it (before `Decide`); a committed formation stays in the capacity of `O`'s candidates (`TheaterPlanner.Massable = MassEligible or committed`, read by `MassFor` only; the capital reserve, the covering pick, the garrison posts and the depot builder keep plain `MassEligible`, so the covering force never pulls a marching corps back) while it is movable, the planner may move it, it is not on retreat, its morale is at least `aiminimummoraleformovement` and its raw supply at least `RecoverSupplyBelow`, **whatever its readiness**: vanilla relieves an operation member only for retreat or morale (14072). **This amends D5** for committed formations only. `ReserveRules.Hold` neither counts nor reserves a committed formation (it is the push's, not the sector's), and `SelectMass` takes the chosen candidate's committed formations first, nearest the target first, then the rest by army as D3. Objective, not `DecisionSerial`, is the key: `Decide` leaves the serial alone on `no-candidate` and bumps it when the same objective is picked again from none, so a serial key would strand or drop the very corps the RC4 chain is about. *Accepted interaction:* with #25 observing (requirement-interception), vanilla's defence may take a mass member (the mass is withheld from offense only); it is then `InDefensiveOps`, busy and not committed until vanilla ends the operation; `[Reserves] massInVanillaDef=<n>` counts it, and the smoke reports it (the user's guidance: the reflex is never switched off).
- **requirement-commitment-mover.** The mover issues no march, and no retry, to a `MainEffort` or `Support` formation at readiness step below `MinReadinessStep` (2) when the target is enemy ground by the mover's own `EnemyHeld` test: `SetWaypoint` would refuse it silently there (91336-91350; regroupment range is not modelled, so this refuses a little more than vanilla does). `heldReadiness=` counts these, after the W&L and `Movable` skips and before the tempo deferral. The tracker judges every issued record as usual (an issued march continues at low readiness, 113937-113965 clears no path, so a moving corps is confirmed); a record judged `Failed` while the formation is below step 2 keeps its one retry until the formation is back at step 2 (the retry is the re-issue this rule withholds), so low readiness alone never produces a give-up and no clock decides it. A `Failed` record is not in transit, so the formation is not withheld from vanilla's defence meanwhile. The mover also takes every `Redeploy` formation out of vanilla's `unitsinoffensiveoperations` before it dispatches (`ListOffensive(id, false)`, in the pass that releases `Recover`): vanilla never de-lists for a role change (14072).
- **requirement-return-home.** A formation is *returning* when it has a sortie that is not committed (its objective is not the current one, or there is no main effort; a builder's sortie once the depot ledger no longer holds it as a builder), stands farther than `max(BugleRange, ArrivalRadius)` from its origin, and is movable by the planner. It is claimed `Returning` in the claim table (requirement-claims), read in `Evaluate` against `PlannerState` as it stands; `MassFor` admits returning formations (the new mass selects first), and after `SelectMass` the `Returning` claims are (those formations, plus the committed formations `SelectMass` did not take this review) minus the mass, each with one move to its origin: `Redeploy`, reason `return-home`, withheld from offense only, until it arrives (sortie deleted), gives up (P8; sortie deleted, it holds in place), or is pruned. A capital reserve may take it on the way (home defence first; the sortie is deleted then); the covering pick, the hold reserves, the department pick and the garrison posts do not; while returning it counts in no sector's or department's hold (it is leaving, as `ReserveRules.Recovers`). A formation the new mass takes keeps its sortie (and its origin) under the new objective. **Identity:** `PlannerState.RekeyInstance` and `RekeyFromLive` (D6 (g)) also move a formation's sortie, every `DepartmentInFlight` value and every `CoveringKeys` element under the old key, and sorties are pruned with the departments (`PruneUnobserved`), so the planner's own daily appointments never strand a corps.

#### Depot builders (root cause 3)

- **requirement-depot-builder.** `ForceAllocator.SmallestIn` (G5c) takes only a formation that `TheaterPlanner.MassEligible` admits (readiness step 2 and above: no unready builder), and on its first pass only a sector **surplus**: the sector's standing hold (`HoldStrength` of the formations whose `HoldSectorOf` is the sector, that count toward coverage, are not recovering, not mass, committed or returning, and are either not yet given a role or hold the sector as Hold or CapitalGuard; measured directly, because `ReserveRules.Hold`'s tally is 0 in a sector with no need, where surpluses live) less this formation still meets `Target(sector)`; on its second pass a member of the mass, never its last free body (`FreeMassBodies() > 1`, the rule recon already keeps). The hold reserve and the covering force are claimed before the depot step, so neither can give a builder. The order (surplus, then the mass) is G3's obligation kept; the investigation's "the main effort's own mass first" lost because D3 sizes the mass to exactly what the objective needs, while a surplus formation is free.

### Roles, lifecycles and conflicts

- **Who writes what.** Only the daily review writes: `TheaterPlanner.Plan` (picture, reserves, covering, department pick, sortie reads, `PlannerState` covering and department fields), `ForceAllocator.Allocate` (roles), the mover in the Execute job (`Sorties`, tracker), `EnemyDetectionRuntime` and `EnemyArmyModel.Update` in the Plan job (marks, beliefs). Patches read the published plan only (`TheaterPlan.Covering`, `TheaterPlan.MinGarrisonStrength`); `PlannerPatchesReadOnlyPublishedState` keeps pinning it. #25 writes nothing while it observes.
- **Shared rows and their conflict rules.** `PlannerState.Sorties` (unit key -> objective, role, origin): written by the mover (create, at first dispatch of a decision; origin never overwritten within that decision) and by the plan once per review after `Decide` (delete on arrival, give-up, prune, or a reserve taking the formation); the plan runs before the mover each review, so a delete and a create of one key never meet in one job; a create for a key with an open sortie of an older decision keeps the origin (a corps pulled into a second push still goes home to where it started). `PlannerState.DepartmentInFlight`: written by the plan only; one record per receiving theater; a second pick for a theater with a record is refused, never overwritten. `PlannerState.CoveringKeys/CoveringForward/CoveringVerdictDays/CoveringVerdictKnown/CoveringPostKey`: plan only. **A formation two steps want** is settled by the claim table (requirement-claims): one claimant per formation, in the reserves' order, computed once by the plan and read by the allocator; the allocator's own later steps take only unclaimed formations or the ones the claim rules release, and `_done` gives each formation one role.
- **Belief rows.** A mark and a Seen detection of one group on one day: the Seen detection wins (the mark is ignored); a mark never re-keys or retires another belief.
