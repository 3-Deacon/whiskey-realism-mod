## Reviewed, not a bug

**Ford pull-off (item 4).** `AICampaign.CheckFinalPositions` (17898-17932) moves an idle AI unit (no path, not retreating, not in a `DefensiveMovingOrder`, not under the W&L player's command) whose `currentterrain` is not in `terrainrestrictions_landmovment` {0, 3, 6, 7, 9-31} (64549-64554) to the nearest such cell within 200 ("found on water"). River Crossings (5) is not in the list, so an AI unit stopped on a ford is moved. The path is real, but it matches vanilla's evident intent:

- Rivers (4), fords (5) and sea (8) are excluded together; bridges (6) and creeks (3) are land. The same list places capital defenders (11831).
- `EstablishCampaignBattle` engages only along a clear terrain line: `CheckIfUnitIsWithinModifiedRange` → `CountTerrainLine`, which checks the start cell first and stops at any id of `terrainrestrictions_combat` {1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8} (27695-27718, 80502-80517, 80683-80684). A unit standing on creek, river or sea uses the water list {1} instead; a unit on a ford does not. So an army idling on a ford can neither engage nor (unless within 2) be engaged across the ford cells. Pulling idle AI units off fords keeps them out of that state.
- Treating a ford as land here would leave AI armies parked on fords immune to campaign engagement.

Fords stay walkable (`AddPath` prices them at cost[5] = 1 for every land unit, 130430; `CheckUnpassableTerrain` counts 5 as passable). No change. Side observation, not acted on: a unit stopped on a **bridge** (6) is in the same engagement shadow and is not pulled off.

**W-2 Engineer Corps once-effect.** Action 27 stores "Engineering Points +0.2" as effect 23 type 1 in `cumulativeeffectsonce` (44757-44759), but effect 23 is read only in running mode (87433). Already fixed on `fix/wl-economy-bugs` (BUG-WL-009, `WlActionOnceEffectsPatch`, `WlEconomyFixes.FoldsOnceEffect` includes 23 after a full sweep of every quality type). Not repeated here. Its other half, effect 14 type 3 "Reinforcement Speed", is read at 114555 as campaign refill speed (works; no battle-arrival consumer exists).

## Campaign forest and swamp (BUG-AI-015)

**Intent existed at authoring time.**
- The campaign prefs define two texture specs, Forest (texture 9) and Swamp (texture 10) (cp:677-680, 1001-1024). The campaign loader reads them into ids 32 and 33 (55826-55870).
- The shipped campaign terrain (`CampaignMap_v6`, `resources.assets` path id 299365, 2048 x 2048 world units, heightmap 1025 at scale 2, 29 layers, alphamap 2048) has layer 9 `DeepForest` and layer 10 `Swamp`. Dominant-layer share of the alphamap (all cells, water included): layer 9 23.4%, layer 10 2.9%. Other forest layers (14, 15, 16, 17, 19, 24-26: 28% together) and swamp layer 13 would stay plain grass.
- The labels are copied from battleprefs ("Texture ID Tex #14"), but the values differ (battle Forest is cost 4, concealment 10).

**But the code explicitly skips it and would misread it.**
- `ReadInTerrainSpecs` guards the texture pass with `!IsCampaign` (26577).
- Ids 32/33 would break campaign code written for mesh ids 0-31: `GetTerrainSpeedAdjustment` treats `currentterrain >= 9` on the campaign map as rail and substitutes the road cost (132891-132894), so forest would move at road speed (faster than grass, the opposite of cost 5); `CheckFinalPositions` would pull every idle AI army out of forest (32/33 are not in `terrainrestrictions_landmovment`); `UnitIsHidden` reads `terrainconcealment[currentTerrainOnPos]` with no campaign guard (101118), so concealment 50 would land on a quarter of the map at once.
- Campaign fatigue has no terrain term: the campaign branch of the regular update returns before `UpdateFatigue` (112203-112270 vs 112348), so only speed and concealment would apply.

**Cost.** The bake would add a `GetAlphamaps(x, y, 1, 1)` call (a fresh `float[1,1,29]`) per cell for 4.2 M cells to campaign load, on top of the existing `NavMesh.SamplePosition` per cell; battle maps already pay this. Not measured, since not implemented.

**Recommendation: leave vanilla.** Enabling it needs the bake plus three more fixes (the rail-speed test, the land list, a concealment decision) and would slow about a quarter of all campaign marches by 20% (cost 4 → 5) and add concealment 50 there: a campaign-wide rebalance, not a bug fix. If the user wants it, it is a design slice (forest/swamp as campaign terrain), with the layer set (9 only, or every forest layer) decided first.
