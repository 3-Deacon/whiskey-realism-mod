# Vanilla Campaign Logistics Bugs

Campaign AI supply, depot, deployment and tempo bugs confirmed on Steam build 14703111 (2026-10-15). Source research: the deploy/supply investigation (ranked findings #2, #3, #5, #6, #7, #11). Every anchor below was re-read in `/tmp/gt_src/asm/Assembly-CSharp.decompiled.cs`; `cp:n` is a line of `<GTCW>/Config/campaignprefs.txt`. Plan and smoke items: [`../superpowers/plans/2026-10-15-campaign-logistics-bugs.md`](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-15-campaign-logistics-bugs.md).

Each item was either **fixed** to vanilla's evident intent (config text, tooltips, the manual, or the surrounding code), or **recorded** with the evidence and left as vanilla where the intent is genuinely ambiguous. Fixes apply to every faction alike; none moves the W&L player's own command (vanilla's own move guards stay in place, and the raid fix skips player-commanded raid groups). All fix flags live in section `Campaign Bug Fixes` and default on.

## Known residue

- `Regiment.OnDestroy` (127111-127146) never destroys `campbuilding` or `entrenchment`, so a merged or disbanded unit leaves its camp model behind; this is vanilla for visible units. With `BUG-LOG-002` a unit removed while hidden leaves an inactive (invisible) camp object instead.
- The capital-garrison loop (11742-11750) and the `CheckForDefensiveOperations` index mismatch (13610) are owned by stage G (spec C-items), not fixed here.

## Not verified in game

All fixes are built and harness-tested (both IL rewrites against the real vanilla IL) but not deployed or smoke-tested. The smoke items are in the plan.
