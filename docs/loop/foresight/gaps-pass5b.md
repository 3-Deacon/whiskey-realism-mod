**GAP-02. Nothing defines a covering member's "own sector" from one review to the next, so the force breaks up once a member reaches the post.** S:303 says a member "stays claimed by its own sector's hold ... it counts in its own sector's hold wherever on the axis it stands". Membership is "the first of the axis sectors' reserved formations, axis sector by axis sector in sector id order", and the incumbents (`CoveringKeys`) are ordered first only inside each axis sector's own list. `ReserveRules.Hold` recomputes the sector every review from position (`HoldSectorOf`, ReserveRules.cs:209), and reserves in a sector only while `held < need[sector]` (ReserveRules.cs:222). Two cases follow. (a) The post stands in a different axis sector B: at the next review every arrived member is B's formation, and B reserves only up to `need[B]`. Members beyond that are reserved by no hold, carry no claim and fall into the capacity of `MassFor`. Meanwhile sector A, which lost them, reserves new formations, and those are drafted first again because of the sector-id order. (b) The post's sector is not an axis sector: an arrived member sits in no axis sector's list and is never a member again, so it is released and the next day's pick sends someone else.
- Severity: blocker
- Impact: the covering force does not survive its own march. `IncumbentsKeepTheirPlace`, `MembersCountInTheirOwnSectorsHold` and criterion-covering-held (90 %, S:373) cannot be met by building S as written, and nothing persists the sector a member belongs to.
- Assumption: a member keeps the axis sector it was first claimed in until it leaves the force. This needs a persisted key -> sector entry next to `CoveringKeys` (for example `coveringKeys: [{unitKey, sectorId}]`), which amends P:33's sidecar shape `coveringKeys: [string]` and so is itself an S/P change to decide, not an assumption the builder may take silently. `ReserveRules.Hold` treats an incumbent as a reserve of its recorded sector wherever it stands, and incumbents are counted before any new pick.
- Grounding: shipped code
